## Why this first
Branded web mentions correlated with AI Overview visibility more strongly than backlinks, domain rating, or referring domains in a 75,000-brand study ([[aeo-citation-method]]). AI engines weigh **consensus** — the same facts about a business repeated in many places. P&G currently has about five such places, and until 2026-09-13 they disagreed with each other on name, phone, and claims. The listing pack fixes the facts and multiplies the sources at the same time.

**Paste-ready copy for every listing:** `Operations/Mentions Sprint/PNG-Listing-Pack_9.20.2026.md` (NAP block, three description lengths, categories, services, attributes, photo list, links).

## Tier 3 — community answers (slow, genuine, high citation value)
Reddit is among the most frequently cited sources in ChatGPT ([[aeo-citation-method]]). Rules: real answers only, disclose the business, never drop links cold, and only where P&G's expertise is genuine.
- **Where:** r/smallbusiness · r/farmersmarket · r/eventplanning · r/streetfoodvendors and similar vendor subs · Quora threads on tent/booth/trade-show questions · LA vendor Facebook groups.

## Rules
1. **Same facts everywhere** — copy the listing pack exactly; a mismatch is worse than a missing listing.
2. **Claims canon** — "Printed in LA," "most orders ship within 5 business days of proof approval," free proof on $500+, rush quoted. Never "guaranteed," never "Made in USA."
3. **No paid link schemes, no fake reviews, no astroturfing.** The value is real consensus.
