# Outbound Fishing Playbook (Net-New Leads)

**Summary**: The engine that fills the TOP of the funnel with net-new demand — because the warm pipeline is squeezed and PNG has no repeatable new-lead machine yet. Four nets: (1) Venus cold-CALLS net-new local businesses, (2) Venus DMs them on IG/WhatsApp, (3) Carlos builds recurring referral partnerships, (4) GBP + reviews capture free "near me" demand. The unlock: cold calls + DMs need ZERO warmup (unlike cold email, which is July), Venus's time is already paid, so this fishing starts Monday at ~$0. It's a distribution problem, not a demand problem — LA is full of event-display demand; this gets PNG in front of it.

**Type**: channel

### The cold-call script (Murray Value Statement — bilingual)
Tonality: warm, confident, **downward inflection**, straight in. The "next step" = the **free proof**, not a meeting. Rehearse it ~10 min so it's natural ([[outbound-method]]).

**Opener + value statement (EN):**
> "Hey [name], this is Venus with **Promo & Grow** — how's your day going? [beat] Quick reason I'm calling: we're a local LA shop that does custom event **tents, banners, and displays** for [event pros / restaurants / etc.] — and what's different is **you see a free proof with your logo before you pay a cent**, and we deliver in **5 business days**. I'd love to start a free mockup for whatever you've got coming up — what events or booths are on your calendar this summer?"

**(ES):**
> "¡Hola [name]! Le habla Venus de **Promo & Grow** — ¿cómo está? [beat] Rápido el motivo de mi llamada: somos un taller local aquí en LA que hace **carpas, banners y displays personalizados** para [planeadores / restaurantes / etc.] — y lo que nos hace diferentes es que **usted ve una prueba gratis con su logo antes de pagar nada**, y entregamos en **5 días hábiles**. Me encantaría empezarle un diseño gratis para lo que tenga próximamente — ¿qué eventos o booths tiene en su calendario este verano?"

**The free-proof pivot (assumptive ask):**
> "Tell you what — send me your logo and I'll get a **free proof** started today, no cost, no commitment. What's the next event you're prepping for?"

**Gatekeeper / not the right person:**
> "No problem — who handles your event signage or booth setup? I'd love to get them a free mockup."

**Voicemail (short, leave the hook):**
> "Hi [name], Venus with Promo & Grow — a local LA print & display shop. We do custom tents and banners with a **free proof before you pay** and **5-day delivery**. I'll shoot you a quick text too. Reach me at [number] — I'd love to send you a free mockup."

**Immediate text after the voicemail:**
> "Hi [name], Venus w/ Promo & Grow 👋 just left you a VM — we do custom event tents & banners, free proof with your logo before you pay, delivered in 5 business days. Got an event coming up? Send your logo and I'll start a free mockup. — Venus"

**Objections (cold = lighter than warm):** use the [[cold-reply-playbook]] branches — *"just send a price"* → a free proof is the fastest real number; *"we have a printer"* → run one free proof to compare; *"not interested"* → leave the door open. **Never argue the objection** ([[outbound-method]]).

### Daily call cadence
- ~**1–1.5 hr morning block** of net-new dials; rest of the day = inbound + closing.
- Target **~25–40 dials/day** (list is pre-built so she's never researching mid-block — Murray's "coil the spring"). Expect ~15–25% connect.
- **ABAB rhythm:** call list A Mon, DM list B Tue, follow-up A Wed… ideally call **and** DM the same prospect within 48h.
- Every connect → light qualify (need / date / product, per [[lead-qualification-system]]) → free-proof pivot → log **Source = ColdCall** + next step.
- Follow-up ladder: a connect that doesn't close gets a 24–48h follow-up; no-answers get VM + text, then 2–3 touches over a week, then rest **6–8 weeks** (often timing, not a no).
- **Track:** dials → connects → free-proofs started → quotes → closes.

## NET 2 — IG / WhatsApp DM outreach (visual product, free)

**Targets:** local LA event vendors, quinceañera/wedding planners, party vendors, food trucks, churches, swap-meet vendors — found via Instagram (hashtags #eventosLA #quinceanera #LAcatering + local geotags) and Google Maps.

**DM template (lead with a real compliment + the free-proof hook; attach a photo of a real tent):**
> **EN:** "Hi [name]! Love what you're doing with [their events]. I'm Venus with Promo & Grow, a local LA shop — we make custom tents, backdrops & table covers, and I can send you a **FREE proof with your logo before you pay anything** (delivered in 5 business days). Want me to mock something up for your next event? No cost, no commitment 🙌"
> **ES:** "¡Hola [name]! Me encanta lo que haces con [sus eventos]. Soy Venus de Promo & Grow, taller local en LA — hacemos carpas, fondos y manteles personalizados, y te puedo mandar una **PRUEBA GRATIS con tu logo antes de pagar nada** (entrega en 5 días hábiles). ¿Te preparo un diseño para tu próximo evento? Sin costo ni compromiso 🙌"

**Cadence:** ~10–20 DMs/day; respond fast; move to WhatsApp/call when they bite. **Consent:** DMing a business that posts publicly is fine; don't add anyone to SMS without explicit opt-in ([[cold-reply-playbook]]).

## NET 3 — Referral partnerships (Carlos lane — the DURABLE engine)

The highest-leverage net: businesses that **own your customer** and need displays for every client they serve. One good partner sends leads every month — this is the difference between a job and a business.

**Target types:** event planners · party-rental companies · event venues / banquet halls · DJs & photographers · caterers · sign installers who don't print large-format · **other small print shops/brokers who don't do tents** (white-label them).

**The pitch (Carlos, owner-to-owner):**
> "You serve clients who need branded tents, backdrops, and banners — we make them. Free proof before they pay, 5 business days, made in LA, bilingual. Send your people to us and we take care of them (and you). Want me to run one free proof on your next client so you see the quality?"

**Structure options:** a referral fee (~10–15%) · wholesale/white-label pricing (your cost + margin, they mark up) · or a simple "preferred vendor" handshake. Pick per partner.

**Sourcing:** Google Maps ("party rentals / event venues / event planners Los Angeles") · Instagram · the Apollo party-rental & planner segments · Carlos's network · BNI / chamber.

**Cadence:** Carlos targets **~5 partnership conversations/week.** Even 1–2 live partners reshapes the funnel.
