# Cold Email — Segment-Tailored Copy (P&G net-new)

> ⚠️ Same rules as [[cold-email-sequence]]: send from **promoteandgrow.com** only (P&G mailbox, not promongrow.com); **Touch 1 = zero links**; plain text, no images; under ~90 words; personalize line 1; one-line unsubscribe + physical address; tracking pixels OFF; auto-stop on reply → Venus replies within 1 hr. Delivery line is always **"5 business days from proof approval"** — never "guaranteed," never the Donut "3 days." (ganas read-aloud test on all ES.)

**Summary**: Ready-to-paste, segment-tailored cold-email copy for the [[apollo-net-new-list|Apollo net-new segments]] — event planners, breweries, caterers, real estate, party rentals. Sits on the 3-touch cadence + deliverability rules in [[cold-email-sequence]]; this page just swaps in each segment's product angle, micro-case, and subject lines for higher reply rates.

**Type**: channel

**Sources**: co-CEO build session 2026-06-18; builds on [[cold-email-sequence]] (PNG-ColdEmail-Sequence-Bilingual); [[apollo-net-new-list]]; voice per [[png-voice]].

## How to run it in Apollo
Two options: **(a)** one sequence using the `{{product_angle}}` / `{{business_type}}` tokens + the angle table below (fastest); **(b)** clone the sequence **per `PNG ·` segment list** and paste the tailored copy (higher reply). ## ① Event Planners — full 3-touch (top fit)

**Touch 1 · Day 1 · intro (no link)**
*Subjects:* `idea for {{business}}` · `{{first_name}}, free mockup for your next event` · *(ES)* `una idea para {{business}}`
> **EN** — Hi {{first_name}}, I'm Venus with **Promo & Grow**, a local LA display shop. We make custom tents, step-and-repeat backdrops, and branded table covers for event pros like you in {{city}}. They're the pieces that make a client's event look high-end. Here's what's different: I'll send a **free proof with the design before you pay a cent**, then **5 business days from approval** to delivery. Got an upcoming build? Reply and I'll put a free mockup together. — Venus, Promo & Grow (formerly PrintCo Direct)

> **ES** — Hola {{first_name}}, le habla Venus de **Promo & Grow**, un taller local de displays aquí en LA. Hacemos carpas, fondos step-&-repeat y manteles con logo para event planners como usted en {{city}}, lo que hace que el evento de su cliente se vea de alto nivel. Lo diferente: le mando una **prueba gratis con el diseño antes de que pague nada**, y al aprobarla, **5 días hábiles** a entrega. ¿Tiene un evento próximo? Respóndame y le preparo un diseño gratis. — Venus, Promo & Grow (antes PrintCo Direct)

**Touch 2 · Day 3 · proof + micro-case (reply on same thread)**
*Subject:* `re: idea for {{business}}`
> **EN** — Hi {{first_name}}, circling back. I know event season keeps you slammed. Here's how we help planners: a {{city}} planner needed a step-and-repeat plus matching tent for a brand activation on a tight timeline. Free proof same day, one color tweak, in their hands in 5 business days. So *they* looked like the pro to their client. Want me to mock up something for an upcoming event? Reply **YES**, no cost, no commitment. — Venus

**Touch 3 · Day 7 · the easy out (breakup)**
*Subject:* `should I close your file, {{first_name}}?`
> **EN** — Hi {{first_name}}, last note from me, I won't crowd your inbox. If custom displays aren't on your radar this season, all good. But if a client event is coming up, say the word and I'll send a **free proof** so you can see it before deciding anything. Either way, I'm rooting for {{business}}. — Venus, Promo & Grow
