## How to use this

This calendar **is** what the weekly approval gate ([[venus-social-ads-playbook]]) runs on: Carlos/Claude sign off a week at a time, then Venus schedules it (Meta Business Suite Planner) and posts solo within it. Cadence ≈ **4–5 IG feed posts/week (reels-led) + near-daily stories + 1 YouTube every ~2 weeks.** Produce with **CapCut** (video) + **Canva** (statics/carousels) on the locked P&G brand kit.

**The arc (why it's ordered this way):** a cold, hesitant lead needs *trust → understanding → proof → a low-pressure ask*. Week 1 introduces the people + the promise. Week 2 puts the free proof on camera. Week 3 removes the price/size/timeline mystery. Week 4 shows real results and makes the month's one soft ask.

**Pillar mix (trust-weighted):** Proof & Trust ~35% · Product & Education ~30% · Client & Community ~25% · Promo ~10%.

## ⚠️ Two gates before anything posts

1. **Client-logo permission.** Several posts feature **Donut Media** (and other clients' logos). Publicly using a client's name/logo needs **Carlos + that client's OK first** (the approval matrix). Donut-story specifics: say **"fast," never a day-count**, do **not** cite a dollar figure, never "guaranteed" ([[josh-craig-donut-media]]). If not cleared, crop the logo out / tell it as "a repeat client." Posts affected are tagged **[client-OK]** below.
2. **Brand-kit templates.** The P&G Canva brand kit exists (colors/fonts/logo) but has **no saved post templates** — build ~3–4 first (a reel end-card, a carousel frame, a static, a story) so output is on-brand by default.

## The batch shoot list (get the month in the can in ~1 shop day)

- **Canva builds:** size-guide carousel (W3), deadline-math calendar (W3), free-proof process carousel (W2), reviews carousel (W4), the CTA static (W4), the "ask us anything" story bg (W3).
