## Standing rules (every send)

1. **Preflight SOP** ([[email-send-preflight]]): ~25-min checklist — code fixes, live-link check, SHOWUP confirm, test send with SPF/DKIM/DMARC all PASS (any FAIL = stop), mobile proof. Owner: VA · Approver: Carlos.
2. **Merge tag**: only `{{ customer.first_name | default: "…" }}` — bare `{{ first_name }}` renders blank; `{{ data.customer.first_name }}` renders raw. Test against a named contact (sent-email-log.md).
3. **Segment**: Reactivation-Apr2026 + Reactivation-2026 + int:tents, excluding DNC:institutional + 30-day purchasers. Never the cold promoteandgrow.com lane. VIP Email 8 stays manual/individual.
4. **Slice-test every ask** on 10–15% of the list ~1 hour ahead.
5. **Honest claims**: deadlines are real calendar math only; "5 business days after proof approval" (+ payment per the 6/30 ruling); free proof scoped to $500+; prices as "from $X"; bundles "from $848" via the builder — never $595/$668/$823/$857/$910; tent banners "Priced by size"; no vendor names; no fake urgency; real photos only, never AI/stock.
6. **Voice**: full [[png-voice]] outward register + anti-AI rules; subject lines speak to ONE persona ([[copywriting-method]]); one primary CTA; reply/call-first close (the Apr 23 reply-format email did 13% clicks vs 0% on hard-sell — reactivation-email-sequence.md); 844.883.3308 only.
7. **Log every send** to [[sent-email-log]] at 48h (opens/clicks/bounce/unsub) — that log calibrates the next month's subjects.

The 2–3×/week target is Carlos's call (2026-07-19); the corpus's proven cadence is weekly and [[email-campaign-engine]] warns small local lists fatigue faster. Ramp with tripwires:
