## Part 2 — Google Business Profile (the bigger local lever)

**Why it matters more than LinkedIn:** P&G is a local Lynwood shop; GBP drives the map pack + "custom tents near me / sign shop near me" local search. Current baseline (from [[dashboard]]): **4.8★, 19 reviews, 385 customer interactions, 492 monthly views.** *(No GBP screenshot was shared — apply these and confirm/adjust against the live profile; share the GBP dashboard and I'll tailor exactly.)*

### The high-leverage moves, in order

1. **Primary category** *(the #1 GBP ranking factor)* — choose the closest high-intent option in Google's list. Recommend testing **"Sign shop"** or **"Print shop"** as primary (broad local intent that matches banners/graphics/signage). Then add **secondary categories** to cover the range: *Screen printing shop · Digital printing service · Banner supplier · Commercial printer · Awning supplier · Promotional products supplier · Graphic designer.* (Custom tents lack a perfect GBP category — the secondaries capture the searches.)
2. **Business name** — keep it **exactly "Promo & Grow."** Do **not** stuff keywords (e.g., "Promo & Grow Custom Tents & Signs") — Google can suspend the listing for it.
3. **Description** (750-char max — paste):
   > Promo & Grow is a family-run custom event-display and print shop in Lynwood, CA, helping small businesses and brands show up like pros. We design, print, and deliver custom event tents (canopies), trade-show displays, SEG fabric backdrops, feather flags, table covers, banners, window graphics, and event signage — built in-house and delivered in 5 business days from proof approval. See a free proof of your logo on your product before you pay (big-ticket orders $500+). Bilingual service in English and Spanish. 20 years of production experience, rated 4.8★ on Google. Serving Lynwood, South LA, Long Beach, and all of Los Angeles, with nationwide shipping. Send your logo and get a free proof today. 📞 844.883.3308
4. **Services** — add each as a service with a 1-line description: Custom Event Tents · Trade-Show Displays · SEG Fabric Displays · Feather/Advertising Flags · Table Covers & Throws · Retractable Banner Stands · Vinyl Banners · Window Graphics · Wall Murals · Vehicle Wraps · Full-Color Printing.
5. **Products** — add product tiles with real photos + "from" prices where honest (tents from $800, 10ft SEG from $888 — [[master-price-sheet]]); each links to promongrow.com.
6. **Attributes** — turn on the ones that boost visibility + trust: **"Identifies as Latino-owned"** (real + a ranking/badge boost), **Language assistance: Spanish**, **Online estimates**, **In-store pickup** (Lynwood pickup is real — [[fulfillment-sop]]), **Small business**.
7. **Photos** — GBP rewards fresh photos: hero install (Donut/Red Bull/Temple of Gainz), the Lynwood shop floor, the team's faces, product shots, logo, cover. Same real-install library Venus is using ([[venus-social-ads-playbook]]). Add a few monthly.
8. **Google Posts** — post ~weekly (offers, new work, tips). Repurpose Venus's calendar posts ([[venus-content-calendar-2026-07]]) — near-zero extra effort, and GBP posts help ranking + conversion.
9. **Reviews** — (a) **respond to all 19** (warm, specific, signed); (b) run a **steady review-ask** on every happy close (Venus's Deposit-to-Lock / delivered orders) — review count + recency is a top-3 local factor, and 4.8★ is core proof stack. Template: *"Gracias, [name]! It was a pleasure making [their product] for [their event] — you showed up like a pro. — The Promo & Grow team."*
10. **Q&A** — seed owner-answered questions: "How fast can you deliver?" (5 business days from proof approval) · "Do you offer a proof before I pay?" (yes, free on $500+) · "¿Hablan español?" (Sí) · "How much is a custom 10×10 tent?" ($800, tent + full-color print).
