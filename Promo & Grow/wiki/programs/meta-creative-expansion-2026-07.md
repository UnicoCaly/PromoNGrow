## Competitor research — verified findings (deep-research wf_0a267c5f)

- **TentCraft**: made-in-USA-frames angle, "3–5 day" turnaround (**time-to-ship, not door-to-door**; big jobs 7+), lifetime warranty, 4.9★ Trustpilot (1,716) — review base NOT attackable; attack via door-to-door framing, pre-payment proofs, bilingual local.
- **MVP Visuals**: budget 10×10 at $549 undercuts our $800, but their **premium 10×10 is $849** — we're price-competitive like-for-like; their true artwork-to-door is **7–9 business days**; free-proof-before-payment claim REFUTED (they don't offer it).
- **BuildASign**: perpetual 25%-off positioning, $34.75 flag floor (flag-only; real kit ~$54+); **documented proof-vs-delivered mismatches**.
- **Vispronet**: 15% + free design + "3-day production," but **1.7★ Trustpilot** + surprise-fee complaints.
- **Displays2go**: urgency-tier merchandising; custom graphics excluded from fastest tiers.
- **Local**: Tapia Design (Anaheim) = confirmed Spanish-language 10×10 canopy marketer in our geography (generalist, not specialist).
- **Live Ads Library pull (7/11)**: free-mockup offers DO exist in-market (Look Our Way "1-hour free mockup," Anymade "free proof," Logo Display "free design") — our edge is **real-product, human-checked, before-payment** proof, not the word "free." Fake urgency is the category default (Banners 4 Less "First 250! Time running out!") — our honest-deadline-math angle is genuinely differentiated.

**Whitespace we own** (all four verified): ① honest **door-to-door** 5-day framing (rivals quote production time) · ② **proof-to-product fidelity** ("what you approve is what arrives") · ③ **all-in transparent pricing**, no post-order fees · ④ **bilingual local specialist**.

## Video content ideas — 18, funnel-mapped (shoot list for Carlos + Venus)

**TOF (hooks — more of what's working):**
1. **Proof-reveal reaction** — screen-record sending a real lead their proof + their reply. Hook: "We put your logo on a tent before you pay. Watch."
2. **Truck-to-storefront POV** — 10×10 setup timelapse, 60s. "From truck to storefront in 8 minutes."
3. **¿Cuál puesto se ve más caro?** (ES) — blank tent vs branded tent split-screen at a real market.
4. **Count-the-tents pan** — market row of white tents, stop on the branded one. "Everyone paid the same booth fee. One of them looks like a business."
5. **Price honesty talking-head** — Carlos at the shop: "A custom 10×10 costs $800. There. Now you know." (whitespace ③)

**MOF (proof — walk them toward trust):**
6. **Proof-vs-delivered side-by-side** — hold the printed proof sheet next to the standing tent. "What you approve is what arrives." (attacks BuildASign's documented mismatch — whitespace ②)
7. **Shop tour** — Lynwood floor, printers running. "Your tent doesn't come from a mystery warehouse."
8. **The Donut Media booth story** — real install b-roll + the brand story.
9. **"5 days means AT YOUR DOOR"** — calendar walkthrough vs the industry's ships-in-X framing (whitespace ①; MVP is 7–9 days door-to-door).
10. **The free proof, made in front of you** — screen-capture a proof pack being generated + sent. Meta-content that demystifies the offer.
11. **Outdoor vs indoor in 30s** — "Will a tradeshow display survive outside? No. Here's what will." (from [[product-application-guide]])
12. **Install sizzle** — 15s cut of real installs (Komen, gym SEG, Red Bull backlit, Donut).

**BOF (close — retarget leads + open quotes):**
13. **"Your proof is in your inbox"** — Venus direct-to-camera: "I sent it Tuesday. Go look. Tell me what to change — it's free either way."
14. **Cost-per-event math** — Carlos: "$1,600 complete set ÷ 30 events = $53 an event to look like the pro you are."
15. **Honest deadline math** — count-back calendar, explicitly "no fake urgency" (anti-category move).
16. **Paid-Monday-delivered-Friday receipts** — follow one real order with timestamps.
17. **Revision culture** — "We changed this proof three times before printing. Free. That's the point."
18. **Quote revival** — "You got a quote from us and life happened. It's still good. Here's Venus."
