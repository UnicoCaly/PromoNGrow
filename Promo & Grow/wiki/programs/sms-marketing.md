## Why SMS for P&G

The email data proves the audience responds to conversation, not hard sells (Apr 23 "reply" email 13% clicks vs Jan 30 discount blast 0% — see [[reactivation-email-sequence]]). Texting *is* conversation, and the market (LA-metro Spanish-speaking small-business owners on their phones) lives on SMS; ~98% of texts get read, usually within minutes (source: PNG-SMS-Marketing-Playbook.md). Core principle: SMS does not push checkout — it starts the conversation that the [[concierge-close]] finishes. Drive to "reply" or "call," not "buy now" (source: PNG-SMS-Marketing-Playbook.md).

## Compliance first (TCPA — legal teeth)

- **Explicit opt-in required.** An email list or a number collected for order updates is NOT marketing-SMS consent (source: PNG-SMS-Marketing-Playbook.md).
- **Build consent going forward:** unchecked-by-default SMS opt-in checkbox at checkout; "Text us for a fast quote: (number)" on site (inbound texts are consented by nature); optional "OK to text me about my order? [y/n]" on quote forms (source: PNG-SMS-Marketing-Playbook.md).
- **Every marketing text:** sender ID ("Promo & Grow") + opt-out ("Reply STOP to opt out") (source: PNG-SMS-Marketing-Playbook.md).
- **Frequency:** low — high-value, low-volume only; one bad blast = instant STOP (source: PNG-SMS-Marketing-Playbook.md).
- **Quiet hours:** no texts before 9am / after 8pm local (source: PNG-SMS-Marketing-Playbook.md).

## Setup

Shopify's native SMS is limited; use an app — **Postscript, SMSBump (Yotpo), or Recart** — that integrates with Shopify checkout opt-in, supports 2-way replies, and handles compliance/opt-out automatically. 2-way is the key feature for P&G: replies must land where the closer/Carlos sees and responds. Start with a US 10DLC number (source: PNG-SMS-Marketing-Playbook.md).

## Funnel fit

1. **Order/proof updates (transactional, highest trust)** — consented by nature; warms the channel.
2. **Warm re-engagement (marketing)** — to opted-in past customers, conversational not salesy.
3. **Quote follow-up** — the unpaid-quote nudge works great as a fast, personal text (see [[conversion-bridge]]).
4. **Inbound "text us"** — instant concierge entry point (source: PNG-SMS-Marketing-Playbook.md).

Every inbound text/reply = a closer lead, worked in the same warm queue as email repliers and inbound calls (source: PNG-SMS-Marketing-Playbook.md). See [[closer-hire]].

## The 8 hooks

8 marketing SMS hooks drafted: conversation-starter, free-proof, deadline, reorder, reframe, owner-note, social-proof, and two-booths contrast — plus Spanish versions. Start with conversation-starter, free-proof, and owner-note (source: PNG-Master-Context-Playbook.md).

## Bilingual templates (Carlos voice — reply/call, not checkout)

The playbook provides 4 written templates in EN + ES (source: PNG-SMS-Marketing-Playbook.md):
1. **Proof ready (transactional):** EN "Hi [Name], it's Promo & Grow — your free proof is ready. Reply YES to approve & we start your 5-day production…" / ES "Hola [Name], somos Promo & Grow — tu prueba gratis está lista. Responde SÍ para aprobarla…"
2. **Warm re-engagement (opted-in only):** EN "Hey [Name], Carlos at Promo & Grow. Event season's starting — want me to send a quick proof…? Reply STOP to opt out." / ES equivalent.
3. **Quote follow-up (unpaid quote):** EN "Hi [Name], following up on your custom tent quote — any questions before we lock production for your [date] event?…" / ES with "cotización de carpa."
4. **Inbound auto-reply:** bilingual in one message ("…Send us your logo + what you need and we'll text back a free proof… Hablamos español.").

All follow the [[png-voice|bilingual craft rule]].

## Sequencing

Set up app + compliance + checkout opt-in NOW (start the consented list); use SMS for transactional/proof updates first (warms the channel, all consented); after ~weeks of a consented marketing list, layer warm re-engagement; keep volume low, value high, always conversational (source: PNG-SMS-Marketing-Playbook.md).
