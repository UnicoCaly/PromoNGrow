## What this is

A self-contained email program that runs almost entirely on Shopify's free Email tier, plus one theme-level exit popup. It is the cheap, compounding revenue layer that sits alongside the human [[concierge-close]]: campaigns wake the list up and drive traffic, automated flows recover and retain 24/7, and the VIP note deepens repeat relationships. It extends and supersedes the earlier [[reactivation-email-sequence]] (see Contradictions). Demand it generates is measured the same way the rest of the funnel is — quote requests and calls, not just checkouts (see [[conversion-bridge]] and [[lead-machine]]).

**Platform facts** (source: PNG-Email-Library_5.9.2026.md):
- Platform: Shopify Email (free tier)
- List size: 647 contacts on the `Reactivation-Apr2026` tag
- Active discount code: `SHOWUP` — free shipping on orders over $500, one per customer, framed for first-time customers
- Send-from / reply-to for automations: hello@promongrow.com (so replies reach Carlos) (source: PNG-Abandoned-Cart-and-Exit-Popup_5.9.2026.md)
- VIP note sends from carlos@proagobranding.co or hello@promongrow.com, individually (source: PNG-Email-Library_5.9.2026.md)

## Automated flows (set up once, run forever)

Built in Shopify admin → Marketing → Automations (source: PNG-Email-Library_5.9.2026.md, PNG-Abandoned-Cart-and-Exit-Popup_5.9.2026.md).

### Abandoned cart
Escalation model: reminder → confidence → incentive. No discount until the final email. Most recoveries happen on Email 1 (simple reminder); Email 2 adds trust signals; the final email drops the `SHOWUP` shipping incentive for holdouts. The plug-and-play HTML implements three steps:

| Step | Delay from abandonment | Subject | Hook |
|------|------------------------|---------|------|
| Cart 1 — Simple reminder | 1 hour | You left something behind | Transactional nudge, under 50 words, one copper CTA |
| Cart 2 — Confidence builder | 24 hours (wait 23h) | Still thinking about it? | Trust stack + Other Off Road Guys review card |
| Cart 3 — Shipping incentive | 72 hours (wait 48h) | Free shipping on your order — just this once | `SHOWUP` code block, soft "no hard feelings" close |

Each step carries Shopify's built-in "customer has not placed an order" condition; recovery URL is `{{ abandoned_checkout.recovery_url }}`; cart contents auto-populate via the "Abandoned checkout" content block. Expected recovery 8-15% across the sequence. (source: PNG-Abandoned-Cart-and-Exit-Popup_5.9.2026.md, PNG-Email-Templates-PlugAndPlay_5.9.2026.html)

### Post-purchase
- **Email 11 — Thank you (Day 3 after fulfillment)**: sets expectations, 10-min setup tip, "call us if damaged," Instagram @promongrow tag ask.
- **Email 12 — Review ask (Day 14)**: 30-second Google review ask; built with a placeholder Google review link `https://g.page/r/YOUR-GOOGLE-REVIEW-LINK/review` that must be replaced before activation. Negative feedback routed to reply-to Carlos.

### Win-back
- **Email 13 — 90-day win-back**: re-engages lapsed buyers; surfaces what's new (bundles, design resources page); referral P.S.

(source: PNG-Email-Library_5.9.2026.md, PNG-Email-Templates-PlugAndPlay_5.9.2026.html)

## Exit-intent bundle popup

Replaces the old first-order confidence popup ("Popup B"). Fires on exit intent (cursor toward top of window; mobile = rapid scroll-up >100px). Shows only on product and collection pages (never homepage, cart, or checkout), once per session via `sessionStorage`, after 5 seconds on page. Headline "Before you go — Bundle and save 15%"; pulls featured images from product handles `10ft-x-10ft-event-tent-canopy`, `feather-convex-flag-copy`, `stretch-table-cover`; price card "Starting at $668 (saves you $100+)"; copper CTA → /collections/bundles. Full design spec and a paste-ready Claude Code build prompt live in the source; the build belongs to the **Claude Code (theme)** lane — file at snippets/popup-exit-bundle.liquid, included before `</body>`. ## Setup notes (Shopify Email)

- **Campaign emails (1-8, 14-15)**: Marketing → Campaigns → Shopify Email → "Brand" template; paste subject + body; filter recipients by `Reactivation-Apr2026` (reactivation) or all subscribers (campaigns); for Emails 2-3 exclude purchasers since April 15; review open/click after 48h.
- **Automated flows (9-13)**: Marketing → Automations; abandoned cart uses the built-in "Abandoned checkout" trigger; post-purchase uses "Order fulfilled" + delays; win-back uses the win-back template at 90 days.
- **HTML build**: all bodies use inline CSS for max compatibility; copper CTA `#C17A3A`; merge tags `{{ customer.first_name | default: "there" }}` (plug-and-play) or `{{ first_name | default: "there" }}` (production bodies). The production HTML bodies carry full UTM tagging (`utm_source=email&utm_medium=campaign&utm_campaign=<name>`) feeding [[conversion-tracking-setup]].

Platform toggles (activating automations, scheduling sends) are the **Carlos / VA** lane; copy and asset production are the **Claude-admin** lane; the exit popup is the **Claude Code (theme)** lane. (source: PNG-Email-Library_5.9.2026.md, PNG-Abandoned-Cart-and-Exit-Popup_5.9.2026.md)

## Voice rules for every email

Plain-language operational rules to keep the program on-brand (source: PNG-Email-Library_5.9.2026.md): sound like Carlos talking to a client, not a marketing department; short sentences, benefit first; every email ends with a way to buy (link) AND a way to talk (phone); no fake urgency (no countdowns, no "only X left"); always pair "design not included" with "we proof before production"; let trust phrases appear naturally (5-day delivery, free proof, 4.8★ / 19 reviews, 20+ years, Made in USA); personalize with first name; phone (844) 883-3308 in every signature. Outward copy itself must be in the [[png-voice]] register.
