# Content Engine

**Summary**: The organic content engine — a repeatable, mostly-automated production system targeting 20+ on-brand posts/month across Instagram (primary) and Facebook (mirror), built on a locked 4-pillar mix, 8 reusable education carousel frameworks, a Judge.me-to-Canva review pipeline, and one weekly 2-hour batch block. This page also logs the Canva build manifests (Month 1 Weeks 1-2, Month 2 Week 1) and the agency-facing social content guide.

## What this engine is

The content engine turns social from a discretionary task into a production system. The Blueprint defines four moving parts (source: PNG-Content-Engine-Blueprint.md):

1. A **Content Pillar OS** — a locked 4-pillar mix with pre-defined formats and hook libraries.
2. An **Education Framework Library** — 8 reusable Matt Gray-style carousel templates, each tied to a PNG product, built once in Canva and reused monthly.
3. A **Judge.me to Canva review pipeline** — converts 4-5 star reviews into approve-then-publish posts, while also generating more reviews/photos per order.
4. A **solo-founder production loop** — one 2-hour weekly batch block produces the next week's content, with Canva Bulk Create doing the heavy lifting.

Operating principle (source: PNG-Content-Engine-Blueprint.md): "Education earns the follow. Product closes the sale. Reviews remove the risk. Promo converts the warm. Every post does one job."

Owner: Carlos, solo founder/operator (source: PNG-Content-Engine-Blueprint.md). The agency-facing guide is prepared by Proágo Branding Dept., Carlos's sister agency (source: png-social-content-guide.jsx).

## Positioning and ICP for content

Primary ICP for the engine: **marketing managers at mid-market brands** who buy event displays for field teams, conferences, activations, and trade shows. They care, in order, about: will we look professional, will it land on time (5-day delivery edge), will the print match the brand guide (free artwork proof), and is it reliable at scale (20+ years, 4.8 star, repeat ordering) (source: PNG-Content-Engine-Blueprint.md).

Secondary audiences are tagged, not targeted: SMB first-time trade-show buyers (served by buyer-education carousels) and event planners/agencies (served by reliability + volume content) (source: PNG-Content-Engine-Blueprint.md). The agency guide frames the audience slightly differently — small business owners, event planners/marketing managers, and repeat buyers (source: png-social-content-guide.jsx). See [[positioning-and-personas]].

Message hierarchy that hits every post: hero benefit = 5-business-day guaranteed delivery; proof layer = 20+ years, 4.8 star Google, Made in USA, free artwork proof. Avoid hobbyist framing, Vistaprint-generic copy, fake urgency, startup-y tone (source: PNG-Content-Engine-Blueprint.md). This aligns with [[png-voice]].

## Content Pillar OS

The Blueprint locks this pillar mix as a monthly target and weekly checklist (source: PNG-Content-Engine-Blueprint.md):

| Pillar | % | Primary format | Posts/mo (of 20) | Job |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Education & Frameworks | 35% | Carousel | 7 | Earn the follow |
| Product Showcase | 30% | Single + Reel | 6 | Show the work |
| Client Results / Reviews | 25% | Single + carousel | 5 | Remove risk |
| Promo & Trust | 10% | Single + Story | 2 | Convert the warm |

**Contradiction (unresolved):** the agency social content guide uses a different mix — Product Showcase 35%, Education & Tips 25%, Client Results 25%, Promo & Trust 15% (source: png-social-content-guide.jsx). The Blueprint (Education-led 35/30/25/10) is the later, more detailed strategy doc and should be treated as the source of truth unless Carlos says otherwise (co-CEO recommendation). The guide also assumes a lighter cadence (2-3 posts/week) vs the Blueprint's 5/week target.

Weekly distribution runs an A/B rotation (A-B-A-B across the month) so 10 slot types yield 20 unique posts (source: PNG-Content-Engine-Blueprint.md):

| Day | Format | Week A | Week B |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | Carousel | Education framework | Trade-show playbook |
| Tue | Single | Product showcase | Client result (review) |
| Wed | Reel | Product in action | Education quick win |
| Thu | Single | Client result (review) | Product detail/comparison |
| Fri | Carousel | Education buyer-selector | Promo & Trust / BTS |

Editorial constraints, auto-checked weekly: no two back-to-back posts from the same pillar; at least one review/client post per week; one framework per week (non-negotiable growth engine); one Reel per week (source: PNG-Content-Engine-Blueprint.md).

## The Education Framework Library (core IP)

Eight carousel frameworks, each 8-10 slides, each tied to a product and ending on a CTA slide. All use DM Serif Display headers / DM Sans body, Navy hook slides, Copper accents (source: PNG-Content-Engine-Blueprint.md):

| # | Framework | Angle | Product tie-in |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The 10x10 Booth Domination Playbook (3 zones: canopy/wall/floor) | Trade-show maximization | 10x10 tent + table throw + retractable banner bundle |
| 2 | Brand Presence Stack (canopy/backdrop/floor/wearables) | Promote & Grow framework | Tents + backdrops + flags + throws |
| 3 | Pre-Show / Show-Day / Post-Show Checklist | Event maximization | Full booth kits + replacement fabrics |
| 4 | Tent Size Selector | Buyer education | 5x5, 10x10, 15x10, 20x10 tents |
| 5 | Print-Ready File Prep (Fix Your Files) | Quick-win tactical | All printed products |
| 6 | Wind Rating Explained | Buyer education | Tent frames (economy vs heavy-duty) |
| 7 | Reorder vs Rebuy (Fabric Only) | Repeat-buyer education | Fabric-only replacements |
| 8 | The 48-Hour Booth Recovery | Crisis / trade-show playbook | 5-day rush capability |

Framework 5 (file prep) maps directly to the production guardrail in [[file-prep-gate]] (CMYK not RGB, DPI on large format, bleed, outlined fonts, no web-pulled logos) (source: PNG-Content-Engine-Blueprint.md). Frameworks 7 and 8 feed reorder and rush demand, supporting [[fulfillment-sop]] and the reactivation angle in [[reactivation-email-sequence]].

Framework template anatomy: Slide 1 hook (Navy bg, Copper accent bar, DM Serif headline, 9-12 words); Slides 2-7 body (white bg, Navy headers, one idea per slide, numbered); Slide 8 payoff/proof; Slide 9 CTA (Navy bg, Copper button, phone + link); optional Slide 10 save/share nudge (source: PNG-Content-Engine-Blueprint.md).

## The Review Engine (Judge.me to Canva to approval queue)

Two halves: getting more reviews/images, and turning them into posts (source: PNG-Content-Engine-Blueprint.md).

**Inbound (get more reviews/photos):** turn on Judge.me photo/video review requests with a coupon incentive; cadence at Day 7 / Day 21 / Day 45 post-delivery; a post-purchase QR insert card ("Show us your setup, tag @promongrow, best monthly photo gets a free table throw"); optional Day-14 SMS follow-up; a monthly Client Spotlight with a $50 store credit for permission. Target: 8-12 new reviews/month with at least 40% carrying a photo (source: PNG-Content-Engine-Blueprint.md). This ties to [[sms-marketing]] and the VIP/repeat surface in [[vip-reactivation-list]].

**Outbound flow (semi-auto with approval queue):** Judge.me review lands on the product page → weekly CSV export of last 7 days of 4-5 star reviews → import into an Airtable/Notion "Review Queue" table → Canva Bulk Create generates 3-5 review-post drafts → Carlos approves/edits in Canva → approved posts scheduled in Meta Business Suite → client DM'd that they were featured (source: PNG-Content-Engine-Blueprint.md).

Two review-post Canva templates: Template A (single image — customer photo right, stars+quote+company left, Copper CTA bar) and Template B (3-slide carousel — quote, full-bleed client photo, CTA + social-proof stack). Bulk Create maps fields {{CustomerName}}, {{Quote}}, {{Product}}, {{UseCase}}, {{PhotoURL}}, {{StarRating}} on a Friday 9 AM cadence (source: PNG-Content-Engine-Blueprint.md). Weekly target: 1-2 review posts (matches the 25% Client Results pillar).

## Canva template system

Build 14 core Canva templates once in Week 1; everything the engine produces comes out of these (source: PNG-Content-Engine-Blueprint.md):

- **Education (6):** Framework Carousel Master (10-slide, used by all 8 frameworks), Quick-Win Tip Carousel (5-slide), Buyer Selector Carousel (7-slide X-vs-Y), Trade Show Playbook Carousel (9-slide checklist), Education Single, Education Reel cover (9:16).
- **Product Showcase (3):** Product Hero Single, Product Detail Carousel (4-slide), Product Reel template (9:16 with lower-third bar).
- **Client Results (3):** Review Single (Template A), Review Carousel (Template B), Client Feature Carousel (4-slide).
- **Promo & Trust (2):** Trust Stat Single ("4.8 star • 20+ years • Made in USA"), Offer Single.

Template rules: Navy (#1B2838) = 60% of cover/hook surface; Copper (#C17A3A) only CTAs/accents, never body text; white/off-white body slides; DM Serif Display headers, DM Sans body; minimum 8% safe-zone margin; every template carries {{Hook}}, {{Body}}, {{CTA}} merge fields (source: PNG-Content-Engine-Blueprint.md). These match the visual system in [[png-voice]] and the theme guide.

## Tech stack and automation map

Committed tools: Canva Pro (design + Bulk Create), Airtable or Notion (pick one — Airtable recommended for Canva Bulk Create), Shopify + Judge.me (review source), Instagram + Facebook (publish) (source: PNG-Content-Engine-Blueprint.md).

Tools to add, prioritized: Meta Business Suite (free, scheduling + cross-posting — start here); Make.com (free tier <1,000 ops/mo, for true Judge.me-to-Airtable automation); Later or Buffer (only if grid preview/multi-caption needed); CapCut or InShot (free Reel editing) (source: PNG-Content-Engine-Blueprint.md).

Current phase is "Assisted Manual" (weekly manual CSV export). Phase 2 ("Lightly Automated," month 2+) adds a Make.com scenario: new 4-5 star review → auto-create Airtable row → Slack/email ping, removing the CSV step (source: PNG-Content-Engine-Blueprint.md). This automation roadmap overlaps with [[zapier-automation-roadmap]] (note: the Blueprint specifies Make.com, not Zapier — flag for tooling decision; co-CEO recommendation: consolidate on whichever the broader stack standardizes on to avoid two automation platforms). Slack pings tie to [[slack-workspace-setup]].

## Solo-founder production workflow

Designed around one 2-hour weekly batch + one 30-minute mid-week touch-up (source: PNG-Content-Engine-Blueprint.md):

- **Sunday night (15 min) — Plan:** confirm next week's 5 slots; pull new reviews into the Review Queue.
- **Monday (2 hrs) — Produce:** Hour 1 draft all 5 captions in Airtable; Hour 2 run Bulk Create on review posts + customize the other 4; load all 5 into Meta Business Suite scheduled Mon-Fri.
- **Thursday (30 min) — Touch up:** review analytics (saves/shares/profile visits), adjust the calendar, reply to DMs/comments.
- **Monthly (45 min, first Sunday) — Recalibrate:** confirm the 20-post pillar mix (7 Edu / 6 Product / 5 Client / 2 Promo), bank top-3 hooks, kill bottom-3 patterns, queue next month.

Batching principles: cluster by template not by day; treat caption templates as sacred (swap variables, don't write from scratch); keep a tagged photo library so you never search mid-batch (source: PNG-Content-Engine-Blueprint.md). The batch discipline mirrors [[operating-rhythm]].

## Hook patterns and caption framework

Nine Matt Gray-style hook patterns (keep open while drafting): Cost-of-Wrong, Hidden Math, Expert Confession, Before-It's-Too-Late, Reframe, Checklist Promise, Behind-the-Curtain, Myth Buster, Client Win (source: PNG-Content-Engine-Blueprint.md).

Non-carousel caption framework: hook line (8-12 words) → context (1-2 sentences) → proof (1 sentence) → soft CTA → hard CTA (phone + link) → 8-12 hashtags (source: PNG-Content-Engine-Blueprint.md). The agency guide provides four pillar-specific caption template structures (Product Showcase, Client Results, Education & Tips, Promo & Trust) for the same purpose (source: png-social-content-guide.jsx).

Hashtag strategy: the Blueprint says 8-12 hashtags/post grouped brand/product/use-case/audience/quality, rotating non-brand sets to avoid IG flagging (source: PNG-Content-Engine-Blueprint.md). The agency guide says 15-20 hashtags/post across Core Brand / Product-Specific / Industry-Use-Case / Trust groups (source: png-social-content-guide.jsx). **Contradiction:** 8-12 vs 15-20 hashtags per post — pick one rule before handing to anyone executing (co-CEO recommendation: follow the Blueprint's 8-12 to stay under IG spam thresholds).

## Agency social content guide

The png-social-content-guide.jsx is an interactive agency playbook (Overview / Visual Identity / Content Pillars / Calendar / Captions / Hashtags / Guidelines) prepared by Proágo Branding Dept., April 2026, for whoever runs IG + FB (source: png-social-content-guide.jsx). It restates the brand facts and adds executional guardrails the Blueprint does not spell out:

- **Cadence options:** 2 posts/week (~$150/mo) or 3 posts/week (~$200-250/mo), each a repeating 4-week rotation (source: png-social-content-guide.jsx). These are agency-pricing-tier assumptions and are lighter than the Blueprint's 5-posts/week / 20-per-month target — flag the gap when staffing the engine.
- **Hard rules (Always):** include phone (844) 883-3308 or "link in bio" in every caption; navy/copper/white palette only; DM Serif Display headlines / DM Sans body; REAL product photos only; logo bottom-right on every graphic; mention "5-day delivery" 2x+ per week; state "design not included"; cross-post to Facebook within 1 hour of IG; short benefit-first shop-foreman voice (source: png-social-content-guide.jsx).
- **Hard rules (Never):** AI-generated product images (Firefly/Midjourney/DALL-E/ChatGPT); off-palette colors; fake urgency/countdowns; script/decorative fonts; posting without a CTA; tagging competitors; politics/religion; more than 8 words of image-overlay text; promising design services; posting without proofreading (source: png-social-content-guide.jsx).
- **Photo asset protocol:** Carlos provides product/production photos; get client permission to repost setups; never use AI or stock; if you run out of photos, ask Carlos — don't fill with stock (source: png-social-content-guide.jsx).
- **Approval process:** all posts submitted 48 hours before scheduled posting; no post goes live without Carlos's written approval (source: png-social-content-guide.jsx).

The guide's "shop foreman" brand-voice description (clear, direct, no jargon, confident from 20+ years, warm-but-professional, benefit-first; never Vistaprint-generic/fake-urgency/startup-y) is a close cousin of the wiki's ganas growth-partner voice but is framed in English-only operational terms. Defer to [[png-voice]] for the canonical voice, and layer in the [[bilingual-spanish-moat]] for any Spanish or code-switched captions — the agency guide does not cover bilingual craft, which is a gap to close before any ES content ships (co-CEO recommendation).

Key facts the guide loads for copy: 5-day guaranteed delivery nationwide; free artwork proof on every order; 4.8 star from 19 Google reviews; 20+ years; Made in USA; design not included (proof before production); phone (844) 883-3308; promongrow.com (source: png-social-content-guide.jsx). The "19 Google reviews" figure is the most specific review-count datapoint in this cluster.
