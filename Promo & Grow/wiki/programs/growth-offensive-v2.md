## Why v2 (the pivot)

v1 (6/15) leaned **paid-first + cold-email-first**. Two facts broke it:
1. **The warm board is squeezed** — weeks of follow-up on the open quotes (Del Rio, Shadi, Cristian) went dark. Harvesting is finite.
2. **Paid doesn't pay back in 30 days** — at ~$93 CPL, $750/mo buys ~1–4 qualified leads/mo. Spending $3K to close $2.2K is negative ROI.

**The reframe:** it's a **distribution problem, not a demand problem.** LA is saturated with event-display demand (quinceañeras, weddings, fiestas, festivals, churches, food trucks, real estate). PNG just isn't in front of it. So v2's job is to **build the repeatable engine that gets PNG in front of that demand** — cheaply, starting now.

## Venus is the throttle — the sequencing model (the part v1 missed)

One closer, **~3–6 quality conversations/day.** Turning on calls + DMs + Meta leads + partnerships + closing all at once **buries her** and everything leaks. So the nets switch on in order:

- **Week 1:** Venus = net-new **calls + DMs + work inbound + close the board tail**. Carlos = **partnerships + GBP + WhatsApp setup** (zero Venus load). Cold email = warming (no sends).
- **Week 2:** add the **Meta UGC test** (its WhatsApp leads flow to Venus). Keep calls/DMs. Partnerships continue.
- **Weeks 3–4:** double whatever's closing; **cold email first sends** after the placement test; reorder loop running.

**Venus's daily model (~6 hrs, 10–4 PT):** ~1–1.5 h cold-call block (off the Apollo sheet) · ~30 min DMs · the rest = speed-to-lead on inbound (<1 hr) + free proofs + closing + logging. **If inbound exceeds ~6–8 fresh/day, throttle the outbound** — don't bury her.

## Corrected economics (honest)

- **Outbound calls (Net 1):** ~25–40 dials/day → ~15–25% connect → ~5–8 conversations/day → free proofs. **CAC ≈ $0** (Venus salaried). The cheapest lead PNG has.
- **Meta UGC test:** $400 killable; break-even at **≤ ~$50 per confirmed lead** at ~15% close / tent AOV.
- **Cold email — corrected DOWN:** v1 modeled Option C (2 inboxes, 60–70/day → ~$3–6K/qtr). **Option A = 1 inbox, ~30–40/day → roughly half the volume → revise to ~$1–3K/qtr.** (Add a 2nd inbox / graduate to Option C if it earns it.)
- **Lean Google Search:** $500/mo, ~1–4 qualified leads/mo, ~$93 CPL — a learning test, not a 30-day engine.
- **Partnerships (Net 3):** 1–2 live partners = recurring monthly leads. Hard to forecast, highest long-run leverage.
- **Reorder/referral loop (Net 5):** every closed customer → reorder cadence + referral ask. For a 20-yr shop, this is found money v1 never operationalized.

## The scoreboard v2

Keep v1's outcome metrics, **add the net-new inputs** so we can see which net is working:
- **Inputs:** dials · connects · DMs sent · partnership conversations · GBP reviews earned.
- **Pipeline:** new leads **by source** (`src-coldcall`/`src-dm`/`src-partner`/`src-gbp`/`src-meta`/`src-googleads`) · free-proofs started · quotes.
- **Outcomes:** closes **by source** · revenue by source · **cost-per-closed-deal by net.**
- **Guardrails (unchanged):** speed-to-lead <1 hr · untouched backlog <8 (else throttle) · blended ROAS ≥3:1 on closed invoices (paid only) · scale <$130/close, fix-or-cut >$250.

## Kill / scale per net
- **Calls + DMs:** free → always on; if connects happen but no free-proofs, fix the script, not the channel.
- **Partnerships:** pursue relentlessly; a partner sending 0 leads in 60 days → deprioritize, keep the warm ones.
- **Meta test:** scale if ≤$50/lead **and** closing; kill at ~$400 if neither.
- **Google Search:** 60-day fair test; cut if cost/close >$250.
- **Cold email:** auto-pause if spam >0.08% or bounce >1.5% (internal guardrail; see [[cold-email-sequence]]).

## 30 / 60 / 90 (honest)

- **30 days:** harvest tail + outbound calls/DMs live + Meta UGC test running + partnerships seeded + GBP refreshed + cold warming. Revenue mostly **outbound + harvest tail + Meta**. Realistic net-new **~$5–10K** (capacity-bound).
- **60 days:** double what's closing; 1–2 partnerships producing; cold email first sends; reorder loop live. **~$8–14K.**
- **90 days:** scale winners; paid compounds on a bigger retargeting pool; cold at steady ~30–40/day; partnerships recurring. On the trajectory toward the **$20K/mo** floor — and, crucially, **the board is never empty again.**

These ride on Venus's realized close rate and capacity. The point of v2 isn't a bigger 30-day number — it's that the lead supply becomes **repeatable** instead of a one-time squeeze.
