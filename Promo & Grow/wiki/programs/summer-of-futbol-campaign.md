## Objective (stated as numbers)

The campaign optimizes for **closed invoice dollars**, not clicks or cart purchases — the site cart converts at ~0.2–0.5%, so the [[concierge-close]] is the conversion event (source: 00-Campaign-Game-Plan.md). See also [[the-20k-math]].

| Target | Number |
|---|---|
| Net-new closed revenue, campaign window | +$4,000–$8,000 on top of the warm/reactivation base |
| Blended cost per closed deal | < $130 |
| Cost per qualified lead | < $40 |
| Speed-to-lead (lead → Venus first touch) | < 1 hour (the #1 KPI) |
| Lead pace (protect Venus) | ~5–6 quality leads/day max; throttle if backlog > 8 for 3 days |
| AOV | ~$350–$400 (the Watch-Party Kit pushes toward $857) |

(source: 00-Campaign-Game-Plan.md)

**Attribution = instrumented, not remembered:** "How'd you hear about us?" on every lead + UTM/landing path + deal-source field → monthly revenue-by-source. Platform ROAS reads near-zero (no purchase pixel) — use it for cost metrics only (CPL/CPM/frequency) (source: 00-Campaign-Game-Plan.md). See [[conversion-tracking-setup]].

## Air + ground: how the pieces work together

Demand (AIR) → funnel → concierge close (GROUND), with one rule: speed-to-lead (source: 00-Campaign-Game-Plan.md).

- **Social** (@promongrow) + **Meta ads** (prospect + retarget) + **Google Search** (high-intent) drive traffic.
- **Email blast** (warm list) feeds the same funnel.
- Everything lands on the **"Get a Free Proof" landing page** with a 60-second qualifier that filters tire-kickers.
- **Venus** calls/texts in < 1 hr → free proof → Shopify draft order → invoice → paid → reorder loop (tag by season).
- Retargeting is the warmest dollar — fire the past-customer Custom Audience while Venus phones the reactivation list. The email blast is the cheapest revenue (owned, warm, ~$0). Google Search catches the "carpas personalizadas los angeles" ready-now buyer (source: 00-Campaign-Game-Plan.md).

See [[lead-machine]], [[google-search-engine]], [[draft-order-quote-system]], [[pipeline-ops-spine]].

5 business days production + 2–3 days shipping ≈ ~8–9 days door-to-door, so the standing message is *"order this week to fly your colors at the next match weekend"* (source: 00-Campaign-Game-Plan.md). See [[fulfillment-sop]].

| Phase | Dates | Message center of gravity |
|---|---|---|
| Group-stage rush | Jun 15 – ~Jun 27 | "Patio packed for every match — start the 5-day clock now." Hero: Watch-Party Kit. |
| Knockout urgency | ~Jun 28 – Jul 11 | "Big matches left. Order by [date] to be ready for the weekend." Add step-&-repeat photo wall. |
| Final push | ~Jul 12 – Jul 19 | "One last big weekend. Last call to be ready for the final." Flags + signage for fast yeses. |

## Guardrails (honored across every asset)

1. **FIFA-safe** — never "FIFA," "World Cup," "Copa Mundial," "Mundial," the emblem/trophy/mascot, team crests/kits/player likenesses, or any implied affiliation. Use *"the matches," "the big tournament," "el torneo," "game day," "watch party," "summer of fútbol / verano del fútbol."* **Team colors yes; crests no.** PNG prints only the customer's OWN logo, and the free-proof step is the checkpoint to reject infringing art (source: Summer-of-Futbol-Context-and-Status_6.16.2026.md, 00-Campaign-Game-Plan.md, 02-Ad-Creative-Meta-and-Google.md, 03-Funnel-Landing-Page.md).
2. **No fake urgency** — real 5-business-day lead-time math only; no countdown timers / "only X left." (Data cited internally: soft "reply" email = 13% clicks; hard-sell blast = 0%) (source: Summer-of-Futbol-Context-and-Status_6.16.2026.md, 00-Campaign-Game-Plan.md).
3. **Premium, never cheap** — navy/copper system holds; team colors live only in real product photos (source: 00-Campaign-Game-Plan.md).
4. **Real photos only** — no AI-generated tents/stadiums/crowds; source real client/production shots (source: 00-Campaign-Game-Plan.md).
5. **"Design not included — we proof before production."** Offer free Canva templates + the free proof, never design services (source: 00-Campaign-Game-Plan.md). See [[file-prep-gate]].
6. **Drives to reply / call / free proof** — never "buy now" (source: Summer-of-Futbol-Context-and-Status_6.16.2026.md).
7. **Protect Venus** — pace to ~5–6 quality leads/day; the qualifier filters tire-kickers (source: 00-Campaign-Game-Plan.md). See [[closer-hire]].
8. **Channel firewall** — email blast = owned warm list only; exclude **DNC:institutional** (City of South Gate, Lynwood/LUSD, Nova Storage); cold prospecting stays on the separate warmed domain (CAN-SPAM); SMS marketing needs TCPA opt-in (source: 00-Campaign-Game-Plan.md). See [[shopify-tag-taxonomy]] and [[sms-marketing]].

**Meta concepts (all route to the same Instant Form):**

| Concept | Wedge | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| A — "See it free first" (HERO) | Free proof before you pay | Carousel (4 cards) incl. a real proof-vs-product mockup shot; EN/ES/Spanglish |
| B — "Start the 5-day clock" | Real 5-day lead-time, honest urgency | Single image / "here's the math" |
| C — "Real people in LA" | Local & bilingual — a neighbor who's a pro | Real Venus/Carlos portrait + carousel |
| D — Retargeting | Recognition-first | D1 = warm site visitors (`int:tents` / page viewers); D2 = past-customer/whale audience (162 list + Rancho Humilde, Alegre Music, Del Rio Brewing, Felix Botanica), rename-forward. Both EXCLUDE DNC:institutional |

(source: 02-Ad-Creative-Meta-and-Google.md)

**Meta CTA-button constraint:** "Get a Free Proof" is not a selectable native button. Use the closest native label (**"Get Quote"** EN / **"Cotizar"** ES, or "Send Message" for Concept C) and carry the free-proof promise in the free-text headline/description. On-image CTA chips and the carousel CTA card *do* say "Get a Free Proof" since those are PNG's own art (source: 02-Ad-Creative-Meta-and-Google.md).

**Meta Instant Form** — `PNG_WatchParty_FreeProof_2026`, "The 60-Second Free-Proof Qualifier." Start with More-volume; switch to Higher-intent if quality dips. 6 fields: what you need / business name / event-or-match-weekend / investment band ($100–$500, $500–$1k, $1k–$3k, $3k+, not sure — no sub-$100 band, $100 order minimum) / phone+email / preferred language. Privacy line = transactional consent only; **does NOT equal TCPA marketing-SMS opt-in** (keep a separate explicit opt-in) (source: 02-Ad-Creative-Meta-and-Google.md). See [[sms-marketing]].

**15-sec 9:16 Reel storyboard** — shared hero asset, real footage only: blank tent → customer's own logo "drops on" (in-camera, not CGI) → copper FREE PROOF stamp → 5-day install/setup → end card mirroring the Instant Form. No countdowns, no AI crowd/stadium (source: 02-Ad-Creative-Meta-and-Google.md).

**Google Search** — 1 ad group: "Custom Tents & Watch-Party Displays — LA." Responsive Search Ad with 10 headlines (pin only H1 "Custom Tents in Los Angeles" to position 1 — over-pinning tanks Ad Strength) + 2 descriptions; callout, call, and sitelink extensions. Keywords (phrase + exact) include "custom tents los angeles," "carpas personalizadas los angeles," "pop up tent with logo," "step and repeat backdrop la," "watch party setup la," "custom canopy with logo." Negatives keep rental/rent/used/cheap/wholesale/jobs but **drop a blanket "near me"** (high-intent local) (source: 02-Ad-Creative-Meta-and-Google.md). See [[google-search-engine]].

**Pricing posture on ads:** No price is stated on the ads by design — the path is concierge (lead → Venus quotes via draft order). SHOWUP/free-shipping lives on the landing page / quote, not the ad (source: 02-Ad-Creative-Meta-and-Google.md). **Lane: Carlos-VA** (stand up forms, audiences, UTMs, Pixel/Lead conversion).
