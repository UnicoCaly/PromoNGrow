## The system under it

- **Give-to-ask** ([[email-campaign-engine]]): 3 asks vs 8 value/goodwill sends across the arc (incl. the holiday pair). Asks live only inside the two windows; value emails carry at most a 1–2-sentence P.S. reply path.
- **Wrapper variety** (the local-fatigue rule): July window = "One last big weekend"; August window = **"The Fall Booth Kit"** — the same Event Pro Bundle economics under a fresh seasonal wrapper (the body says plainly it's the Event Pro Bundle, starting at $857, save 15%, SHOWUP free shipping over $500).
- **Value equation in the asks**: vivid outcome (the booth people remember) · likelihood (free proof before paying — the quick-win taste) · time (real dated math) · effort (reply with your logo, done).
- **Parr craft** ([[copywriting-method]]): curiosity subjects, punched first lines, open loops chained ACROSS emails (7/2 teases the series → each email plants the next → 05 tees up the 8/13 window → 09 teases October).
- **Steal-this as proof-of-craft**: emails 02 + 08 give away ready-to-post promo copy for the customer's OWN marketing — the most forwardable sends, and the templates themselves demonstrate the shop's craft.
- **Voice**: [[png-voice]] anti-AI hardened; delivery claim only "5 business days from proof approval" + 2–3 shipping — email 01 additionally reflects the 2026-06-30 decision (clock = proof approval **+ payment**); no fake urgency; FIFA-safe throughout.

## Merge tags — the Shopify Email gotcha (fixed 2026-07-02)

Shopify Email's customer first-name variable is **`{{ customer.first_name }}`**, NOT `{{ first_name }}`. The bare form has no variable in scope and renders **blank** ("Hi ,") — this is why Carlos's first paste showed no names. The confirmed-good syntax for this store is `{{ customer.first_name | default: "…" }}` (it's what the **April reactivation email that sent successfully** used). **All 11 emails were swept to the correct tag 2026-07-02** — greetings fall back to "there", mid-sentence vocatives to "friend", so a nameless contact never renders a broken comma. Paste the HTML as-is; no personalization setup needed. Subject lines with a name need the same tag (already inlined in each file's subject-option block). *(The already-sent `email-en.html` from 6/26 was left as the historical record and still carries the old syntax — if that blast rendered blank names, this is why; check its Shopify send.)*
