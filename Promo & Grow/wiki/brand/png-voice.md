## The voice in one line

A bilingual growth partner — warm, confident, encouraging, and real. Talks *with* you like a neighbor who happens to be a pro, never *at* you like a vendor (source: PNG-Brand-Strategy-and-Voice-Brief.md).

The locked register: *"Tu evento, tu marca, hecho bien. Send your logo, get a free proof, booth-ready in 5 days. Easy."* — bilingual, community, easy, reassuring (source: PNG-Brand-Strategy-and-Voice-Brief.md).

## Note on the earlier voice (superseded)

The April 2026 Full Context document framed the voice as a "shop-foreman-turned-editor" / "knowledgeable shop foreman" — clear, direct, no jargon, benefit-first, short sentences (source: PNG-Full-Context_4.14.2026.md). The May 2026 Brand Strategy & Voice Brief supersedes this with the bilingual *ganas* growth-partner register, which CLAUDE.md treats as canonical. The shop-foreman attributes (plain talk, no jargon, no fake urgency) survive *inside* the new voice; the framing and the bilingual layer are the upgrade.

## The five voice attributes

1. **Encouraging** — believe in the customer's hustle; hype the dream, not the product (source: PNG-Brand-Strategy-and-Voice-Brief.md).
2. **Direct & clear** — plain talk, no jargon, no fluff; specific ("the 5 days," "the free proof") (source: PNG-Brand-Strategy-and-Voice-Brief.md).
3. **Warm & human** — a real person who cares about your event going well (source: PNG-Brand-Strategy-and-Voice-Brief.md).
4. **Confident, never boastful** — confidence comes from delivering (the Donut Media setup went up fast and closed ~$30K in one night), not bragging (source: PNG-Brand-Strategy-and-Voice-Brief.md). *(The literal "3-day" Donut stat is retired from OUTWARD copy per the SLA-honesty rule — internal history only; outward copy says "fast," not a day count. See [[josh-craig-donut-media]].)*
5. **Bilingual by nature** — natural code-switching the way real bilingual Angelenos talk (source: PNG-Brand-Strategy-and-Voice-Brief.md).

## The anti-brand — what outward copy must NEVER be

- Never salesy or pushy — we equip, we don't pressure; the free proof is the opposite of a hard sell (source: PNG-Brand-Strategy-and-Voice-Brief.md).
- Never cold or corporate — no faceless print-vendor energy (source: PNG-Brand-Strategy-and-Voice-Brief.md).
- Never condescending — the *ganas* entrepreneur is a hero, not someone who "can't afford better" (source: PNG-Brand-Strategy-and-Voice-Brief.md).
- Never cheap-looking — accessible ≠ bargain-bin; the work and brand must look premium and legit (the #1 visual guardrail) (source: PNG-Brand-Strategy-and-Voice-Brief.md).
- Never generic — no stock-photo, templated, "any print shop" vibe (source: PNG-Brand-Strategy-and-Voice-Brief.md).
- Never overpromise — we say 5 days and hit 5 days; confidence through delivery, not hype (source: PNG-Brand-Strategy-and-Voice-Brief.md).
- Never unenthusiastic — genuinely excited about the customer's project and win (source: PNG-Brand-Strategy-and-Voice-Brief.md).

The April Full Context "we never sound like" list aligns: no Vistaprint-generic, no desperate/fake-urgency (no countdowns or "only 3 left"), no startup-disruption talk, no overly-casual "hey friend!" (source: PNG-Full-Context_4.14.2026.md).

## The bilingual craft rule (critical)

Spanglish only works with taste. Use natural code-switching the way bilingual LA actually speaks — a Spanish phrase that carries warmth (*"hecho bien," "con ganas," "tu marca"*) dropped into clear English, or fully parallel ES/EN where needed. Never machine-translated, never gimmicky, never forced. Done right it reads confident and modern to everyone, including the corporate buyer; done lazily it reads as a gimmick and cheapens the brand. When in doubt, read it aloud — if a real bilingual person wouldn't say it that way, rewrite it (source: PNG-Brand-Strategy-and-Voice-Brief.md).

## The anti-AI rule (sound like a person, not a bot)

Every outward line should read like a real bilingual Angeleno who happens to run a print shop wrote it — texting or emailing someone they respect. If it smells like an AI assistant being helpful, rewrite it. The test holds: **read it aloud; if you wouldn't actually say it to the person, cut it.** (source: Carlos direction 2026-06-25)

**Kill these AI-tell phrases on sight:**
- "Quick one" / "Quick question" / "Just a quick…"
- "I wanted to reach out" / "I'm reaching out to…" / "I hope this finds you well"
- "Happy to…" / "I'd be happy to" / "Feel free to…" / "Just wanted to…"
- "Thank you for reaching out" as a robotic opener (a warm, *specific* thanks is fine)
- "Rest assured" / "Look no further" / "At your earliest convenience"
- Corporate filler: "leverage," "seamless," "elevate," "unlock," "streamline," "in today's world," "we're thrilled to"

**Kill these structural tells:**
- **Em-dash overuse** — cap it around one per message; a period or comma usually does the job.
- The reflexive rule-of-three ("warm, fast, and easy") on every line — vary the rhythm.
- Over-symmetry, over-politeness, and stacked hedging ("just," "maybe," "I think").
- Starting every sentence with "I."

**Do this instead:** contractions, short real sentences, one clear ask, a specific detail about *them*, and a little ganas. Get to the point the way a busy person who genuinely cares would.

## Voice in action (examples)

- **Hero line:** "Tu evento, tu marca, hecho bien." / "Your event. Your brand. Done right." (source: PNG-Brand-Strategy-and-Voice-Brief.md)
- **Speed:** "Booth-ready in 5 business days from proof approval." (not "fast turnaround available") (source: PNG-Brand-Strategy-and-Voice-Brief.md; the brief's original "Guaranteed." wording is superseded — any customer-facing delivery line reads "5 business days from proof approval," never an unconditional guarantee (co-CEO 2026-06-17), see [[spanish-money-page-copy]])
- **Proof:** "Free proof before you pay — nothing prints without your approval." (source: PNG-Brand-Strategy-and-Voice-Brief.md)
- **Encouragement:** "You bring the ganas. We'll make sure you show up like a pro." (source: PNG-Brand-Strategy-and-Voice-Brief.md)
- **CTA:** "Send your logo →" / "Get your free proof →" — low-friction, conversational, never "BUY NOW" (source: PNG-Brand-Strategy-and-Voice-Brief.md)

## Repeating trust phrases

Used across pages, emails, and social: "Delivered in 5 business days from proof approval" (never an unconditional "guaranteed" — co-CEO SLA ruling 2026-06-17), "Free proof before you pay" (scope it to **$500+ / big-ticket** — tents, displays, banners; **not** "on every order," which over-promises on sub-$500 print where design is charged, per the [[master-price-sheet]] $500+ ruling), "Made in the USA," "4.8★ from 19 Google reviews," "20+ years in production," "Design not included — we proof before production" (source: PNG-Full-Context_4.14.2026.md; free-proof scope corrected 2026-07-13 per Carlos).

## The visual system

Working palette (core hexes per Brand/Design System/colors_and_type.css and [[visual-system]]; April Full-Context values superseded where they differ):

- **Navy `#1B2838`** — primary text + structural (nav, footer, headings, step numbers).
- **Copper `#C17A3A`** — accents / CTAs only; if it's copper, it must be clickable. Hover = copper-dark `#A66629` (`--pg-copper-dark`) (source: Brand/Design System/colors_and_type.css; the April Full-Context's `#A8682F` hover is superseded).
- **Trust Green `#1E6B4A`** — earned trust only (delivery badges, guarantee icons, savings).
- **White `#FFFFFF`** dominant surface (70%+); cream `#F5F2ED` (primary warm section break) and cream-soft `#F7F7F5` (lighter break) for section breaks/cards.
- Supporting (per tokens): secondary text `#495057` (`--pg-fg-2`), body `#6C757D` (`--pg-fg-3`), muted `#999999` (`--pg-fg-4`), borders `#F0F0F0` / `#E5E5E5` (source: Brand/Design System/colors_and_type.css; the April Full-Context's Slate `#2E3E50` and border `#EEEEEE` are superseded).

**Type — canon confirmed 2026-07-29 (Carlos):** display = **Cooper Black** (site-wide since 2026-06-24, matching the logo lettering; theme ships `cooper-black.woff2`; ad graphics aligned 2026-07-12) · headings = **Fraunces** 500–600 · body/UI = **Inter**. DM Serif Display remains the theme fallback behind Cooper + the print-collateral display face (catalog/proof sheets). DM Sans is still the live theme's body face — the body migration to Inter is an open Claude Code item. Eyebrow labels: Inter 500, 10–11px uppercase, 0.16em tracking, always copper. Full detail + tokens: [[visual-system]] (source: Carlos decision 2026-07-29; Brand/Design System/colors_and_type.css).

The Company & Creative Brief treats palette and type as the *current working system*, evolvable by the design lead ([[positioning-and-personas|Andrea engagement]]); the premium/warm/legit guardrail is the only non-negotiable, and CMYK/neon/vintage are exploratory prompts, not instructions (source: PNG-Company-Creative-Brief-for-Andrea.md).

## Two registers, kept apart

Wiki prose is plain and operational; the *ganas* voice is for outward copy only (PDPs, emails, SMS, social, decks). When generating an outward draft, switch fully into this voice; when filing facts, stay plain (per CLAUDE.md schema).
