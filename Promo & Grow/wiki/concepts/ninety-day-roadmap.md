# 90-Day Execution Roadmap

**Summary**: The four-phase, 90-day execution roadmap to a $20K-capable funnel — verify the bridge, establish an organic baseline (the green-light gate), then cold email, then paid. Operating law: prove organic conversion → THEN cold email → THEN paid. No spend until the funnel passes both the Strategist and Direct-Response checks.
**Type**: concept
**Sources**: PNG-90-Day-Execution-Roadmap.md
**Last updated**: 2026-06-18 (cost-per-sale reconciled: ~$60–70 superseded by <$130).

---

## Goal & operating law

Bridge cold web traffic into the [[concierge-close]] that already converts, prove it organically, THEN layer cold email + ads. Target: a $20K-capable funnel by peak season. **Operating law:** prove organic conversion → THEN cold email → THEN paid. No paid spend until the funnel passes BOTH the Strategist and Direct-Response checks (the CMAA lesson) (source: PNG-90-Day-Execution-Roadmap.md). This is the execution plan for the [[customer-journey-map]] / [[conversion-bridge]].

**Owners:** [C] = Carlos/VA · [CC] = Claude Code (theme) · [AI] = Claude (admin/copy/data).

## The funnel truth (what we're fixing)

180-day Shopify funnel: **3,936 sessions → 7 add-to-actions → 31 orders.** More orders completed than carts started = the revenue runs through the [[concierge-close]], not the web cart. The leak is at the top: cold visitors have nothing to do but a checkout they'll never use. The whole roadmap builds + feeds the "Get a Free Proof" on-ramp (source: PNG-90-Day-Execution-Roadmap.md).

## The two-mode gate (paste before every launch)

1. **Strategist:** right audience? right message? right time? protects the brand?
2. **Direct Response:** works on a phone Wednesday 9am? one-sentence ask? form tested? tracking live? follow-up staged? baseline established?

**Both must pass. Either fails → do not launch.** (The lesson that cost 82 burned visitors.) (source: PNG-90-Day-Execution-Roadmap.md)
