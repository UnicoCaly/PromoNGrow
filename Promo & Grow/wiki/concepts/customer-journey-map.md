## The leaky path (where it is today)

Cold traffic is asked to self-checkout a custom ~$900 order from a brand it just met. Most don't; the few who convert were already warm (source: PNG-Customer-Journey-Map.html).

| Stage | What happens | Metric |
|-------|--------------|--------|
| 1. Discover | Finds PNG via search/social/referral — mostly rented (ad-driven) traffic, no organic engine underneath | ~2,000 peak monthly sessions |
| 2. Browse (leak) | Lands on a PDP; sees price, "Add to Cart," little reassurance. Custom anxiety: *will it look right? are they legit? what's MY price?* | high bounce/exit |
| 3. Decide (leak) | Only path is full self-checkout — no "get a free proof," no easy way to ask. Cold visitors leave | 0.2–0.4% conversion (high-traffic months) |
| 4. Buy / Reorder | Warm & referral buyers call/email → draft order → invoice → pay. This motion **works** (2.5–3% in low-traffic months) — it's just starved of volume | ~$350 AOV |

**The core problem:** not a conversion problem — a **missing bridge**. The [[concierge-close]] (call → proof → invoice) converts beautifully, but cold organic traffic has no on-ramp into it — the only door is "Add to Cart," the wrong door for custom. Volume and the close never meet in the same month (source: PNG-Customer-Journey-Map.html).

## The concierge-bridged path (where it should be)

Give cold traffic a low-friction yes — "Get a Free Proof" — that drops them straight into the close PNG already wins. Keep checkout for the confident (source: PNG-Customer-Journey-Map.html). This is the [[conversion-bridge]], built per [[concierge-conversion-build]].

1. **Discover (earned)** — organic SEO/AEO/GEO + optimized Google Business Profile drives *owned* traffic; local "custom event tent LA" searches → calls. Target 2,000+ qualified sessions/mo.
2. **Trust** — PDP answers the 3 fears: free-proof guarantee, 4.8★ reviews on-page, "starting at" pricing, how-it-works in 3 steps → lower bounce.
3. **Raise hand** — "Get a Free Proof" is the hero CTA on custom items (a tiny yes); checkout stays primary on simple items. Lead captured either way.
4. **Close (concierge)** — speed-to-lead reply → free proof → draft order → invoice; auto follow-up on unpaid quotes recovers silent drop-offs; good-better-best tiers lift AOV.
5. **Reorder (LTV)** — saved profiles + "fabric refresh" win-back; brands & institutions (Donut, South Gate) reorder every season — the compounding layer.

## The bridge, in parts

What actually connects volume to the close (source: PNG-Customer-Journey-Map.html):

- **Free-proof CTA** — low-friction yes for custom items; the single highest-leverage change.
- **Trust on PDP** — reviews + proof guarantee + starting price kill the 3 hesitations.
- **Speed-to-lead** — reply in minutes, not hours; biggest predictor of closing a quote.
- **Quote follow-up** — 3-touch sequence on unpaid invoices recovers silent drop-offs.
- **GBP → calls** — local search drives phone calls straight into the best close ([[google-search-engine]]).
- **Reorder engine** — saved profiles + refresh win-backs turn one sale into many.

## The hidden engine — Proágo + Promo & Grow are one ecosystem

Same tagline ("Helping Brands Promote & Grow"), same LA market, same concierge motion — but they don't feed each other. The agency builds the brand; the store ships the physical presence. A client who gets a logo from Proágo needs tents, throws, and displays from PNG — and vice versa. **Cross-routing these two is a near-zero-cost growth channel sitting in plain sight** (co-CEO recommendation; surfaced as opportunity in PNG-Customer-Journey-Map.html).
