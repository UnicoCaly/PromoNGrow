**Summary**: The end-to-end play Venus runs on every lead from the Meta UGC ad — the funnel she now owns solo (Carlos gave her Meta access to messages, calls, and leads). Intake → 60-sec research → first touch → get the logo + spec → estimate → artwork to the **#promo-and-grow-artwork-proofer** Slack channel → proof → quote in Shopify → an aggressive-but-thoughtful follow-up cadence (Sam McKenna + Connor Murray blend). Leads are tracked in a **separate pre-Shopify sheet** until they're quoted, then they graduate to Shopify. Built for the live lead-form test (WhatsApp-vs-form A/B is parked until the WhatsApp number activates).

## The 8-step play (every Meta lead)

**1 — Intake (≤1 min).** New row in the **Meta Test Leads sheet** (below). The moment a lead exists it has a row — no lead lives only in Meta.

**2 — Research, 60 seconds ([[show-me-you-know-me-method]]).** Open their IG. Find one real, specific thing — a recent event they worked, what they sell, their vibe. That detail is the opener. This is the "thoughtful" half of the cadence; it's what makes the DM read 1:1 instead of like a vendor blast.

**3 — First touch (IG DM + email), assumptive ([[outbound-method]]).** Lead with the research hook → warm Venus intro → the free proof they asked for → **get what the estimate needs: the logo/branding (first + most important), what they want (product + qty), the event date, and the missing contact info (phone + address).** Scripts below. Then update the lead's **Meta status** (→ *qualified* once it's a real lead — see step 8).

**4 — Spec the job.** Once you know product + qty + size, that's the spec. Run the logo through the **[[file-prep-gate]]** (vector/hi-res, right size). Decide the offer lane with **[[proof-vs-design-policy]]**: tent/table-cover/flag work is **$500+ big-ticket → free proof applies**; if they pivot to small print (cards/flyers, sub-$500) → no free proof, design is charged off the [[design-price-rubric]].

**5 — Estimate.** Quote from the **[[master-price-sheet]]** (catalog = set price, no math). For a caterer, a tent + table covers + flags is a natural **bundle** (from ~$848 via the builder). Custom/not-listed → **route to Carlos**, never guess.

**6 — Artwork → #promo-and-grow-artwork-proofer.** Post the logo + the spec to the channel. A clean handoff carries everything the proof sheet needs (client, product, **finish size**, qty, material/lamination, notes) and the logo **at production spec** — for a tent that's CMYK / 150 dpi / safe 115"×78.85" / bleed 119"×84.28" (see [[proof-and-production-templates]]). **Interim process:** the proof is made by Carlos (for SOP/doc capture) or sent to **graphxsource** (Carlos's account) — until a part-time remote designer comes on. Carlos loads sent proofs into the **Proofer app** (Shopify) and is giving Venus access so she can see when each proof went out and when to follow up ([[venus-brief-2026-06-25]]). The future press team (human + AI) is being built around this channel.

**7 — Proof → approval → quote in Shopify.** Send the proof; *"nothing prints until you approve it."* Approval starts the **5-business-day** clock. Build the draft order in Shopify (SOP-02), attach the customer (SOP-03) — from here the deal lives in **Shopify**, and the standard [[deal-followup-playbook]] Day-1/3/7 quote-chase takes over.

**8 — Log everywhere.** Update the lead's **Meta status** — **intake → qualified → converted → lost** — on every lead, every stage. *This is not busywork:* Meta's algorithm uses these statuses to **optimize the ad** (it learns who actually converts → better future leads), so the priority is moving real ones to **converted** (confirmed in the [[venus-brief-2026-06-25]]). Also update the **Meta Test Leads sheet** and post to **#leads / #wins**.

## The aggressive-but-thoughtful cadence (pre-quote — get the logo + engagement)

The blend Carlos asked for: **Connor Murray** supplies the *aggression* (tight multi-touch, assumptive, channel-switching, no lead dies from silence); **Sam McKenna** supplies the *thought* (every touch references their business, pre-empts the objection, easy 1-2-3 out, no nagging). Channels: IG DM + email now; add SMS once a phone + consent exist. This runs **until they engage** (send the logo / reply) — then the deal moves to Shopify and the [[deal-followup-playbook]] quote-chase takes over.

| Touch | When | Channel | The move |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | First thing AM | DM + email | The research-led first touch (above): logo + spec + date, assumptive. |
| 2 | Same day, ~3–4 hrs later | switch channel | One line, references their business: *"Still want to get your free proof going for [La Joya / Raw Edge] — send the logo and I'll have a mockup back to you today."* |
| 3 | Day 2 | DM/email | The thoughts line + easy out: *"What are you thinking — even a 1-2-3 works: 1 let's go · 2 love it, not yet · 3 not now. Saw you've got [event/season] coming, so I can get the proof ready."* |
| 4 | Day 4 | switch channel | Pre-empt the objection (SMYKM): *"Most folks already have a printer — the difference is you see a free proof with your logo before you pay, made here in LA, ready in 5 days. Want me to run one so you can compare?"* |
| 5 — **takeaway** | Day 7 | text/email | Highest-response touch: *"I'll close your file so I'm not crowding your inbox — but your free proof's still here whenever [event] gets close. Just reply 1, 2, or 3."* |

After the takeaway with no reply → **Nurture**: tag `stage:nurture`, route to the [[reactivation-email-sequence]], 30-day re-touch, move on. **ABAB rhythm:** working two leads at once → alternate them day-by-day so each cadence stays light and the morning block stays ~an hour. **Replace the Proofer app's light auto-follow-up with this** — one voice, not two competing nudges.

## Two campaigns are running — consolidation is the plan

As of 6/24 there are **two** live campaigns: (1) an **in-app boosted** post (cheaper, broad, less control — ~13 leads at **$3.67** on the first read) and (2) the **Ads-Manager campaign** "Promo N Grow - Free Proof" ($35/day, EN + ES ad sets — the scalable infrastructure). The plan (Carlos + senior-exec read, 2026-06-26): **let the boost ride until its budget is spent, then consolidate onto the Ads-Manager campaign** as the one engine. The dollars are trivial at this scale; the binding constraint is **Venus's close capacity, not lead volume**, and the only number that decides anything is the **first close**, not CPL.

**Carlos's 6/26-AM todos (the clean-up that makes the Ads-Manager campaign a fair, scalable test):**
- **Move budget to the ad-set level** (it's currently CBO at the campaign level, which will starve one language) so EN and ES each get a fair share.
- **Swap the EN form → high-intent form with phone verification** (ES handled alongside) so form type is constant across languages → a clean ES-vs-EN read + closes the no-phone gap (phone = the highest-value channel for the concierge close). Expect **fewer leads at higher CPL** after — re-baseline; don't misread the jump as decay.
- **Spanish LP bounce (88.9%)** is under watch — `/pages/prueba-gratis` may be redirecting ES visitors to home or the EN page; Carlos is giving it a few days before acting. *(Instant-form leads never hit the LP, so this affects only the website-click slice — but if ES is misrouting, the ES read is muddied until it's fixed.)*
