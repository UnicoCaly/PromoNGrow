## How to use
- Sizes are **width × height** unless noted. Metric: **1 ft = 30.48 cm ≈ 0.30 m**; **1 in = 2.54 cm**.
- Human anchors: a person ≈ **6 ft**; a table ≈ **2.5 ft tall** (30"); a doorway ≈ **6.7 ft** (80").
- **Sanity-check size against the use before quoting** (see warnings at the bottom).

## 🎪 Tents (footprint)
| Tent | Footprint | Covers |
|---|---|---|
| 10×10 | 10 ft × 10 ft | ~100 sq ft — 1 booth |
| 10×15 | 10 ft × 15 ft | ~150 sq ft |
| 10×20 | 10 ft × 20 ft | ~200 sq ft — 2 booths |
Pop-up canopy: ~**6.5 ft** entry clearance, peak ~**9–10 ft** *(confirm exact peak with Carlos)*. **Back wall** 10 ft × ~6.5 ft · **half wall** 10 ft × ~3.25 ft.

## 🚩 Flags (total standing height, incl. pole)
| Flag | S | M | L | XL |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Feather (Angled / Convex) | 9 ft | 10.5 ft | 14 ft | 18 ft |
| Teardrop | 7 ft | 9 ft | 12.2 ft | 13.5 ft |
| Rectangle | 8.5 ft | 11.8 ft | 15 ft | — |
| Econo Feather | 16 ft (one size) | | | |
Most event use = **Medium or Large**. The printed graphic area is shorter than the total height (the pole/base adds height).

## 🟦 Table covers (fit the table)
| Table | Size | Cover options |
|---|---|---|
| 4 ft | ~48" W × 30" H | Draped throw |
| 6 ft | ~72" W × 30" H | Runner + blank · Draped · Stretch |
| 8 ft | ~96" W × 30" H | Draped · Stretch |
Ask the customer: **6 ft or 8 ft table?** — then match the cover.

## 🖼️ Displays & backdrops (width × ~height)
| Display | Width | ~Height |
|---|---|---|
| 3 ft SEG (tabletop) | 3 ft | tabletop |
| 8 ft SEG | 8 ft | ~7.5–8 ft |
| 10 ft SEG backdrop | 10 ft | ~7.5–8 ft |
| 20 ft SEG backwall | 20 ft | ~8 ft |
| Pillowcase backdrop | 3 ft / 4 ft | ~7.5 ft |
| Tension fabric | 6 / 8 / 10 / 20 ft | ~7.5–8 ft |
Backdrop standard height ≈ **89" (7.4 ft)** *(confirm per product)*. A **10 ft** backdrop fills behind one table; **20 ft** = a full step-and-repeat wall.

## 🏴 Banners — read this carefully (the 1×6 lesson)
**Tent banners (valance strips, mount on the tent):** widths 4'–20' but only **1 ft tall**. A "**1×6" = 6 ft wide × 1 ft tall = a thin strip** — fine as a tent-top valance, **too small to be a real backdrop or standalone sign.** Backdrop tent banners run **8'×3' · 8'×4' · 8'×5'** — that's what a customer who asks for "a banner" usually means.
**Standalone vinyl banners (13 oz):** **2×4 · 2×6 · 3×5 · 3×6 · 4×6 · 4×8 · 4×10 · 5×10 ft.** Price = width(ft) × height(ft) × **$5/sq ft** (round half-feet up).
**Default:** if a customer just says "a banner," a **3×6 or 4×8** reads at event distance. Only quote a 1-ft-tall strip when they specifically want a tent valance.

## 🖼️ Wall art (inches, not feet)
Acrylic standoff: **16×24 · 20×30 · 24×36 in**. Floating-frame & stretch canvas: 16×24 up. Remember a 24×36 is **inches** = 2 ft × 3 ft, not feet.

## ⚠️ Size warnings (say this before quoting)
- **"A banner"** → ask: backdrop (3×6+) or a tent valance strip (1 ft tall)? Don't default to the smallest.
- **Flags** → Medium/Large for visibility; Small is short.
- **Table cover** → confirm 6 ft vs 8 ft table.
- **Wall art** numbers are **inches** — much smaller than the same number in feet.
