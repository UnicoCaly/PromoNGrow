## The rule of thumb
**Built to take wind + weather = outdoor.** Frame-and-fabric pieces meant to stand still indoors **tip or tear outside.** When unsure, ask: *"Indoor, outdoor, or a covered patio?"* and match below.

## Outdoor-ready ✅
| Product | Why | Watch-out |
|---|---|---|
| **Event tents / canopies** | 600D polyester + heavy-duty aluminum frame, built for outdoor events | Always stake / weight the legs in wind |
| **Feather / teardrop / rectangle flags** | Designed to flex in wind on a ground/cross base | Use the right base (ground spike outdoors) |
| **13 oz vinyl banners** | Weatherproof vinyl, grommets for tie-down | Tie taut or they catch wind |
| **Tent banners (valance)** | Mount to the tent frame itself | n/a |
| **A-frame wraps · vehicle magnets · wraps** | Outdoor adhesive vinyl | Magnets: clean flat steel only |

## Indoor or sheltered only ⚠️ (will fail outdoors in wind)
| Product | Why | What to say |
|---|---|---|
| **SEG / tension-fabric displays** (3/8/10/20 ft, pop-ups, backlit) | Lightweight aluminum frame + stretched fabric — **catches wind like a sail and tips** | "These are built for indoors or a fully covered space — for an open outdoor booth I'd put you in a tent." |
| **Pillowcase backdrops** | Same tension-fabric build | Indoor / covered only |
| **Retractable / banner stands** | Tall, narrow, light weighted base — **tips in a breeze** | Indoor or set behind a table |
| **Wall art** (acrylic, canvas, frames) | Décor — indoor | Indoor |
| **PVC / foam-board signs** | Rigid board, not weather-sealed | Short indoor / covered use |

## Either, with care 🔁
- **Table covers / throws** — fine indoors or out; at an outdoor table, secure against wind. Fabric is washable.
- **Velcro pop-up displays** — primarily indoor; covered outdoor only, weighted *(confirm rating with Carlos)*.

## How to use it on a call
1. **Ask the environment first** — indoor / outdoor / covered.
2. **Outdoor open-air → tent + flags + vinyl banner.** Don't sell an SEG display or banner stand for an open outdoor booth.
3. If they ask for an indoor-style display outdoors, **say so and steer to the tent** — it protects them (and us — no wind-damage complaint).
4. Unsure on a specific item's rating → **route to Carlos**, don't guess.
