# Venus Cold-Email Training Module

**Summary**: The cold-email segment that layers onto the Monday [[venus-sales-seminar|seminar]] — ~30–40 min added after Part 3 (the method). The seminar teaches the *general* method (value statement, assumptive language, follow-up ladder, objection bank) on warm leads she works now; this module teaches the **cold-email engine that will feed her in July** (the Murray cadence + the Hormozi owned-list logic + how a cold email is built) **and** the **reply-handling skill she uses this week** on the inbound ads + Fútbol replies already landing in her queue. Pairs with the tools she'll run daily: [[venus-email-writing-engine]] (how she drafts) and [[cold-reply-playbook]] (how she handles a reply).

**Type**: concept

## Where this fits

Run it Monday **right after Part 3 of the [[venus-sales-seminar]]** (the method), before "working the queue." The seminar already gave her the spine on warm leads. This module shows her the **machine** that fills the top of her pipeline so she never depends on grinding dials, and the **reply skill** that turns any inbound into a free proof. Keep the energy: she's "employee #1 of the sales machine" — the machine prospects, she closes.

**The honest frame to open with:** *Cold email does not blast Monday.* The domain is still warming; first real cold sends are ~early–mid July ([[launch-week-roadmap]] gates). So this module is **(1) learn the engine now** + **(2) use the reply skill today** on the inbound that's already live.

## Part A — Why cold email, and what Venus's job is in it (5 min)

- **The machine emails; Venus closes the replies.** From [[outbound-method]]: a rep doesn't personalize 100 emails by hand — the prep is "coiled" upfront (homogeneous lists by segment, one template per segment), the tool sends, and **70–80% of replies come from follow-ups, not email #1**. Venus's job is **not** to send cold blasts — it's to take a reply and run it to a free proof.
- **Where the leads come from:** the [[apollo-net-new-list]] segments (event planners, breweries, caterers, real estate, party rentals) → loaded into the sender → **only responders graduate to her [[venus-work-queue]]**.
- **Her metric here is replies, not sends.** She owns the close; the machine owns the volume.

## Part B — The cold cadence (8 min)

Straight from [[cold-email-sequence]] — the bilingual **3-touch over 7 days**, every-other-day:

| Touch | Day | Job | The line |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Day 1 | The intro (**no link**) | who we are + free proof + 5 days → "worth a quick look?" |
| 2 | Day 3 | The proof (one link max) | a micro-case + "reply YES and I'll start a free mockup" |
| 3 | Day 7 | The easy out (breakup) | "should I close your file?" — removing pressure pulls the reply |

- **Why every-other-day (24–48h):** past 48h reply rates drop sharply; never more than ~2 business days between touches ([[outbound-method]]).
- **Why stop at ~4–5 touches:** after that you devalue yourself — move to a **fresh list**, return to non-responders in **6–8 weeks** (usually bad timing, not a real no).
- **The two highest-leverage follow-up lines** (she'll reuse these everywhere, including warm): *"Please give me your thoughts on this"* and the assumptive break-up *"Is next month a better time? Just want to close the loop either way."*
- **ABAB rhythm:** the morning email block is ~1 hour (list A Mon, B Tue, FU-A Wed, FU-B Thu) — the rest of the day is calls + closing. This is the same rhythm as her warm follow-up micro-sequence in [[deal-followup-playbook]], so it's one habit, not two.

## Part C — How a cold email is built (8 min) — so she can judge and edit any draft

The structure she must be able to recognize (and fix) — from [[outbound-method]] + [[cold-email-sequence]]:

1. **WHO / WHY / WHAT** in **3 short paragraphs**, **4–6 sentences, under ~90 words**, fits one phone screen. Short reads personal; long reads like a blast.
2. **Touch 1 = zero links** (links tank first-touch deliverability); touches 2–3 = one link max.
3. **Assumptive CTA**, never permission: *"reply and I'll start a free mockup,"* not *"would you be interested?"*
4. **The wedge in every touch:** free proof before you pay · 5 business days from approval · we speak Spanish.
5. **Subject lines: clarity beats cleverness** — 3–4 words, lowercase, human (`quick idea for {{business}}`, `una idea para {{business}}`); a genuine free offer is fine, a discount-blast subject is not.
6. **Bilingual only with taste** — match the prospect's language; read it aloud (the [[png-voice]] test).

She won't write these from scratch each time — she'll generate them with the [[venus-email-writing-engine]] and edit against this checklist. The point of this part is she knows what *good* looks like.

For the **highest-value targets**, there's a hand-built upgrade to the templated approach: the [[show-me-you-know-me-method]] (SMYKM — Apollo Academy / Sam McKenna). Same non-negotiables (Touch-1 no link, no calendar link, "5 business days from proof approval"), but a research-led subject line and an objection-pre-empting value prop — reserved for ~20 high-research emails/week on accounts worth the time.

## Part D — The warm list compounds (the Hormozi layer) (5 min)

From [[email-campaign-engine]] — the *other* email engine, the warm one:

- **The list is the asset.** Cold email + ads + inbound *build* the list; re-selling someone already on it costs ~$0, so list revenue is near-pure profit. PNG's ~1,221 dormant + warm universe is a free re-sell asset, and it opens at **32–39%** (vs Hormozi's 18%) because it's warm.
- **The cadence:** ~**4 seasonal "wrapper" campaigns a year** (Summer of Fútbol, event season, election-year, holiday), each with a **deadline + a quick-win (the free proof IS the quick win)**. Vary the wrapper so the small local list doesn't fatigue.
- **Give-to-ask:** value emails between promos so every ask is earned. Honest scale for PNG: **+$4–8K per campaign**, not Hormozi's millions (different economics).
- **Venus's role:** she works the *replies* these sends generate (same reply skill), and the [[reactivation-email-sequence]] / Thursday send is already part of her week.

## Part E — The reply-handling skill (8 min) — the part she uses THIS WEEK

This is the bridge to the [[cold-reply-playbook]] (her daily tool). The skill is identical whether the reply came from a cold email (July) or a paid ad / Fútbol DM (today):

1. **Triage in 30 seconds** — HOT / WARM / COOL using the [[lead-qualification-system]] fast read (real event + real date = WARM; clears budget + reachable in 5 days = HOT).
2. **Pivot to the free proof** — *"send me your logo and I'll get a free proof started, no charge, no commitment."* That's the next step, every time.
3. **Don't argue objections in the thread** ([[outbound-method]]): acknowledge → "makes sense to connect anyway" → point back at the proof. Lighter than a warm objection — she hasn't earned trust yet (the cold-objection lines live in [[cold-reply-playbook]]).
4. **Speed-to-lead by tier** ([[lead-qualification-system]]): paid/ad-form = **5 min**; warm inbound (incl. a cold-email reply) = **<1 hr** in her 10–4 PT window.
5. **Log it** — Source = Cold Email (or the real source), tier + next step in the Sheet, swap the Shopify `stage:` tag. If it's not tagged, it didn't happen.

## Live exercise (10 min)

1. **Critique an email (3 min):** show Touch 1 from [[cold-email-sequence]]; have Venus name the WHO/WHY/WHAT, confirm no link, spot the assumptive CTA. Then show a bad version (5 paragraphs, salesy subject, a link) and have her fix it.
2. **Handle a cold reply (4 min):** role-play a reply — EN *"how much for a 10x10 tent?"* and ES *"sí, mándame info."* She runs: triage → free-proof pivot → assumptive next step → name the log. Scripts she can lean on: [[cold-reply-playbook]].
3. **One objection (3 min):** *"just send me a price."* She acknowledges → "fastest real number is a free proof — what product + qty?" → starts the proof.

## Venus's takeaways (end of module)

1. The machine prospects; **she closes replies** — her cold-email metric is replies, not sends.
2. Every reply → **triage → free proof → assumptive next step → log it**, within her speed-to-lead tier.
3. Her draft tool is [[venus-email-writing-engine]]; her reply tool is [[cold-reply-playbook]]. Both also work on the inbound landing this week.
4. Cold doesn't blast Monday — it **warms**; first real sends are July ([[launch-week-roadmap]]).
