# Close Cadence Kit

**Summary**: The single asset Venus uses to chase every open quote to paid. A tight Day-1 / Day-3 / Day-7 + breakup cadence (call first, then text/email), bilingual follow-up templates EN+ES, an objection quick-bank, a frictionless Shopify pay-link step, and the priority order (Pedro first). The operating goal: **convert open quoted leads → paid.** The bottleneck is the close, not lead supply — ~28–30 Meta leads/day but no Meta-funnel closes yet, so this kit exists to land the first one.

**Type**: concept

**Sources**: [[deal-followup-playbook]]; [[draft-order-quote-system]]; [[concierge-close]]; [[meta-lead-intake-playbook]]; [[operating-rhythm]]; [[venus-work-queue]]; [[png-voice]]; co-CEO build session 2026-06-29.

**Last updated**: 2026-07-13 (folded in the ratified Deposit-to-Lock + 10%-in-trade discount authority; Venus quotes solo).

## When this kit applies

A quote is **sent** (a Shopify draft order is out) and the customer hasn't paid. From that moment the quote runs this cadence until it's **Won**, **Nurture**, or **Dead** — a decision, never a drift (the two ironclad rules from [[deal-followup-playbook]]: *no quote dies from silence; no lead sits without a next step*). Pre-quote lead-nurture (getting the logo + spec before a quote exists) lives in [[meta-lead-intake-playbook]]; this kit picks up the second the quote lands.

## The cadence — tight, capped, then nurture

Lead with the channel the customer used, **call first**, then text/email if no answer. Keep touches **24–48h apart** while warm; each touch carries one reason and one easy next step. Cap at the breakup, then move to monthly nurture — never the daily nag (the failure mode that cooled Del Rio + Smile Klub). Grounded in the Day-1/3/7 chase in [[deal-followup-playbook]] and [[draft-order-quote-system]].

| Touch | When | Channel | Job |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1 — sent the proof** | Day 1 (within 24h of the quote) | call → text/email | "Sent your free proof — what do you think? Even a 1-2-3 works." Re-open on the proof. |
| **2 — still here** | Day 3 | call → text/email | "Any questions on the proof?" Offer to adjust size/qty/budget. |
| **3 — close the loop** | Day 7 | call → text/email | Assumptive: hold the slot / start the 5-day clock. One clear go/no-go. |
| **4 — the breakup** | Day 10–12 | text/email | Takeaway + 1-2-3 reply — usually the **highest-response** touch. |

After the breakup with no reply → tag `stage:nurture`, route to [[reactivation-email-sequence]], set a 30-day re-touch, and **move on.** Stop dialing daily. **ABAB rhythm:** working two open quotes at once → alternate them day-by-day (and channels — call one day, text/email the next) so the block stays light. Each touch ends on an easy next step; nothing is a permission-seeking "let me know if…" (assumptive-language rule, [[deal-followup-playbook]]).

## Follow-up message templates (outward PNG voice — EN + ES)

> Bilingual *ganas* growth-partner — warm, confident, direct, never pushy. Read aloud; if a real bilingual Angeleno wouldn't say it, rewrite it. Anti-AI: no "just checking in," no "I wanted to reach out," one ask per message ([[png-voice]]). Channel-agnostic — paste into a text, read on a call, or send as email. Swap `[name]` / `[product]` / `[event]` per lead.

**ES:**
> "¡Hola [name]! Soy Venus de Promo & Grow. Ya te mandé tu prueba gratis del [product] — ¿qué te parece? Así la ves con tu logo antes de pagar nada. Aunque sea un 1-2-3 me sirve: **1** vámonos · **2** me encanta, pero aún no · **3** por ahora paso. En cuanto la apruebes, en 5 días hábiles lo tienes listo."

### Day-3 — still here, any questions on the proof

**EN:**
> "Hi [name] — still here for you on the [product]. Any questions on the proof? If the size, the quantity, or the budget needs a tweak, tell me and I'll adjust it. Want to make sure you show up like a pro for [event]."

**ES:**

### Day-7 — assumptive close-the-loop

**EN:**

**ES:**

### Stuck / cold quote revival (the breakup)

**EN:**

Reply **1** (lock it in), **2** (love it, not yet), or **3** (pass for now). Lo que sea, sin pena — and if it's budget or timing, dime y lo resolvemos."

**ES:**

Contéstame **1** (lo cerramos), **2** (me encanta, pero aún no) o **3** (por ahora paso). Whatever it is, sin pena — y si es presupuesto o timing, dime y lo resolvemos."

## Objection quick-bank

The move on **every** objection: **acknowledge → reframe to value → end on the free-proof / next step.** Value levers: free proof before you pay · delivered in 5 business days from proof approval · we handle the art · hablamos español · 4.8★ Google · 20+ yrs in LA. **Discount rule (RULED 2026-07-13): Venus's 10% closer's margin, in-trade only** (deposit today / pickup / Google review / referral) — never as an opener, never stacked; deeper or off-sheet → Carlos. **First answer to any budget objection = Deposit-to-Lock: 50% on $800+** freezes the price + a production slot for 60 days, balance at proof approval (full ruling on [[master-price-sheet]]). Full base copy lives in [[deal-followup-playbook]]; these are the four to know cold.

**Price — "more than I wanted to spend":** *(On $800+: lead with Deposit-to-Lock, then right-size, then 10%-in-trade — never open with the discount.)*
> EN: "Totally hear you — and you don't have to do all of it today. On this we can do **50% now to lock your price and a production slot for 60 days**, and the rest isn't due until you approve the proof. Or I can right-size it — a smaller tier or qty — so you still show up sharp. Want me to start a free proof so you see it first?" · ES: "Te entiendo — y no tienes que hacerlo todo hoy. En esto podemos hacer **el 50% ahora para apartar tu precio y tu lugar de producción por 60 días**, y el resto no se paga hasta que apruebes la prueba. O lo acomodo — un tier o cantidad más chica — y aun así llegas looking pro. ¿Te empiezo una prueba gratis para que la veas primero?" *(10% off only if they commit today — deposit/pickup/review/referral; deeper or off-sheet → Carlos.)*

**Timing — "not right now / can you go faster":**
> EN: "No problem — give me your in-hand date and I'll confirm the rush. I'm starting your proof now so the 5-day clock can start the second you approve it." · ES: "Sin problema — dame tu fecha de entrega y te confirmo el rush. Voy empezando tu prueba para que el reloj de 5 días arranque en cuanto la apruebes." *(Rush = custom → confirm with Carlos.)*

**"Just thinking about it" / "I'll get back to you":**
> EN: "Of course. So I help you right: is it the timing, the budget, or running it by someone? Either way I'll send the free proof now so it's in your hands when you decide." · ES: "Claro. Para ayudarte bien: ¿es el timing, el presupuesto, o lo tienes que consultar con alguien? Sea lo que sea, te mando la prueba gratis ahora para que la tengas en mano cuando decidas."

**"Found it cheaper":**
> EN: "Makes sense to shop it. The difference here: a free proof before you pay, we handle the art, delivered in 5 days, and a real person in LA who answers — 4.8★, 20+ years. Let me run one free proof so you can compare apples to apples, no switching just to look." · ES: "Tiene sentido comparar. La diferencia aquí: prueba gratis antes de pagar, nosotros hacemos el arte, entrega en 5 días, y gente real en LA que te contesta — 4.8★, 20+ años. Déjame correr una prueba gratis para que compares parejo, sin cambiarte nomás por ver."

## Get the logo first — proof before price (the 2026-06-30 reframe)

**The bottleneck:** of 48 Meta leads, only ~3 sent a logo — and a few got *quotes before a proof*. No logo → no proof → no desire → no close. **So lead with the logo, not the price.** The proof (their brand on the tent) is what sells; show it before any number.

**Proof-first push (EN):**
> "Hey [name]! The fastest way to see this is to *see it* — send me your logo (any format, even a screenshot) and I'll put it on your [tent/flag] and send you a **free proof today**. No cost, no commitment — you just react to the picture. Got a sec to send it over?"

**Proof-first push (ES):**
> "¡Hola [name]! La forma más rápida de verlo es *verlo*: mándame tu logo (cualquier formato, hasta una captura) y te lo pongo en tu [carpa/bandera] y te mando una **prueba gratis hoy**. Sin costo ni compromiso — solo reaccionas a la imagen. ¿Me lo puedes mandar ahorita?"

Hold pricing until after they've seen the proof. (The WhatsApp + DM ad funnels — see [[meta-ugc-launch-kit]] — exist to make sending the logo one tap, since the Meta lead *form* can't accept a file.)

## Summer / World Cup urgency + the downsell ladder (Carlos 2026-06-30)

**The urgency is real, not a tactic.** The 2026 World Cup is on *now* through **July 19** (LA is a host city) and it's peak event season — these vendors make their summer at exactly these events. Use it:
> EN: "The events are happening *right now* — let's get your setup ready so you're capturing this summer, not watching it pass." · ES: "Los eventos están pasando *ahora mismo* — vamos a dejarte listo para que aproveches este verano, no que se te vaya." *(Honesty: don't promise 4th-of-July delivery — production is 5 days — sell the summer, not Saturday.)*

**"Out of budget" → lock half, then step down — don't walk.** On an $800+ quote the first move is **Deposit-to-Lock: 50% today freezes the price + a 60-day production slot**, balance at proof approval (ruling on [[master-price-sheet]]) — they don't need all of it now, just half to freeze the deal. If even half is too much, step down: a couple of **feather flags ($120–185)** or a **table cover** gets them set for the summer, and the tent follows. A small order (or a deposit) today beats a big quote that dies.
> EN: "Totally get it. Let's start you smaller — a couple of flags or a table cover — so you're ready for this summer's events now, and we build up to the tent when you're ready. Want me to proof a flag with your logo?" · ES: "Te entiendo. Empecemos más chico — unas banderas o un mantel — para que estés listo para los eventos de este verano, y subimos a la carpa cuando puedas. ¿Te hago una prueba de una bandera con tu logo?" *(Time is the most valuable thing they have — start small, start sooner.)*

## The "why didn't you buy?" diagnostic (run on every pass)

A decline is data. Ask kindly — the answer tells you how to win them, and feeds the Meta lead-status loop:
> EN: "Totally understand — and no pressure at all. Just so I can help better: is it the **price**, the **timing**, or wanting to make sure we're **legit**? Whatever it is, it helps me." · ES: "Te entiendo perfecto — sin presión. Solo para ayudarte mejor: ¿es el **precio**, el **timing**, o quieres asegurarte de que somos **reales**? Sea lo que sea, me ayuda saberlo."

- **Price** → the downsell ladder above. · **Timing** ("saving up / event's later") → set a *dated* follow-up, don't lose them. · **Legit?** → send the **Google Business Profile link** (4.8★) + "made in LA, 20 yrs" — trust, not a visit. Log the reason in the tracker + set the Meta status (*lost* if truly dead — feeds the algorithm).

## Frictionless pay step — get paid fast

The money loop stays in **Shopify** — do not route the payment anywhere else. Once the proof is approved and the quote is right:

1. **Send the pay-link.** In the Shopify draft order, use **Send invoice** (emails a branded invoice with a *Pay now* / checkout link) — or **Collect payment → Send payment link** to share the link directly by text/WhatsApp ([[draft-order-quote-system]] SOP step 8). One clear ask: pay here.
2. **Confirm receipt.** "Did the invoice land? Tap *Pay now* on it and you're set." Make the click the only thing left to do.
3. **Restate the promise on payment.** The moment it's paid: *"¡Cerrado! Delivered in 5 business days from proof approval."* Tag `stage:won`, hand to [[fulfillment-sop]].

**Pay-link push (outward):**
> EN: "Sent your invoice — tap **Pay now** on it and you're locked in. The second it's paid, your 5-business-day clock starts and we're off. Let me know the moment it lands." · ES: "Te mandé tu factura — dale a **Pagar ahora** y quedas listo. En cuanto se pague, arranca tu reloj de 5 días hábiles y nos vamos. Avísame cuando te llegue."
