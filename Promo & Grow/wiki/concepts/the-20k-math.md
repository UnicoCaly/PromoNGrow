# The $20K Math

**Summary**: The arithmetic behind the $20K/month goal — 2,000 qualified sessions × 2.5% conversion × $400 AOV — plus the peak/floor timing and the December seasonality reality.

**Type**: concept

**Sources**: PNG-Master-Context-Playbook.md, PNG-Full-Context_4.14.2026.md

**Last updated**: 2026-06-17.

**Metric**: $20,000/month target; AOV ~$400 (Master Playbook) / ~$350 Shopify (Full Context); conversion 2.5%.

---

## The math

> 2,000 qualified sessions × 2.5% conversion × $400 AOV = $20,000/month (source: PNG-Master-Context-Playbook.md).

Each number has already been individually achieved: the 2.5% conversion is the proven warm/concierge rate (see [[concierge-close]]), and **$400 is the bundle-lift TARGET AOV** — the *measured* Shopify average is ~$350, concierge/phone orders running higher (source: PNG-Master-Context-Playbook.md, PNG-Full-Context_4.14.2026.md). The lever that closes the gap is volume of *qualified* sessions routed into the close, via the [[conversion-bridge]].

## AOV — two figures

- **~$400 AOV — the bundle-lift TARGET** used in the $20K math (source: PNG-Master-Context-Playbook.md).
- **~$350 AOV on Shopify**, higher for phone/quote orders, per the April Full Context (source: PNG-Full-Context_4.14.2026.md).

Both are cited and dated; the $400 figure is the one used in the goal math. Phone/quote (concierge) orders run higher than the Shopify-checkout average.

## Timing — peak and floor

- Hit $20K at **peak (Oct–Nov 2026).**
- Lock it as a **sustained floor by Q1 2027** conference season (source: PNG-Master-Context-Playbook.md).

## Seasonality reality

B2B events die over the holidays; **December is historically weak (~$1K).** Don't force $20K against December — counter with wall-art/gifting + Q1 pre-booking (source: PNG-Master-Context-Playbook.md).

The YTD pattern supports the seasonality read: Jan $4,598, Feb $693, Mar $390, Apr $1,553 (Shopify, Jan–Apr 11 2026) — a strong January then a sharp trough (source: PNG-Full-Context_4.14.2026.md).

## Goal-target evolution (resolved 2026-06-17)

The April Full Context framed P&G's target as **$5–8K/month** ("scale from ~$2K/month autopilot"), with P&G as bridge revenue funding the Proágo transition (source: PNG-Full-Context_4.14.2026.md). The May Master Playbook raises the goal to **$20K/month by year-end, 85% automated** (source: PNG-Master-Context-Playbook.md). **Carlos confirmed 2026-06-17 that $20K/month is the live target; the April $5–8K figure is superseded.** See [[dashboard]].
