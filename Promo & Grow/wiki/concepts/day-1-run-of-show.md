## Week-1 guardrails

Carlos reviews every quote before it sends (Week 1); aggressive = speed + persistence, never pressure (respond in minutes, follow up Day 1/3/7, high daily activity — the data says conversation closes and hard-sell flops, 13% vs 0%); no discounts without Carlos's OK during the trial; list price is the price; the floor is the $100 minimum order; tag every lead in Shopify so follow-ups don't slip (source: 00-START-HERE-Day-1-Run-of-Show.md).
