# Competitive War Room

**Summary**: A top-down teardown of six direct competitors (user journeys + ad posture), the conversion-mechanic gaps that explain "nice catalog, no carts," a two-track game plan (concierge bridge now / self-serve machine in parallel), and the five wedges no competitor combines. Predecessor analysis the Growth Offensive sits inside.
**Type**: concept
**Sources**: PNG-Competitive-War-Room_6.08.2026.html
**Last updated**: 2026-06-18 (cost-per-sale reconciled: ~$60–70 superseded by <$130).

---

## The funnel truth being fixed

PNG's 180-day Shopify data: **3,936 sessions → 7 add-to-cart actions (0.18%) → 31 orders** via the human concierge close. More orders than carts means revenue runs through the [[concierge-close]], not the web cart; the leak is at the top — cold visitors have nothing to do but a checkout they'll never use. The $20K reference math: 2,000 sessions × 2.5% × $400 AOV (source: PNG-Competitive-War-Room_6.08.2026.html). See [[the-20k-math]] and [[conversion-bridge]].

## The strategy spine — two tracks, sequenced

- **Track A · Now (revenue):** the concierge bridge — give cold traffic a low-friction on-ramp into the close that works: "Get a Free Proof." Tiered CTA (quote-first on tents/displays/bundles, cart-first on flags/covers), free-proof form → auto-reply + speed-to-lead alert → draft order, trust stack on every PDP, pixels installed now. Measure quote requests, not checkouts.
- **Track B · Build-toward (the machine):** engineer the $1,500 add-to-cart the way Expoprint & Signwin do — package-ize SKUs + visible 4-step ribbon, locked Canva templates now → 3D configurator later, "Free AI Artwork Check," Shop Pay installments, bilingual Spanish — moats none of them combine.

Every concierge close documents what the website must eventually self-serve (source: PNG-Competitive-War-Room_6.08.2026.html). See [[draft-order-quote-system]] and [[pipeline-ops-spine]].

## The gap matrix (where PNG stands vs the target)

**PNG already wins the rows nobody else stacks:** free proof before pay ✓ + 5-day default ✓ + real owned LA production ✓ + bilingual (partial today → target ✓). **PNG is behind on self-serve rows** (3D/live configurator ✗, AI file check ✗, financing ✗, instant price partial) and **trust-surfacing rows** (reviews on PDP partial, hard guarantee partial). Track A closes the trust rows fast; Track B closes the self-serve rows over the quarter (source: PNG-Competitive-War-Room_6.08.2026.html).

## What to steal (highest-leverage)

- 4-door homepage intent router (ExpoMarketing) — cleanest model for sequencing self-serve vs concierge.
- Per-grid star ratings on collection pages (Displays2go) — decision-moment proof.
- Site-wide stacked trust ribbon: price + satisfaction + fast shipping + free proof (Signwin).
- 4-step "buy → design → approve → deliver" reassurance ribbon on high-ticket PDPs (Packeze).
- Size-segmented landing pages 10×10 / 10×15 / 10×20 + named "meet your team" reassurance block (Instent).
- Multi-product 3D scene + three artwork lanes on every PDP (Expoprint).
- Badge a named "Free AI Artwork Check" — Signwin proves the market wants it (source: PNG-Competitive-War-Room_6.08.2026.html).

## The gated game plan (6/08 version)

Gated discipline — prove organic → then cold email → then paid (source: PNG-Competitive-War-Room_6.08.2026.html):

| Phase | Window | Focus | Gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Wks 1–2 | Verify the bridge: tiered "Get a Free Proof" CTA + quote form + trust stack; install Meta Pixel + Google tag | Funnel verified, pixels collecting |
| 2 | Wks 3–4 | Organic baseline: GBP, VIP + South Gate outreach, SEO/AEO, 4-door router, size-segmented pages | Quote requests ~2 → 20+/mo; add-to-action 0.18% → 1%+ |
| 3 | Wks 5–8 | Cold email: separate sending domain, LA-metro ICP, drive to free proof | Reply 2–5%, positive 1–3% |
| 4 | Wks 9–12 | Paid + retargeting: $5–10/day retargeting then Google Search/Shopping; CPS < ~$60–70 *(superseded → <$130)* | Profitable cost-per-sale on one channel → scale into Oct–Nov peak |

**Cost-per-sale — canonical figure:** this 6/08 plan held cost-per-sale under **~$60–70**; the later, hardened **2026-06-15** [[growth-offensive]] scoreboard sets the canonical target at **cost-per-closed-deal <$130** (rolling 60-day), with a fix-or-cut line at >$250. **<$130 is the figure to use**; the ~$60–70 above is superseded and kept only as the original 6/08 plan of record (source: PNG-Growth-Offensive-GamePlan_6.15.2026.md). See [[growth-offensive]], [[google-search-engine]], [[south-gate-account]], [[vip-reactivation-list]].

## PNG's five unfair wedges (positioning spine)

Every one of the six competitors is missing at least one; PNG can stack all five: (1) **5-day delivery** (hard named promise vs their 7–15); (2) **free proof before you pay** (half proof only after payment); (3) **bilingual Spanish** (zero of the six); (4) **real LA production** (owned, 20 yrs, not a broker network); (5) **AI artwork check** (coming — only Signwin badges it today) (source: PNG-Competitive-War-Room_6.08.2026.html).
