Segment bundles: **El Mercado Kit $1,099** (vendors) · **Grand Opening Kit $1,749** · **Food & Catering Kit $1,349** · **Fight Camp/Gym Kit $2,249** · **Car Club/Show Kit $2,799** · **Trade Show Pro $2,999** — each with a free-banner-class incentive; margins honest at current 44% structure (43–51%), recompute post-repricing (workbook Bundles tab is formula-live).

## The ladder
UP (one bump per touch): wheels bag **+$79** (currently an accidental freebie) · walls +$240/$320 · 2nd flag −10% (the sanctioned use of Venus's trade discount) · matching tent banner half-off (best economics in the store) · LED slot +$480 when the sample passes ([[led-tent-vendor-cfm]]). DOWN (in order): Deposit-to-Lock → **Sidewalk Kit $159** (flag + banner) → Print-Only Canopy $350/$420 (61–62% margin — activate from DRAFT) → Starter $899. Downsell = future upsell only if the deferred item is logged.

## Incentive menu (non-discount currency)
Free proof ($500+ scope per the 6/22+7/13 rulings) · free banner w/ bundle · Deposit-to-Lock · free 2nd revision · review = $50 credit · referral = $100 each side · pickup 10% (in-trade) · true slot scarcity (7/wk) · LED early-access waitlist ($100 refundable). **Deliberately absent:** blanket % discounts, sitewide free shipping, price-matching.
