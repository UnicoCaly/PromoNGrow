# Concierge Close

**Summary**: The core diagnosis behind P&G's whole growth strategy — the business converts through a human concierge close (call/email → draft order → invoice → pay), not the web cart. The 180-day funnel data shows more completed orders than carts started, so the problem is a missing on-ramp, not a conversion problem.

**Type**: concept

**Sources**: PNG-Master-Context-Playbook.md, PNG-Full-Context_4.14.2026.md, PNG-Closer-Onboarding-Workbook.md

**Last updated**: 2026-06-17.

**Metric**: on-site add-to-action 0.18% (near-dead cart); concierge-warm conversion 2.5–3% in low-traffic/warm months vs 0.2–0.4% in high-traffic/cold months (source: PNG-Master-Context-Playbook.md).

---

## The diagnosis

The business runs on a **concierge close, not self-serve checkout.** Customers call/email → Carlos builds a Shopify draft order → sends an email invoice → they pay. This is why most products sold are "custom," and why ~$20K of historical revenue logged against blank/untitled products (those were invoices, not bugs) (source: PNG-Master-Context-Playbook.md).

The current fulfillment flow confirms the human touch: client orders or emails/calls → Carlos reviews artwork manually → places production order with B2Sign or 4over → product ships to client or to Lynwood for pickup, ~5 business days (source: PNG-Full-Context_4.14.2026.md). See [[store-and-catalog]] for vendors.

## The funnel data (180 days)

3,936 sessions → 7 add-to-cart actions → **31 completed orders.** **More orders completed than carts started** = revenue comes through the concierge close, not the web cart (source: PNG-Master-Context-Playbook.md).

- On-site cart is nearly dead: 0.18% add-to-action (source: PNG-Master-Context-Playbook.md).
- The human close works: 2.5–3% conversion in low-traffic/warm months vs 0.2–0.4% in high-traffic/cold months (source: PNG-Master-Context-Playbook.md).

## The conclusion: a missing bridge

P&G does not have a conversion problem — it has a **missing bridge.** Cold organic traffic has no low-friction on-ramp into the concierge close that already converts; the only on-site door is "Add to Cart" (the wrong door for custom). The fix: add a **"Get a Free Proof" / quote path** that feeds the concierge close, and keep checkout for simple/low-ticket items (source: PNG-Master-Context-Playbook.md). See [[conversion-bridge]] and [[the-20k-math]].

## How the close is run

The bilingual closer (see [[closer-hire]]) works a single warm queue — email repliers/clickers, SMS repliers, inbound calls, the VIP list, and inbound quote requests — closing with warmth, not pressure, anchoring every quote on the **free proof** and **5-day delivery** (source: PNG-Closer-Onboarding-Workbook.md). Quotes are built as Shopify draft orders; catalog items use a set price sheet, custom items use a markup formula (low-ticket ×4–6, high-ticket 80–100% markup), with a never-quote-below floor (source: PNG-Closer-Onboarding-Workbook.md). Good-better-best tiers are offered where natural (e.g. Tent Only / Half Wall / Complete Set) (source: PNG-Closer-Onboarding-Workbook.md). The step-by-step SOP for building those draft-order quotes is [[draft-order-quote-system]] (Station ③ of the [[lead-machine]]).

This human bridge runs until the website self-serves — the long-term automation goal (source: PNG-Master-Context-Playbook.md). See [[reactivation-email-sequence]] and [[sms-marketing]] for the channels that feed the queue.
