## The system (headline)
Five beats, 60–90s, one take: face+name → **their proof/logo/quote ON SCREEN** (the trick) → ONE helpful thing (price said proudly / deposit line / the stall answer) → [LAUNCH BLOCK, off until the 7/24 GO] → one-question close. Full EN+ES power-line bank on the source doc. Rules: video never delays the quote (same-day draft stands) · never a discount on camera · every video = a logged cadence touch · standing rule: **no quote goes out naked** (every estimate ships with a video, all sizes).
