## Venus's role here

Once a client commits (a paid order or a **[[master-price-sheet|Deposit-to-Lock]]**), Venus becomes the **project point of contact**: she pulls the details out of the client, collects and vets the files, sets the right expectations, and hands a clean, complete brief to the design/proof team (**#promo-and-grow-artwork-proofer**). She doesn't design — she makes sure design gets everything it needs the first time. Done well, this is what makes the 5-day promise real and keeps the client feeling looked-after.

## The intake flow (six steps)

### 1. Gather the brief — what, where, how big

Get on a call or message and pin down:
- **The pieces + specs:** finished **dimensions** (W×H, confirm inches/feet), **material/substrate**, **quantity**, **single vs double-sided**, **orientation**, and **placement / where it lives** (indoor vs outdoor, wall vs window, vehicle — this drives material + durability).
- **Placement + messaging:** for multi-surface jobs (like a storefront), exactly **what copy goes where**. Get the final, typo-checked text.
- **The deadline:** the **event / in-hands date**, not "whenever" — and whether it's date-critical.
- **Color-match needs:** must it match an existing sign, a brand Pantone, or a prior order? Get the reference in hand.
- **Look at their world:** check the client's **website + past designs** for real brand colors and style before recommending anything.

### 2. Request the files — vector first

Ask for these, in order (the **logo in vector** is the single most important file):
- **Vector logo** — native **.AI**, plus **.EPS** and/or a **vector .PDF** (and **.SVG** if they have it). Vector scales to any size and always prints razor-sharp.
- **Source / editable files** — "Do you have the file this was made in?" (layered .AI/.PSD/.INDD) so design can edit colors, resize, outline fonts.
- **Raster only if no vector exists** — the **highest-resolution PNG (transparent background) / JPG / TIFF** they have. Flag immediately it may not be print-ready (step 3), and note the pixel dimensions.
- **Brand assets** — brand colors as **Pantone/CMYK** and the **font names**; logo variations (full-color, all-black, white/reverse); any photos to print (at full resolution).

### 3. Check file quality — and handle a low-res logo honestly

Quick checks + what to say:
- **Vector preferred**; if raster, confirm the **real pixel dimensions** vs the finished size — a small screen/social logo blown up to banner size pixelates. (Resolution needs scale with viewing distance: close-up ~240–300 DPI, signs from several feet ~150 DPI, big banners from a distance lower.)
- **Color mode = CMYK** (see caveat in step 4); **fonts outlined/embedded**; **bleed + safe area**; **transparent background** on logos.
- **If the logo is low-res or screen-only, say so at intake — don't quietly print something blurry.** Then offer:
  - **Best/free:** ask for the original vector, or for them to get it from whoever made the logo.
  - **Paid fix:** offer a **cleanup / vectorize** (redraw) so it prints sharp at any size — quote from the **[[design-price-rubric]]** (vectorize $50 · etc.), note it's reusable forever. Position it as *protecting their brand*, not an upsell.
  - If they decline and insist on the low-res file, **note it on the order** and get their written OK that quality was flagged.

### 4. Catch production caveats — before proofing

Flag anything that won't reproduce **before** design spends time on it:
- **Out-of-gamut / neon / fluorescent colors** can't be printed — explain the light-vs-ink reality and steer to brand colors or the specialty-vinyl upgrade. Full script + options: **[[print-color-and-material-limits]]**.
- Any specialty material/finish (neon film, metallic, backlit) is a **scope + price change** → route to Carlos ([[master-price-sheet]] custom items).

### 5. Set expectations — the flow, up front

Say it plainly at intake so there are no surprises:
- **Free proof → your written approval → production clock starts.** *"Nothing prints until you approve the proof."* (Free proof is on **$500+ big-ticket** items — [[master-price-sheet]].)
- **The proof is the last free-change moment** — spelling, colors, sizes, phone/URL, quantity, sides, correct logo version. After approval, materials are allocated; later changes mean re-charges + lost time.
- **Approval must be in writing** (email "approved" or a click-to-approve). A verbal OK doesn't start production — even under deadline pressure.
- **The clock = 5 business days from proof approval *and* payment** ([[venus-brief-2026-06-30]]), so slow approval pushes the finish. Tie it to their date: *"To hit [event date], I need your approval by [date]."*

### 6. Hand off to design/proof

Package a clean brief into **#promo-and-grow-artwork-proofer**: files + specs + placement/messaging + deadline + any flagged caveats. Then chase the proof → approval → payment per [[close-cadence-kit]] / [[draft-order-quote-system]].

## Design-advice patterns (what to recommend)

Venus can guide clients toward designs that work:
- **Storefront / windows:** keep the **entry doors simple** — hours, phone, website, social, a QR code — and put **promotions on the other windows**. (The Smile Klub pattern, below.)
- **Less is more / brand-consistent:** steer toward the client's real brand colors and clean hierarchy over cluttered, off-brand asks — it looks more pro *and* prints reliably.

## Worked example — Smile Klub Dental (Shadi)

Window vinyl graphics for a dental clinic ([[venus-work-queue]]):
- **Brief:** simple front doors (hours · phone · website · social · QR); promo windows with the offers (FREE EXAMS & XRAYS · acceptamos Medical / most PPO · braces $75/mo · implants $69/mo · veneers $49/mo).
- **File/asset check:** pull her logo (vector if possible), check her website for brand colors.
- **Caveat:** she wants a **neon-yellow background** → can't be printed; recommend brand colors, or the ~$800 fluorescent-film upgrade ([[print-color-and-material-limits]]).
- **Missing piece:** email her to confirm **exact store hours**.
- Proof already exists (`SK_windows_proof.pdf`); Venus reviews placement/messaging with her, then finalizes.
