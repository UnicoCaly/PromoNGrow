## Lead qualification
Carlos and Venus aligned on the qualification tiers (00:18:17–00:20:56), matching [[lead-qualification-system]]:
- **Hot** — a real event date / concrete purchase interest.
- **Warm** — interested, asks roughly what it costs, but no timeline / not ready.
- **Cold** — newly aware ("I didn't know you guys did that").
- **Disqualified** — unresponsive, a competitor / does the same thing, or otherwise a poor fit (more of these appear as volume kicks up).
