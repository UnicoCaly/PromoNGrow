## Part 2 — The two lead lanes (10 min)

## Part 3 — The method (25 min) — the heart of the seminar
From [[outbound-method]] (read it after). The spine for every call and email:
1. **Value statement — WHO / WHY / WHAT.** One line who you are, the *value* (free proof + 5-day + we handle the art), and the ask. Deliver value first.
2. **Assumptive language (the #1 habit).** Not *"would you be interested in a quote?"* → **"I'll get a free proof started — what quantity are you thinking?"** State the next step as if it's happening; ask only about a detail.
3. **The next step = the free proof.** You're not closing the whole order in one breath — you're getting them to a **free proof** (the quick win that builds belief). Then the quote, then the close.
4. **The follow-up ladder** ([[deal-followup-playbook]]): no quote dies from silence. Day-1 benefit-of-the-doubt → *"what are your thoughts? even a 1-2-3 works"* → assumptive close-the-loop. Tight (24–48h), capped, then nurture.
5. **The objection bank** ([[deal-followup-playbook]]): acknowledge → reframe to value → **end on the free-proof next step.** Don't argue price — right-size and send the proof. (Never discount past the $100 floor — escalate to Carlos.)
6. **Tonality:** warm, confident, never pushy; bilingual by nature. Rehearse the openers so they're natural.
