## Venus's daily cadence

The close runs on rhythm, not heroics. A repeatable day:

**1. Morning queue (first 30 min).**
Open the [[lead-tracker-sheet|Pipeline tab]]. Sort by **Tier** then **Next Action Due**. Triage in the closer game-plan order: inbound today → recent email engagers → VIP/repeat → broader openers → cold-not-yet (source: PNG-Closer-Onboarding-Workbook.md). Anything overdue (red) is handled first.

**2. Speed-to-lead (all day, interrupt-driven).**
Any new lead in **#leads** gets a response **within minutes if possible, same business day at worst** — first to respond usually wins (source: conversion-bridge.md, PNG-Closer-Onboarding-Workbook.md). Log it as a row immediately, set Stage = Contacted, claim it in Slack.

**3. Follow-ups (the Day-1/3/7 cadence).**
Work every **Quote Sent** lead on the schedule: Day 1 resend the proof, Day 3 offer to adjust, Day 7 a deadline nudge (source: PNG-Closer-Onboarding-Workbook.md). The Sheet's amber-after-7-days flag surfaces stalled quotes — the hidden revenue (source: conversion-bridge.md).

**4. End-of-day sync (last 15 min).**
For every lead touched today: update **Stage**, **Next Action**, **Next Action Due**, **Notes** (datestamped) in the Sheet, and **swap the matching `stage:` tag** in Shopify (source: shopify-tag-taxonomy.md). Post the day's wins to **#wins**. This EOD sync is the discipline that keeps the Sheet and tags honest until Zapier takes it over (see [[zapier-automation-roadmap]]). (co-CEO recommendation)

## Weekly 15-min scoreboard review (Carlos + Venus)

One short standing meeting — 15 minutes, same time weekly [Carlos to provide: day/time; suggest Monday 10am PT to set the week]. Read the [[lead-tracker-sheet|Scoreboard tab]], do not rebuild it. The KPIs:

| KPI | Why | Healthy direction |
|---|---|---|
| Leads in | Top-of-funnel volume | Up (toward 2,000 qualified sessions/mo — source: the-20k-math.md) |
| Qualified | Lead quality | Up; track qualified ÷ leads |
| Quotes sent | Close activity | Up |
| **Quote → paid %** | The close's core conversion | The headline number; 2.5-3% warm is proven (source: concierge-close.md) |
| AOV | Deal size | Hold ~$400 (source: the-20k-math.md) |
| On-time delivery % | The 5-day promise = the brand | 100% target (source: png-voice.md) |
| Reviews earned | Compounding trust (4.8★/19 GBP) | Up weekly |

The review answers three questions: where are we leaking, what's the one fix this week, and are we on pace for the [[the-20k-math|$20K math]]. (co-CEO recommendation) Feeds the [[dashboard]].

## Outward copy (ganas voice)

The scripts Venus uses, in the bilingual ganas growth-partner register (read aloud — a real bilingual person must say it that way) (source: png-voice.md):

**Speed-to-lead first touch (text/WhatsApp):**
> "¡Hola [Name]! It's Venus from Promo & Grow 👋 Got your request about [product] — love it. Quick Q so I can get you a free proof: what's your event date? Te ayudo a que llegues booth-ready en 5 días."

**Inbound call open:**
> "Promo & Grow, this is Venus — ¿en qué te ayudo? Tell me about your event and your brand, and I'll walk you through getting a free proof, no pressure."

**Quote-sent (with the draft order/proof):**
> "Here's your proof, [Name] — nada se imprime hasta que tú lo apruebes. [Product], booth-ready in 5 business days. Take a look and tell me what you think — happy to adjust anything."

**Day-3 follow-up:**
> "Hey [Name], checking in on your proof — ¿qué te pareció? If the budget or the size needs a tweak, dime y lo ajustamos. Want you to show up like a pro."

**Day-7 deadline nudge (no fake urgency — real dates only):**
> "[Name], to keep you on track for [event date] we'd want to start production by [date]. Still on? Just reply sí and I'll lock it in."

**Won / thank-you + review ask:**
> "¡Cerrado! 🎉 Thanks [Name] — we're on it, delivered by [date]. Cuando lo recibas, una reseña de Google nos ayuda un montón. ¡A brillar en tu evento!"

Never salesy, never fake-urgent, never machine-translated (source: png-voice.md). (co-CEO recommendation)
