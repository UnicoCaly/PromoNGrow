## What this is

The SOP library is the written checklist version of Carlos's recorded workflow walkthroughs — the cataloged playbook of how Promo & Grow runs an order, end to end (source: 00-SOP-Index.md). It is owned by Carlos and built from his ~10 recorded Loom walkthroughs (June 2026) plus workflow notes (source: 00-SOP-Index.md).

**The "Jorge pattern" that produced it:** record the workflow → drop the notes → they get organized, polished, indexed, and cataloged into clean SOPs; each SOP also gets an improved re-record outline so cleaner, shorter videos can be refilmed on QuickTime and dropped back in (source: 00-SOP-Index.md). This is the [[concierge-close]] captured as a repeatable, eventually-automatable process, which is the point of the automation/capacity growth lens.

**How to use it:** Venus reads the SOPs in order and keeps the relevant one open as a checklist while she works, watching the matching Loom for the visual; Carlos uses the matching re-record outline when he refilms, then updates the Loom link in the index (source: 00-SOP-Index.md).

## SOP-01 — Quoting & Pricing a Job

**Purpose:** produce a fast, accurate quote a customer can pay — without exposing cost or guessing (source: SOP-01-Quoting-and-Pricing.md). Triggered the moment a warm lead asks "how much?" by phone, email, or text; speed-to-lead is #1, respond within minutes (source: SOP-01-Quoting-and-Pricing.md).

**The flow:** get the 5 inputs (product, quantity, size/specs, art/logo, event/needed-by date) → find the price → check the floor → offer good-better-best where natural → quote it with the warm note (source: SOP-01-Quoting-and-Pricing.md).

**Where the price comes from:** on the [[master-price-sheet]] → use the set price; tent/display by configuration → build the combo in the draft (SOP-02) and price builds itself; print collateral → now a hidden quoting product in the Shopify catalog, search the name and pick the run size; not listed / fully custom → route-through to Carlos for the number, never invent it (source: SOP-01-Quoting-and-Pricing.md).

**Guardrails:** minimum order **$100**; no discounts without Carlos's OK in Week 1; rush adds cost and needs a Carlos-confirmed closing quote; complex branding (wraps, channel letters, brand systems) escalates to Carlos/Proágo (source: SOP-01-Quoting-and-Pricing.md). See [[the-20k-math]] for why the floor and AOV matter.

**Product knowledge so she sounds expert** (source: SOP-01-Quoting-and-Pricing.md):

| Product | Default spec | Turnaround | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Business cards | 16PT, matte, double-sided (4/4) — ~95% of orders | ~4 business days | UV is the glossy option; **Spot UV** is an upsell |
| Postcards | 16PT, gloss; always quote double-sided (≈ same price as single) | — | Sizes 4x6, 5x7, 5.5x8.5 |
| Brochures / flyers | 8.5x11 all-inclusive, aqueous coating, folding included | ~3 days | Pricier than template shops; win on speed + real person + free proof |

## SOP-02 — Create a Draft Order in Shopify

**Purpose:** turn a quote into a Shopify draft order that becomes an invoice the customer pays — the concierge close in action; we build the cart for the customer and send the invoice instead of making them self-serve (source: SOP-02-Create-Draft-Order-Shopify.md). Home base is **Shopify Admin → Orders → Drafts**; drafts flip to Completed when they turn into paid invoices (source: SOP-02-Create-Draft-Order-Shopify.md).

**Steps:** Create order from Drafts → add catalog products (for tents, remove the default and scroll to the exact combo — reinforcement strip color, sewing thread, bag type, wall config: Tent Only / Half Wall / Full Wall / Complete Set) → add print collateral (hidden quoting products: search name, pick run-size variant, price auto-fills) → attach the client (SOP-03) → add the shipping line → save → send the invoice email (source: SOP-02-Create-Draft-Order-Shopify.md). Truly custom items not in the catalog get added as a named custom item (e.g. "16PT Business Card — 4/4 — 1,000") with price-per-unit = total ÷ quantity, after a Carlos route-through (source: SOP-02-Create-Draft-Order-Shopify.md). Week 1: Carlos reviews every draft before it sends (source: SOP-02-Create-Draft-Order-Shopify.md). This is the manual front-end of the [[draft-order-quote-system]].

**Shipping rule:** orders **$500+ ship free (ground)** — most tents, canopies, and large items clear that automatically; smaller items ~**$30 standard ground** ($25–45 by size); faster = a Carlos-confirmed custom/closing quote (source: SOP-02-Create-Draft-Order-Shopify.md).

## SOP-03 — Add / Find a Client in Shopify

**Purpose:** attach the right customer with complete, verified contact info and capture marketing consent correctly (source: SOP-03-Add-Client-Shopify.md). Done while building the draft, ideally while the customer is still on the phone (source: SOP-03-Add-Client-Shopify.md).

**Steps:** search first by name/email/phone (select if they exist, verify what populated, fix gaps live) → if new, add first name, last name, email, phone → capture marketing consent live (ask warmly about text + email updates/offers — this is real opt-in consent and is what lets us text them later under TCPA) → address auto-verifies as you type, add company name, confirm before saving → if the receiver differs from the payer, payer goes in billing and receiver on the shipping address → keep tags light at creation, add lead-stage/VIP/reorder/language tags once she starts working the client (source: SOP-03-Add-Client-Shopify.md). Tag conventions live in [[shopify-tag-taxonomy]]; consent rationale in [[sms-marketing]]. If they decline marketing texts, respect it — do not opt them in; transactional/order updates are still fine (source: SOP-03-Add-Client-Shopify.md).

## SOP-04 — Draft the Invoice Email & Notes

**Purpose:** send the invoice with a warm, persuasive note that gets the customer to open the checkout link and pay (source: SOP-04-Invoice-Email-and-Notes.md). Week 1: Carlos reviews before send (source: SOP-04-Invoice-Email-and-Notes.md).

**Send mechanics:** send from your own promongrow.com address (not generic hello@) via the top-right **From** dropdown so it comes from *you*; **CC hello@promongrow.com** to keep a copy; CC the client's accounting/partner if asked (source: SOP-04-Invoice-Email-and-Notes.md).

**Subject convention:** `Promo Invoice [number] — Promo & Grow (formerly PrintCo Direct Inc.)` — helps legacy clients recognize the order (source: SOP-04-Invoice-Email-and-Notes.md). *(Canonical spelling = **PrintCo Direct Inc.** — the form used across the brand brief, the reactivation strategy, and every piece of Venus's outward copy. The SOP-06 source and the `pcd.ordant.com` / "PCD" abbreviation spell it "Print Code Direct"; that's a transcription variant in that one doc — same legacy entity. Resolved 2026-06-18, see [[dashboard]].)* Ties to history via SOP-06.

**The note hits:** a personal opener referencing their event; what's in the order; **free proof** before anything prints and the sequence (production starts only after the invoice is paid AND the proof is approved — then the 5-day clock begins, 4 on print); a clear nudge to the checkout link; bilingual if they speak Spanish (source: SOP-04-Invoice-Email-and-Notes.md). Carlos uses AI (prefers Claude) to draft fast, then personalizes — paste screenshots of the prior conversation so the AI has full context (source: SOP-04-Invoice-Email-and-Notes.md). Voice rules: [[png-voice]]. No reply → run the Day 1/3/7 follow-up cadence (source: SOP-04-Invoice-Email-and-Notes.md).

## SOP-05 — Proof, Checkout & Shipping

**Purpose:** know exactly what the customer experiences after the invoice is sent, so Venus can guide them to paid and start the production clock (source: SOP-05-Proof-Checkout-Shipping.md).

**What the customer sees:** they click the email link → it opens the Shopify checkout for their order → they verify their phone number → most use Shop Pay (fast, familiar) → checkout shows estimated shipping + sales tax → they pay → the draft flips to a paid invoice → production starts (source: SOP-05-Proof-Checkout-Shipping.md).

**The proof step (the risk-remover):** a free proof goes out before anything prints; nothing goes to production until the customer approves it; approval starts the **5-business-day** delivery clock (4 on print). Close line: "You'll see a free proof with your logo before we print a thing — zero risk" (source: SOP-05-Proof-Checkout-Shipping.md). This is the [[file-prep-gate]] from the customer's side.

**Shipping rules:** $500+ ships free (ground); smaller items ~$30 standard ground ($25–45 by size); faster than standard ground = a custom/closing quote — get the date, confirm the rush figure with Carlos, then add the shipping line (source: SOP-05-Proof-Checkout-Shipping.md). Changes after paying → flag Carlos before production locks (source: SOP-05-Proof-Checkout-Shipping.md).

## SOP-06 — Legacy Order Lookup in Ordant

**Purpose:** pull a returning/legacy client's last price charged and their artwork file so we can quote and run a reprint fast and consistently (source: SOP-06-Legacy-Order-Lookup-Ordant.md). Scope is returning clients from the PrintCo Direct era whose history lives in Ordant (`pcd.ordant.com`), not Shopify; most legacy clients just reprint the same job (source: SOP-06-Legacy-Order-Lookup-Ordant.md, which spells the legacy name "Print Code Direct" — same entity).

**Access caveat:** Ordant is legacy / read-only. Carlos retired the system (it was the PrintCo Direct system); the owner (Ali) kept access alive so we can pull history. You can look up data but do not add users or change anything (source: SOP-06-Legacy-Order-Lookup-Ordant.md).

**Steps:** log into pcd.ordant.com → go to the order/job board → search the client by name (e.g. "Nova Storage") → open previous order history for the two things that matter: **what we charged them last** (the single most valuable number for a reprint quote) and the **order number** (which tells you where the artwork file lives on the NAS server, organized by year + order number) → use the last price as the quote basis (apply current pricing if costs changed, confirm with Carlos if unsure) and build the order in Shopify (SOP-02) (source: SOP-06-Legacy-Order-Lookup-Ordant.md). Going forward, the order lives in Shopify, not Ordant.

## SOP-07 — Worked Example: Canopy + A-Frame Quote

**Purpose:** a real, start-to-finish quote build that puts SOP-02 (build the draft) and SOP-04 (the quote email) together on a large-format job, including how to use ChatGPT/Claude to draft the email the right way (source: SOP-07-Worked-Example-Canopy-and-AFrame-Quote.md). Added June 15, 2026.

> Note on the example client: the request came from an institutional account (City of South Gate), which is Carlos-only / DNC for Venus. Venus does not reach out to institutional accounts (South Gate, Lynwood/LUSD, Nova Storage) — Carlos handles those. Use this as a how-to for the build, not a lead to chase (source: SOP-07-Worked-Example-Canopy-and-AFrame-Quote.md). See [[south-gate-account]].

**The scenario:** an existing customer requested pricing on a 10×10 canopy and 10 A-frame posters (we'd done standard 24×36 A-frame posters for them before); they didn't specify canopy options (source: SOP-07-Worked-Example-Canopy-and-AFrame-Quote.md).

**The build:**
1. Start with the anchor item (the 10×10 canopy) and build it first. Since the config wasn't specified, quote the standard/most basic option (white reinforcement + standard background) and mention other configs/bundles in the email. Example price: **$800** for the standard 10×10 canopy — always confirm against the current [[master-price-sheet]]; walls/print configs change the price (source: SOP-07-Worked-Example-Canopy-and-AFrame-Quote.md).
2. Add the A-frame PVC signboards (catalog item): PVC 8mm, 24×36, qty 10, **$50 each** in the example — and confirm the size with the client rather than assuming (source: SOP-07-Worked-Example-Canopy-and-AFrame-Quote.md).
3. Attach the returning client — search and attach, don't re-create (SOP-03) (source: SOP-07-Worked-Example-Canopy-and-AFrame-Quote.md).
4. Draft a **short, inquisitive** quote email with AI that uncovers needs + timeline. The first AI draft came back too long; the fix is to tell it "keep it brief and concise," then trim. A long quote email buries the question; a short one gets a reply (source: SOP-07-Worked-Example-Canopy-and-AFrame-Quote.md).
5. Send from the rep's own email, CC hello@promongrow.com, per SOP-04 (source: SOP-07-Worked-Example-Canopy-and-AFrame-Quote.md).

**Reusable takeaways:** anchor item first, then add-ons; unspecified config → quote the standard and mention upgrades; confirm sizes/specs; returning client → find, don't duplicate; AI drafts the email but you steer it (feed specs + prices, ask for short + inquisitive, include a site link, then trim); price is the start of a conversation, not the end (source: SOP-07-Worked-Example-Canopy-and-AFrame-Quote.md).

## Re-record outlines

Clean scripts for refilming tighter QuickTime videos, each built from the original Loom and trimmed to essentials (source: Re-Record-Outlines.md).

**Universal rules:** keep each 2–4 minutes, one task per video; say the goal in the first 10 seconds; narrate what you click; end with a one-line recap; on screen hide vendor cost, passwords, and other customers' private data (blur or use a test record); re-record if you ramble (source: Re-Record-Outlines.md).

**Confidentiality split:** Videos 1–3 are the internal pricing engine ("top-down," not for entry-level reps) — keep private or record a separate Venus-facing version that quotes from the Master Price Sheet without showing 4over cost. Videos 4–10 are Venus-facing operational SOPs (source: Re-Record-Outlines.md). The two-version split for Video 1 is explicit: an internal version showing the 16PT/matte/4/4 cost → markup → rounded price build, and a Venus-facing version that just reads "1,000 cards = $108" off the sheet (source: Re-Record-Outlines.md).

**Status:** Videos 4–9 were already re-recorded (June 2026) as one continuous Master Order Walkthrough because it flowed better as a single video; the per-video outlines are kept for reference and future re-records. After refilming, update the Loom/QuickTime links in `00-SOP-Index.md` (source: Re-Record-Outlines.md).
