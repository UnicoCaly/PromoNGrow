## Meta lead-status discipline — and why it matters

Venus updates each lead's **status inside Meta**: **intake → qualified → converted → lost.**
- **intake** = it just came in · **qualified** = a real lead we want to sell · **converted** = they bought · **lost** = didn't win it.
- **Why (the real reason):** Meta's algorithm uses these statuses to **optimize the ad** — the more it learns about who actually converts, the better the future leads it serves. *"The most important thing is to get people into converted."* So this isn't busywork; updating status is how the ad gets smarter.
- These are Meta's funnel labels; they parallel the Sheet/Shopify stages but the names differ — use **Meta's exact labels inside Meta**.
