# Bilingual Spanish Moat

**Summary**: The bilingual Spanish strategy — three wins (trust/conversion, a near-empty Spanish SEO/AEO surface, a defensible moat), built on Shopify Markets, with copy in Carlos's real US-Latino business Spanish, hero pages staged first.

**Type**: concept

**Sources**: PNG-Master-Context-Playbook.md, PNG-Brand-Strategy-and-Voice-Brief.md, PNG-Closer-Onboarding-Workbook.md

**Last updated**: 2026-06-17.

---

## The three wins

1. Trust/conversion for Spanish-speaking buyers.
2. A near-empty Spanish SEO/AEO surface (little competition to rank/be-cited in).
3. A defensible moat national POD shops can't copy — reinforced by the made-in-Lynwood identity (source: PNG-Master-Context-Playbook.md).

This is a top-priority moat layer in the strategy stack (bilingual → SMS → cold email/ABM → paid) (source: PNG-Master-Context-Playbook.md). It directly serves [[positioning-and-personas|Persona 1, "La Emprendedora"]].

## Foundation: Shopify Markets

Built on **Shopify Markets** (native, free, best for SEO) — NOT a JavaScript translate widget. Includes hreflang, `/es/` URLs, a language toggle, and a bilingual quote form. Spec'd in PNG-Bilingual-Build-Spec (a Claude Code build; not yet ingested into raw) (source: PNG-Master-Context-Playbook.md).

## Spanish copy approach

All 8 [[store-and-catalog|money pages]] + quote form + UI strings translated in Carlos's voice — real US-Latino business Spanish, not a machine dump. FAQ blocks preserved = a Spanish AEO surface. Copy lives in PNG-Spanish-Money-Page-Copy (not yet in raw) (source: PNG-Master-Context-Playbook.md).

The translation must follow the [[png-voice|bilingual craft rule]]: natural code-switching the way bilingual LA actually speaks, never machine-translated or gimmicky (source: PNG-Brand-Strategy-and-Voice-Brief.md).

## Hero-pages-first rule

**Stage hero pages live first** — never half-translated. The full site is the destination, but ship the high-value pages completely before broadening (source: PNG-Master-Context-Playbook.md).

## Key terms

- "carpa" / "toldo" — tent.
- "prueba gratis" — free proof.
- "hecho a la medida en Los Ángeles" — made-to-order in Los Angeles (source: PNG-Master-Context-Playbook.md).

The closer's bilingual script models the spoken register: *"Tu evento, tu marca, hecho bien. Mándame tu logo y te hago una prueba gratis — sin compromiso — y te lo entregamos en 5 días."* (source: PNG-Closer-Onboarding-Workbook.md). Note: "toldo" is also the gate word on the bilingual closer job post (see [[closer-hire]]).
