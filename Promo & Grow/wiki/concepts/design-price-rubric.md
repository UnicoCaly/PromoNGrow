## The one rule

**We place YOUR art for free. We CREATE or FIX art for a fee.** Every design question resolves to: *am I placing their art, fixing their art, or creating new art?* Quote the fee up front, add it as a line on the draft order, never deliver custom work then ask for money.

## The fee table (set prices — quote straight from this)

### Tier 1 — Free Proof (the hook) · **$0**
Customer sends print-ready art → we place it on the product + send the mockup. Includes ~2 placement-tweak rounds. **Offered on $500+ big-ticket only** (tents, displays, bundles). Sub-$500 print (cards/flyers/brochures) gets no free proof — that's the 6/22 ruling.

### Tier 2 — File Fix / Light Design · **flat fee**
We make their existing art usable, or arrange supplied elements. We're not inventing the design.

| Service                                 | What it is                                                                                              | Fee     |
| --------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------- |
| Logo vectorize / cleanup — **simple**   | wordmark or text + 1–2 basic shapes, flat color → redraw to print-ready vector                          | $50     |
| Logo vectorize / cleanup — **detailed** | crest, seal, badge, mascot, fine linework, script/ornate lettering, many small parts, gradients/shadows | $80     |
| Light layout                            | drop supplied logo + supplied text into a simple one-sided piece (card, flyer, sign)                    | **$70** |
| Multi-piece light layout                | same, across a 2–3 piece set (front/back, card + flyer)                                                 | **$85** |

### Tier 3 — Custom / Complex Design · **charged, never credited**
We CREATE the design. Always quote before the work.

| Level | What it is | Fee |
|---|---|---|
| Simple custom | one-sided, built mostly from their elements + a little new art / minimal original composition | **$95** |
| Standard custom | a piece designed from scratch — flyer / banner / sign / single tent-side; we choose layout, type, hierarchy, create new graphic elements | **$175** |
| Complex custom | multi-element, step-and-repeat for a tent/backdrop, full display set, or new illustration | **$295+ → confirm with Carlos** |

### Route to Proágo (not catalog design)
Pitch warmly, then escalate to Carlos — Proágo quotes separately (typically $500+):
- Logo **design** / brand identity / brand systems
- Creating a **crest, mascot, seal, or custom illustration from scratch** (vectorizing an *existing* one is Tier 2 detailed)
- Vehicle wraps, channel letters, environmental/signage fabrication design
- Anything that would run past ~$300 / ~3 hrs of design

## How to rate the job (which tier) — the complexity reference

**Step 1 — the one question:** *placing, fixing, or creating?*
- **Placing** ready art → Tier 1 (free on $500+) or just run it as-is on sub-$500 print.
- **Fixing** their art (vectorize, clean up, lay out supplied elements) → **Tier 2.**
- **Creating** new art → **Tier 3** (or Proágo).

**Step 2 — the bump signals.** Each signal you hit pushes the job *up*. Land it at the **highest tier any signal triggers**:

1. **We have to draw something new** (not place/arrange what they gave) → Tier 3 minimum.
2. **More than one side or piece** → up a level (one card face = base; front + back, or a 2–3 piece set = up).
3. **Step-and-repeat or full-coverage pattern** (tent canopy, backdrop, full-bleed table throw) → Complex.
4. **Intricate / fine-detail art** — crest, badge, seal, mascot, script or hand-lettering, illustration → up a level. *Redrawing* an existing one = Tier 2 detailed ($80); *creating* one from scratch = Complex or Proágo.
5. **Color/print tricks** — spot-color matching, complex gradients, die-lines → up a level.
6. **Brand-building** — designing a *logo* or a coordinated brand look → not a tier at all → **Proágo.**

**Tie-breakers:** when two signals fight, **price the higher one.** When you're between two tiers, **quote the higher and tell Carlos.** Never guess a design number — same rule as the price sheet.

## Credit-to-order rule (confirmed 2026-06-24)

- **Sub-$500 print:** the design fee **stays on the invoice** — never waived or credited. This is the exact margin the $500 free-proof ruling protects.
- **$500+ orders:** Venus may fold **one** light file-fix in as goodwill (a Carlos-approved close lever — the margin's there on a big-ticket order).
- **Tier 3 custom:** **never credited**, on any order size.

## Revisions

- **Free:** moving/resizing/recoloring the customer's *supplied* art (~2 rounds). Use the ~2-round mark as the moment to ask *"still placing, or now creating?"* — not as a hard wall; keep it warm.
- **Becomes paid the moment it's creation** (new elements, redraws, "can you design something that…") → quote the Tier 2/3 fee before doing it.
- **Post-approval = locked.** The approved proof is what prints; no changes after the order. Then the 5-day clock starts.

## The warm scripts (ganas voice)

- **Proof-is-free / new-art line:** *"El proof siempre es gratis — that's your logo on your tent, no charge. What you're describing is *new* artwork, so that's a quick design fee — déjame get you a number."*
- **Sub-$500 print:** *"For cards and flyers we skip the free proof, but if your file's print-ready we run it as-is. Si quieres que lo limpiemos o lo armemos bien, that's a small design fee — quick and easy."*
- **Vectorize:** *"Tu logo's a little low-res to print clean — for $50 we redraw it sharp so it looks crispo on everything, y ese archivo bueno is yours to keep."*
- **Custom, quote-first:** *"Love it — that's a custom piece, so let me get you a quick design quote before we start, y de ahí lo hacemos bien."*

## Guardrails (protect the offer)

1. Free proof = **placement only**, $500+ items only — stated plainly.
2. **Quote design before doing it** — never deliver then ask for money.
3. Warm framing, never bait-and-switch: *"The proof's always free — that's your logo on your tent. What you're describing is new artwork, which is design work — let me quote that for you."*
4. **Unsure on tier, or it smells like Proágo → route to Carlos.** Don't guess.
