## The strategy (co-CEO recommendation)

1. **Triage run-1 first.** Anyone who replied / answered / asked for a quote last week is a **live deal** — run the Day-1/3/7 close ([[draft-order-quote-system]]), don't lump them with non-responders.
2. **Run-2 = top-value only, new angle.** Venus's 6-hr/day window can't meaningfully multi-touch 162. Concentrate her calls on the **40 accounts with 10+ past orders**, led by a *different hook* than run-1.
3. **The cross-sell is the move.** These print buyers don't know P&G now does **custom tents, table covers, and trade-show displays in 5 days.** Touch-2 isn't "reorder cards?" — it's "we've grown; got an event or booth this summer?" Start with accounts that already bought **Tents** (Tropicanna, Del Rio Brewing).
4. **Email carries the long tail.** Put all 162 (+ the ~640 legacy contacts) on the [[reactivation-email-sequence|8-email sequence]] so the 12mo+ dormant accounts get warmed automatically while Venus calls only the top-value + responders.
5. **Open fresh supply.** Venus burned the whole list in a week — she's **lead-starved**. Near-term renewable sources while ads are gated on [[conversion-tracking-setup|tracking]]: reorder outreach to *recent/active* customers (live Shopify, not this lapsed list), Apollo net-new prospecting for event-display leads, and GBP/inbound. See [[lead-machine]].
6. ⚠️ **SMS caution:** the import shows `Accepts SMS = no` for most of these contacts — **do not text them** without consent (TCPA). Email + call only until they opt in.

## Cadence

A reactivation is a **sequence, not a call.** Run-1 (call + email) was touch 1. Touch 2 = the cross-sell angle (call the top-value; email the rest). Touch 3 = a value/offer drop ~1 week later. Space over 2–4 weeks; stop after 3–4 touches on non-responders and let the email sequence carry them.
