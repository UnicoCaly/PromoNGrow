## The 3 guardrails (every quote, every product)
1. **Quote ONLY from the [[master-price-sheet]].** Never expose 4over / B2Sign or their cost. *(You CAN lean on the truth that it's "produced locally in LA, fast turnaround" — the plant is in Glendale — just never name the vendor.)*
2. **The promise:** free proof before you pay + **5 business days from proof approval** (4 on flat print). Frame free-proof as risk-removal: *"you don't pay until you've seen and approved it."*
3. **The reflex:** every order is a doorway — ask *"what else could they use?"* and attach the cross-sell.

## 🔫 Stat ammo (drop these in conversation)
- Direct mail gets a **77%** view rate vs **23.7%** email open rate — it can't go to spam.
- **68%** of people made a purchase because a **sign** caught their eye.
- **72%** judge a business by its **business card**; **39%** won't deal with a "cheap-looking" one.
- People judge your brand in **13 milliseconds** — before reading a word.
- Missing compliance signage: **OSHA ~$13,653/violation**, **ADA up to $75,000**.

*(source: 4over "how to sell" guides — EDDM / Large Format / Majestic; sell sheets SS_1072–1094. Use as conversational proof points, not as written guarantees.)*

## Product playbooks

### 📬 EDDM — Every Door Direct Mail (the reactivation hero)
**What:** P&G prints postcards and USPS mails them to EVERY home on the carrier routes the customer picks — no list, no addresses, full-service. **Who:** restaurants, dentists/medical, realtors (farming a neighborhood), home services, salons, auto, tax prep — any walk-in/local business. **Talk track:** *"You don't need a list or a single address — point at the neighborhood on a map and we drop your card in every mailbox on that route."* + the 77%/23.7% stat. **Top objection — "direct mail is junk":** *"Junk is national blast mail. EDDM is your immediate neighborhood, so it reads personal — and 77% of mail gets looked at vs 23% of email."* **Cross-sell trigger:** ANY postcard/flyer/menu/card buyer → *"Ever considered a direct-mail postcard?"* Turns a one-time print order into a **recurring monthly neighborhood blanket.**

### 🪧 Large Format & Signage (P&G's core cross-sell engine)
Reflex: anyone reordering print → *"any worry about people finding your location, or your event getting noticed?"* → attach a sign.
- **Banners:** the 68%-of-buyers stat. Lapsed banner customers likely have a faded one up NOW — *"let's refresh what's on your wall."* Every tent buyer needs a matching banner.
- **Yard signs:** fundraiser reframe for teams/schools/churches — *"player yard signs beat the flyer everyone tosses; they raise money AND advertise all season."* Realtor open-house play.
- **Feather flags:** height + motion for auto/realtor curb appeal — *"people can't buy from a lot they drove past."* Completes every tent/A-frame booth.
- **Window graphics (3 types):** see-through perforated vinyl (you see out, customers see your message — one-way), see-through + laminate, and white/clear clings — *"your windows are the busiest billboard you own and they're blank."* For stores, real estate, restaurant promos. Floor decals *"stop your staff playing tour guide."*
- **Aluminum heavy-duty / directional & wayfinding:** rust-proof .08" aluminum (6"×6"–46"×36", contour/round cut, drill holes/standoff) for parking, traffic, factory/warehouse, realtor, and directional/wayfinding signage — durable, professional, indoor/out. A facilities / property-manager / office door-opener.
- **Compliance signage** (fear-opener for cold offices/medical/restaurants): OSHA/ADA fines → bridge to branded wall posters.
- **Rigid-sign durability ladder (upsell):** Foam Core (cheap) **< Gator Board** (tough, framed-art look, indoor/out) **< Aluminum Sandwich Board** (buy-once, true exterior; brushed-silver reads upscale for realtors). Qualify indoor-vs-outdoor + how long it must last, then place them on the ladder.
- **Banner stands / displays:** the lineup IS the menu — X-Stand (economy first-yes) → Regular → Deluxe (aluminum, double-sided) → Backdrop wall (95"×96" step-and-repeat = *"every guest photographs your logo"*). ⚠️ These overlap P&G's **core event-display line** — don't double-sell; right-size and quote the one SKU.

### 💎 Majestic — premium print (the business-card upsell)
**What:** 4over's luxury line — Akuafoil (exclusive foil), silk & suede soft-touch cards, across 7 matched products. **Who:** jewelers, luxury real estate, high-end restaurants, boutiques/salons, med-spas, attorneys, financial advisors (LA is dense with these). **Talk track:** the 13-ms hook + **mail a physical sample** — *"feel the suede; once it's in your hand you'll get why clients won't throw it away."* **Reactivation gold:** every lapsed business-card customer → *"You reordered standard cards before — this time let me show you the version that makes you look like the high end of your market. Same design, same turnaround."* **Bundle:** match the suite (cards → folders → postcards → sell sheets) for one premium brand look.

### 🗳️ Election Year 2026 (seasonal — call NOW)
It's a **2026 election year** — front-load LA/CA races *before* the Sept–Oct scramble.
- **Who:** candidates (City Council, School Board, Judge, County, Assembly), ballot-measure / PAC / union committees, and politically-active LA SMBs (restaurants, dispensaries, contractors) that host signs or endorse.
- **The "Campaign Launch Kit"** (one conversation, many line items): business cards + yard signs + a banner + push cards/door hangers + bumper stickers/buttons → then the event layer (tent + retractable stand + counter cards) at their first festival/fundraiser.
- **The pattern — start small, ride the reorder:** campaigns ALWAYS under-order signs + field lit. Land a cheap first order, say *"we keep your art on file,"* and bank the reorder wave (primary → runoff → general).
- **EDDM is the biggest upgrade** for any candidate "without a list" (precinct saturation by carrier route).
- **Reusable-hardware angle:** A-frames, retractable stands, fabric displays, tents = buy the hardware once, reprint the graphic each cycle.
- **List-match the warm queue:** lapsed card/NCR/postcard buyers → campaign stationery + push cards + mail; past banner/sign/tent buyers → yard signs/banners/displays/tents; past promo buyers → supporter merch.
