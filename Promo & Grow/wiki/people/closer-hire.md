**Summary**: The closer-first hiring decision and its execution — two job posts (bilingual closer gate word TOLDO; "unicorn" gate word CINCO), the Spanish-voice-clip-first screening, El Salvador pay band, the day-one warm-leads rule, and the onboarding workbook (closer named Venus).

## The closer-first decision

Decision: **closer-first** (flipped from a generalist hire), because (a) warm leads exist to close now (reactivation repliers, the VIP list, inbound), (b) a closer trains on warm leads then graduates to cold, and (c) Carlos reserves his sales chops for high-level Proágo deals. Phased toward adding a design/ops hire later (source: PNG-Master-Context-Playbook.md).

The closer staffs the human bridge of the [[concierge-close]] and works the warm queue fed by [[reactivation-email-sequence]], [[sms-marketing]], and [[conversion-bridge|inbound quotes]].

## The two job posts (run both, let the market decide)

- **Bilingual Closer / Account Manager** — gate word **TOLDO** (source: PNG-Master-Context-Playbook.md). ("Toldo" = tent — see [[bilingual-spanish-moat]].)
- **"Unicorn" design + sales + AI + ops post** — gate word **CINCO**, with a 4-skill test (source: PNG-Master-Context-Playbook.md).

Gate words filter the ~80% who don't read the post (source: PNG-Master-Context-Playbook.md).

## Screening

The **60–90 second Spanish voice clip is the hire** — judge warmth/trust first, before anything else (source: PNG-Master-Context-Playbook.md). Triage: kill non-gate-word applicants → judge the Spanish voice clip FIRST → shortlist 3–5 → paid trial on real warm leads before committing (source: PNG-Master-Context-Playbook.md). Pending (next round): scorecard, structured interview questions (including spotting fake AI-proficiency), paid-trial setup, SOP/Loom capture system, 90-day ramp (source: PNG-Master-Context-Playbook.md).

## Pay

**El Salvador, $15–25/hr** — the sweet spot (US-Central time overlap, native Spanish, a real design/tech talent pool) (source: PNG-Master-Context-Playbook.md).

## Day-one rule

Point the hire at **warm leads** (email repliers/clickers, VIP list, inbound) — NOT cold outreach. Cold comes later, once the funnel and the hire's skills are proven (source: PNG-Master-Context-Playbook.md). The closer game plan ranks the queue: (1) inbound today, (2) recent email engagers (the Apr 23 clickers), (3) VIP/repeat buyers (Josh @ Donut Media, South Gate, Pathita @ Tao Kae Noi), (4) broader reactivation openers, (5) cold — not yet (source: PNG-Closer-Onboarding-Workbook.md). See [[vip-reactivation-list]], [[josh-craig-donut-media]], [[south-gate-account]].
