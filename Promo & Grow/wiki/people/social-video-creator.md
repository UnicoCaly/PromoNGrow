Short-form video is the discovery surface on Instagram, and Spanish-language delivery maps directly onto **La Emprendedora**, the persona defined as the heart of the brand in [[positioning-and-personas]]. Note this gap is **already assigned** — see the contradiction above.

**Risk noted (Carlos direction 2026-08-03):** Duo Sculpt is a fitness brand whose aesthetic runs brighter and more casual than the Promo & Grow premium guardrail. The premium-never-cheap rule in [[png-voice]] is the #1 visual constraint, and whether her eye flexes to it is the real test — not whether she can drive engagement.

## Scope and deliverable

**Cadence:** 2–3 short videos per week, on her own schedule. Her on camera, in Spanish, teaching and demonstrating.

**Phased ownership** (decided 2026-08-03):

| Phase | She does | Carlos does | Trigger to advance |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1 — Produce** | Creates videos, hands off ready to post | Schedules and posts; approves everything | ~4 weeks of consistent, on-brand delivery |
| **2 — Run the feed** | Produces *and* posts/runs Reels | Oversight only | — |

Rationale: the Instagram algorithm penalizes inconsistent cadence, and spare-time availability is by definition variable. Phase 1 keeps posting rhythm off her schedule, preserves the 48-hour approval rule from [[content-engine]], and lets Carlos verify the premium bar before anything runs live. Comp is expected to scale with the phase (per-video in Phase 1, retainer in Phase 2).

## The four content pillars

Locked 2026-08-03. Product demos were added as the fourth pillar and identified by Carlos as the strongest angle. BTS was explicitly excluded for now.

1. **How to Promote & Grow** — educational value; earns the follow.
2. **Product demos & features** — the strongest angle.
3. **How easy it is to order** — send logo → free proof → 5 days; removes first-order friction.
4. **Why us** — 4.8★, 20+ years, 5-day turnaround, free proof, a bilingual human; the trust layer.

Suggested weekly mix: 1 value + 1 demo + 1 trust/ordering, no two of the same kind back-to-back.

**BTS deliberately parked.** The Lynwood production floor is one of the strongest premium-proof assets available (real domestic production is what national online printers cannot show), so it is earmarked for Phase 2 rather than dropped (Carlos direction 2026-08-03).

## Tent-first decision

Carlos scoped her starting content to **tents only** — the funnel is currently tent-heavy, and the intent is that she learns one money product well before the full catalog is opened to her (source: Carlos direction 2026-08-03). Tent facts supplied to her are drawn from [[product-size-guide]] (10×10 ≈ 100 sq ft / 10×15 / 10×20; back wall 10 ft × ~6.5 ft; half walls 10 ft × ~3.25 ft) and [[product-application-guide]] (600D polyester + heavy-duty aluminum frame, outdoor-durable, stake or weight the legs).

**Side effect:** with BTS excluded and content limited to tents, most Phase-1 output is her on camera plus supplied product assets, which requires little to no shop access. The "mix of both" footage pipeline matters mainly from Phase 2 onward.

## Guardrails given to her

Condensed from [[png-voice]]: the five voice attributes; premium-never-cheap as the #1 rule; the bilingual craft rule (natural code-switching, never machine-translated); the anti-brand "never" list; real product footage only, never AI-generated or stock (a hard rule carried over from the agency guide in [[content-engine]]).

**Pricing deliberately withheld.** Her materials contain no prices. All cost questions route to "DM us / link in bio for a free proof," preserving the [[concierge-close]] and keeping [[pricing-engine]] and [[master-price-sheet]] out of a contractor's hands.

Hashtags specified at **8–12 per post**, consistent with the co-CEO recommendation on [[content-engine]] (which resolves that page's 8–12 vs 15–20 contradiction in favor of the lower count).
