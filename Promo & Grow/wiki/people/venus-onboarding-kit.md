## Who this is for

Venus Castro is the first hire on the sales team — "employee #1 of the sales machine" — a remote closer based in El Salvador working 10:00 AM–4:00 PM Pacific (11:00 AM–5:00 PM her time) (source: PNG-Your-First-Day.md, Venus-Welcome-Email.md). The kit is built to be reusable: swap in the next closer's name (source: PNG-Venus-30-60-90-Ramp-and-Certification.md). See [[closer-hire]] for the hiring/screening context and [[operating-rhythm]] for the cadence this plugs into.

The enablement design is grounded in sales-onboarding research: structured, competency-gated ramps retain ~50% more reps, cut ramp time ~34%, and produce ~23% more first-year revenue; highly structured programs cut ramp from ~9.1 to ~5.7 months (source: PNG-Sales-Onboarding-Best-Practices-Brief.md). Six research findings shaped the build — 3-phase ramp, gate on competency not activity, a consistent enablement kit (playbook/scripts/battlecards/personas/templates/role-play/quizzes), LAER objection backbone, microlearning + spaced repetition, and recorded-call libraries for remote ramp (source: PNG-Sales-Onboarding-Best-Practices-Brief.md).

## The vision (why her seat matters)

The business runs on a [[concierge-close]] that converts ~10x better than the website cart; the missing piece was the **bridge** between people finding PNG and that warm human close — and Venus is that bridge (source: PNG-Where-PNG-is-Going-Vision.md). See [[conversion-bridge]].

The road, as told to Venus:
- **Now → $20K/month** — work the warm queue, close, repeat. Speed and follow-through win it. The math: 2,000 qualified visits x ~2.5% x ~$400 = $20K (source: PNG-Where-PNG-is-Going-Vision.md). See [[the-20k-math]].
- **Next → $50K/month (12–18 months)** — a different lever: higher AOV via institutional POs, reorders, bundles, repeat relationships. The accounts she nurtures today become the $50K engine tomorrow (source: PNG-Where-PNG-is-Going-Vision.md).
- **Then → it scales** — bilingual Spanish moat, SMS, cold outreach, paid ads, layered on after the warm engine is proven (source: PNG-Where-PNG-is-Going-Vision.md). See [[bilingual-spanish-moat]], [[sms-marketing]].

Carlos is deliberately stepping off the day-to-day close so Venus can own it (source: PNG-Where-PNG-is-Going-Vision.md).

## The 30/60/90 ramp + certification gates

Learn → Apply → Accelerate, gated on skill not just activity. Weekly 15-min Friday check-in. No quota pressure in month one — reward learning velocity and warm wins (source: PNG-Venus-30-60-90-Ramp-and-Certification.md).

### Phase 1 — LEARN (Days 1–30) · "Foundations"
Know the product, price, process, and voice; bank a few warm wins with the safety on. Work: read the kit, study Carlos's Loom SOPs like recorded top calls, build practice draft orders (1 tent + 1 print item, Carlos reviews), work the warmest supervised queue (open drafts #D77/#D66, then 25 hottest reactivation accounts — every quote reviewed), speed-to-lead from Day 1, tag every touch (source: PNG-Venus-30-60-90-Ramp-and-Certification.md).

**Gate — "Foundations Certified"** (Carlos signs off): quote any catalog + print item correctly with no help; build a clean Shopify draft order → client → invoice email end-to-end; pass the Knowledge Quiz at 85%+; name the floor + escalation triggers; run one live warm call reviewed by Carlos; first reactivation conversation logged (source: PNG-Venus-30-60-90-Ramp-and-Certification.md). See [[draft-order-quote-system]].

### Phase 2 — APPLY (Days 31–60) · "Closer Certified"
Sell independently on the warm queue, close first deals, build follow-through into muscle memory. Quotes go out with spot-checks, not full review. Reactivation calling at volume (162-account list, warmest first), Day 1/3/7 cadence, objection drills 2x/week (LAER + Feel-Felt-Found), re-take the quiz weekly, start tracking AOV (source: PNG-Venus-30-60-90-Ramp-and-Certification.md).

**Gate — "Closer Certified"** (~Day 60–90, the trial→commit milestone): speed-to-lead and follow-through green 3+ weeks; first handful of paid invoices closed; quotes consistently accurate, floor respected; handles top 5 objections in a role-play (Carlos scores); voice on-brand; self-manages off the scoreboard. Passing aligns with the 3-month rate bump to $15.75 and a higher weekly hours cap (source: PNG-Venus-30-60-90-Ramp-and-Certification.md).

### Phase 3 — ACCELERATE (Days 61–90) · "Senior Closer track"
Own the warm queue + inbound (Carlos out of the day-to-day close), push AOV via bundles/reorders/private POs/complete-set tents, reverse-shadow to confirm independence, feed the system (her saved playbook trains the next hire). Unlock candidate: once the close is proven, set up her Cowork account for voice-matched follow-up drafting (source: PNG-Venus-30-60-90-Ramp-and-Certification.md).

### Certification = the rate ladder

| Cert | ~Timing | Unlocks |
|---|---|---|
| Foundations Certified | ~Day 30 | Quotes go out with spot-checks (more autonomy) |
| Closer Certified | ~Day 60–90 | 3-month rate bump ($15.75) + higher hours cap |
| Senior Closer track | ~Day 90+ | Graduated scope + Cowork automation |

(source: PNG-Venus-30-60-90-Ramp-and-Certification.md)

## The scorecard — manage to the numbers, not the clock

Four numbers, reviewed Friday (~10 min). If they're green, Venus runs her own day (source: PNG-Your-Scorecard.md, PNG-Weekly-Scorecard-and-30-Day-Checkpoint.md):

| # | Metric | Target (steady state) | Ramp (Weeks 1–2) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Speed-to-lead (warm lead → first response) | < 15 min in business hours | < 60 min |
| 2 | Daily activity (calls + emails + texts + quotes) | 15–25/day | 8–12/day |
| 3 | Follow-through (% of open quotes worked on Day 1/3/7) | 100% | 100% |
| 4 | Quote → paid (% of quotes sent that get paid) | 30–40% on warm leads | track it |

Speed-to-lead and follow-through are **effort metrics** — fully in her control, green right away. Quote→paid is an **outcome metric** that climbs as she learns the catalog and close. Coach effort first (source: PNG-Your-Scorecard.md, PNG-Weekly-Scorecard-and-30-Day-Checkpoint.md). She keeps a running tally in Shopify tags and drops the numbers in Friday. No KPI app needed (source: PNG-Weekly-Scorecard-and-30-Day-Checkpoint.md). See [[lead-tracker-sheet]], [[conversion-tracking-setup]].

### Rate ladder
$15.00 → $15.75 (3 mo) → $16.54 (6 mo) → $17.36 (9 mo) → $18.23 (12 mo) — 5% every 3 months; honor the $15 start (source: PNG-Your-Scorecard.md, PNG-Weekly-Scorecard-and-30-Day-Checkpoint.md).

### The ramp arc, week by week
- **Week 1 — Learn the rails.** Access works, first practice quotes, first supervised real touches; Carlos reviews every quote. Win = one real warm touch with sign-off.
- **Week 2 — First close + the unlock.** She closes her first warm lead; *after* that win, introduce the voice-capture habit (see Your Playbook below). Raise hours cap if she's flowing.
- **Weeks 3–4 — Rhythm.** Effort metrics at target, quote→paid trending up; Carlos spot-checks instead of reviewing every quote (source: PNG-Weekly-Scorecard-and-30-Day-Checkpoint.md).

### 30-Day checkpoint (trial → commit)
Pass criteria: closed real warm leads (first handful of paid invoices); speed-to-lead + follow-through green; quotes accurate + floor respected; voice on-brand; self-manages. If she passes: raise the weekly hours cap (the clean trial→commit mechanism — no separate contract), keep her on the rate ladder, graduate her toward more of the warm queue and eventually cold, and set up her Cowork account for voice-matched drafting (only after the close is proven). If close but not there: name the one or two metrics, give 2 more weeks, don't move the goalposts (source: PNG-Weekly-Scorecard-and-30-Day-Checkpoint.md).

**Discipline flag:** the bar right now is Venus closing her first warm leads — not the $50K number. Keep $50K as the 12–18 month north star; hit the $20K path first, then restage with a real AOV plan. Raising the goal before she's closed a single lead is moving the goalposts (source: PNG-Weekly-Scorecard-and-30-Day-Checkpoint.md).

## Your first day (run-of-show)

Kick off at 10:00 AM PT on a video call. Flow: meet + vision → set up tools (Gmail, Slack, Shopify) → learn essentials (Master Price Sheet, Brand Voice, Closer Workbook) → watch the Master Order Walkthrough (one video: build order, add client, send invoice, proof & checkout) → two practice quotes (one tent + one print, Carlos reviews) → first real warm touches (open quotes incl. the $3,400 tent — ask for Temo — plus past customers; Carlos reviews quotes this week) (source: PNG-Your-First-Day.md).

Guardrails for Day 1: hold the floor ($100 minimum, no discounts without Carlos); don't contact anyone tagged `DNC:`; bigger branding → pitch and loop in Carlos (Proágo); on the sheet → quote it, not on the sheet → ask (source: PNG-Your-First-Day.md). See [[file-prep-gate]], [[fulfillment-sop]].

Kit reading order: tonight (10 min) read the vision + first-day page; tomorrow together the Workbook, Price Sheet, SOP Library + walkthrough, and the Cheat Sheet (keep open while working); this week the Ramp + Certification path, Knowledge Quiz, Role-Play & Objection drills, and the reactivation playbook + call list (source: PNG-Your-First-Day.md).

## Your Playbook (the Week-2 voice-capture unlock)

**Do NOT hand this to Venus on Day 1** — unlock it in Week 2, right after her first win, so it reads as celebrating her, not auditing her (source: PNG-Your-Playbook-Capture-Page.md). It's a folder she owns (`Venus's Playbook/`) where she saves the gold — winning email replies, objection responses that landed, a 2–3 min Loom after a standout call, and quotes that closed — in ~5 minutes at the end of each day (source: PNG-Your-Playbook-Capture-Page.md).

The payoff: once her voice is captured, it plugs into an AI assistant that drafts follow-ups in her tone and queues her day — so documenting now = her semi-automated sales engine later. Framing rules (non-negotiable): it's HER playbook (an asset she owns, never "data we're collecting"); celebrate, don't audit; keep it light (source: PNG-Your-Playbook-Capture-Page.md). This is the Day-1-of [[zapier-automation-roadmap]] capacity story and ties to the 30-day Cowork unlock.
