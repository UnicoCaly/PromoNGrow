# Hiring & Screening

**Summary**: Promo & Grow's hiring system for its first bilingual hire(s): two parallel Upwork posts (a focused Sales Closer and a long-shot "Unicorn" generalist), each gated by a hidden code word (TOLDO / CINCO) and a Spanish voice clip, followed by a structured interview + weighted scorecard, a short paid warm-lead trial, and a Day-1 onboarding kit with scoped security access.
**Type**: concept.
**Sources**: PNG-Closer-Hiring-JobPost.md, PNG-Unicorn-Hiring-JobPost.md, PNG-Closer-Interview-and-Onboarding.md.
**Last updated**: 2026-06-18.

## Why this exists

PNG's revenue today runs on a [[concierge-close]] — a warm human who answers fast, sends a free proof, and closes by phone/email rather than the cart. The hire fills that surface. The strategic rationale for hiring a closer first (vs. a designer or marketer) lives in [[closer-hire]]; this page is the operational machinery: the posts, the filters, the interview, and onboarding.

Two posts run **at the same time** to let the market decide which talent shows up (source: PNG-Unicorn-Hiring-JobPost.md):

- **The Closer** — a focused bilingual sales/account manager. Safe, fundable, the day-one need.
- **The Unicorn** — a rare generalist who can do design + AI + sales + Shopify ops. A long shot; few true fits expected. If one lands, they can be the single hire; if not, fall back to the closer (source: PNG-Unicorn-Hiring-JobPost.md).

The gate words differ so an applicant's first word tells you which post they answered: **TOLDO = closer, CINCO = unicorn** (source: PNG-Closer-Hiring-JobPost.md; PNG-Unicorn-Hiring-JobPost.md).

## The two posts at a glance

| | The Closer | The Unicorn |
|---|---|---|
| Title | Bilingual Sales Closer & Account Manager — Custom Event Products | Bilingual Creative Operator — Design + AI + Sales + Shopify |
| Type | Part-time (15–20 hrs/wk) → full-time | Part-time (15–20 hrs/wk) → full-time |
| Rate | $15–25/hr DOE | $20–30/hr DOE |
| Location | Latin America (El Salvador / Central America ideal); US Central/Pacific overlap required | Same; must overlap US Pacific hours |
| Gate word | **TOLDO** | **CINCO** |
| Core skill tested | Spanish phone warmth (voice clip) | Same voice clip + design portfolio + real AI use |
| AI | Bonus, not a barrier | Core requirement, must prove it |

(source: PNG-Closer-Hiring-JobPost.md; PNG-Unicorn-Hiring-JobPost.md)

The unicorn rate is set a notch higher because a true generalist won't bite at closer pay; design alone is only "nice-to-have," so the premium pays for the *combination*, not design polish (source: PNG-Unicorn-Hiring-JobPost.md).

## What both roles actually do

Both posts frame the work as **warm leads, not cold calls** — past customers to reconnect with, on-site quote requesters, and repeat clients. This is stated explicitly to attract relationship-closers (not boiler-room types) and to keep the hire from failing on the hardest, unproven part (source: PNG-Closer-Hiring-JobPost.md). The warm queue ties to [[vip-reactivation-list]], [[reactivation-email-sequence]], and [[lead-tracker-sheet]].

Day-to-day (closer): respond fast to incoming quotes (speed wins), call/email warm leads in both languages, walk customers through logo → free proof → 5-day delivery, build draft orders in Shopify and send invoices, follow up on unpaid quotes, and keep clean notes (source: PNG-Closer-Hiring-JobPost.md). The unicorn adds design/content, daily AI execution, and Loom-documenting workflows (source: PNG-Unicorn-Hiring-JobPost.md). The draft-order workflow is [[draft-order-quote-system]]; the proof step is [[file-prep-gate]]; the delivery promise is [[fulfillment-sop]].

## The screening test (the filter)

**Closer post** — applicants must (source: PNG-Closer-Hiring-JobPost.md):
1. Begin the application with **"TOLDO"** in all caps (anything without it is ignored — proves they read to the end).
2. In 3–4 English sentences, describe turning a hesitant customer into a sale.
3. Record a **60–90 sec voice clip / Loom in Spanish**, role-playing a taquería owner unsure about ordering a custom tent online. This is the most important part.
4. State time zone and weekly availability overlapping LA 9am–5pm Pacific.

**Unicorn post** — applicants must (source: PNG-Unicorn-Hiring-JobPost.md):
1. Begin with **"CINCO"** in all caps.
2. **Design:** link a portfolio or attach 2–3 samples; bonus — make one clean branded social graphic for "Promo & Grow."
3. **AI:** give one specific prompt/workflow used in the last week and what they did with the output (vague "I use ChatGPT" answers expose it as fake).
4. **Sales voice:** the same 60–90 sec Spanish voice clip.
5. **Ops:** time zone, LA-overlapping availability, and one sentence on how they stay organized.

The design of the unicorn test is that it demands all four skills in one application — a pretender nails design + AI but freezes on the Spanish clip, a pure salesperson skips the design — so the test does the filtering because almost nobody fakes all four (source: PNG-Unicorn-Hiring-JobPost.md). The Spanish voice clip is the make-or-break for *both* roles: "The voice clip is the whole hire" — judge whether the customer would trust it, warm and natural vs. stiff or scripted (source: PNG-Closer-Hiring-JobPost.md). This is the [[bilingual-spanish-moat]] enforced at the hiring gate.

## How to run the posts

1. Post both, wait 48–72 hrs (source: PNG-Closer-Hiring-JobPost.md).
2. Delete every application missing the gate word — ruthlessly; it's the point (source: PNG-Closer-Hiring-JobPost.md).
3. **Listen to the Spanish voice clip FIRST**, before reading anything else, and shortlist on voice/warmth. No warmth = pass regardless of design (source: PNG-Closer-Hiring-JobPost.md; PNG-Unicorn-Hiring-JobPost.md).
4. For the unicorn, then check design (clean, trustworthy, small-brand vibe) and the AI answer (specific or vague); shortlist only those strong on clip + at least 2 of the other 3. A true 4/4 unicorn moves fast — they get poached (source: PNG-Unicorn-Hiring-JobPost.md).
5. Top 3–5 → short paid trial on real warm leads (source: PNG-Closer-Hiring-JobPost.md).

**Market-research read on both posts (source: PNG-Unicorn-Hiring-JobPost.md):**
- Strong unicorn lands → make them the single hire (design + sales + ops).
- Only strong closers land → hire the closer now, add a designer later ("Phased C").
- Only strong designers land (great design, weak phone) → that's a future design/ops hire, NOT the closer. Don't force a designer into the sales seat.

## The structured interview (30–40 min)

Format is a 30–40 min phone/Zoom call; the named candidate is **Venus C.** (source: PNG-Closer-Interview-and-Onboarding.md). The one question the whole interview answers: *would my customer — a taquería owner, a stressed brand manager — trust this voice and feel taken care of?* (source: PNG-Closer-Interview-and-Onboarding.md). Re-listen to the voice clip beforehand.

Core questions probe (source: PNG-Closer-Interview-and-Onboarding.md):
- **Relationship selling** — a real story of turning a hesitant customer happy; handling "that's more than I wanted to spend" without discount-dumping; following up on a quote that went quiet without being annoying.
- **Speed + reliability** — the system that keeps leads from slipping.
- **The live Spanish test** — switch to Spanish and have her role-play the unsure taquería owner, unscripted. The key test.
- **AI** — one specific example from last week (vague = padded résumé).
- **The work** — how she gets up to speed on a new product fast.

Close by confirming hours, LA overlap (9–5 PT), start date, the $15/hr rate, and the paid-trial structure, framed positively (source: PNG-Closer-Interview-and-Onboarding.md).

**Red flags:** talks at/over you or over-scripted; vague on AI; can't give a specific customer story; stiff in the live Spanish prompt (the one non-negotiable miss) (source: PNG-Closer-Interview-and-Onboarding.md).

## The scorecard

Filled out right after the call, rated 1–5, with **phone warmth weighted double** (source: PNG-Closer-Interview-and-Onboarding.md):

| Trait | Weight |
|---|---|
| Phone warmth / would my customer trust her? | ×2 |
| Relationship-selling (empathy, no pressure) | ×1 |
| Speed / organization / follow-through | ×1 |
| Live Spanish warmth | ×1 |
| AI-proficiency (real, not vague) | ×1 |
| Learns fast / self-starter | ×1 |
| Reliability signals (prepared, on time) | ×1 |

**Decision rule:** strong on phone warmth + Spanish + relationship-selling → move to paid trial. A miss on phone warmth → pass, no matter the résumé (source: PNG-Closer-Interview-and-Onboarding.md).

## The paid trial

A short paid trial (1–2 weeks, ~10–15 hrs) de-risks the "new on Upwork" flag. Give 3–5 real **warm** leads — email repliers/clickers and a couple of VIP/reactivation contacts, never cold outreach. Watch response speed, call/text tone, note-logging, and whether she follows up unprompted. Success = she handles warm leads with warmth + organization and ideally moves one toward a quote/sale. If strong → onboard fully; if not → cheap lesson (source: PNG-Closer-Interview-and-Onboarding.md). The warm queue here is the same one in [[lead-machine]] and [[vip-reactivation-list]].

## Onboarding package (productive Day 1)

**Access & identity** (source: PNG-Closer-Interview-and-Onboarding.md):
- Crescendo VOIP extension — her own line for inbound + outbound calls.
- Company email (e.g. firstname@promongrow.com) for quotes and customer comms.
- Shopify **staff account, scoped** — orders, draft orders, products, customers only; NOT settings/payments/theme.
- Any quote-app access, plus shared [[lead-tracker-sheet]] access (warm queue + her pipeline notes).

**Reference docs (the "do her own estimates" kit)** (source: PNG-Closer-Interview-and-Onboarding.md):
- Price list / estimate sheet — base + bundle + "starting at" so she quotes without asking each time (the single most important doc for autonomy). Maps to [[master-price-sheet]] and [[pricing-engine]].
- Product cheat-sheet — the catalog in plain language ([[store-and-catalog]]), sizes, use cases, the 5-day promise, the free-proof process.
- How-to-build-a-draft-order SOP (ideally a Loom) — see [[draft-order-quote-system]].
- Lead-handling SOP — log a lead, response-time expectation, follow-up cadence (Day 1 / 3 / 7 on unpaid quotes), escalation rules. See [[pipeline-ops-spine]] and [[operating-rhythm]].
- Brand voice one-pager — warm, bilingual, never pushy, "free proof" framing ([[png-voice]]).
- FAQ / objection-handling sheet — price, turnaround, proof, shipping, rush orders.

**The "record and loop" habit:** from Day 1 she Looms her workflows as she learns them, building the SOP library *as she works* so the next hire onboards faster and more can be automated later (source: PNG-Closer-Interview-and-Onboarding.md). This feeds the automation-and-capacity lane and [[zapier-automation-roadmap]].

**First-week ramp** (source: PNG-Closer-Interview-and-Onboarding.md):
- Day 1–2: access setup, read docs, shadow a couple of calls, learn product + draft-order flow.
- Day 3–5: supervised warm-lead trial; quotes reviewed before sending.
- Week 2: more autonomy; she builds estimates, you spot-check.
- Day 30: review against scorecard traits; decide on expanded hours/scope.

## Security (credentials)

Use scoped Shopify staff permissions, never the master login — give the minimum the role needs. Share passwords through a proper manager (1Password / Bitwarden shared vault), not plain text or email. For a brand-new contractor, start at trial-level access and expand once trust is established (source: PNG-Closer-Interview-and-Onboarding.md).

## Outward copy (ganas voice)

The outward-facing artifact this hire produces is the Spanish voice clip / live phone close. The benchmark, paraphrased from the test prompt: a taquería owner calls about a custom toldo but is unsure about ordering online — talk to them the way a warm, trustworthy neighbor-who-happens-to-be-a-pro actually would (source: PNG-Closer-Hiring-JobPost.md). A model of the warmth being screened for:

> "Híjole, claro que sí — yo te ayudo con eso. No te preocupes por lo del internet; aquí no compras a ciegas. Tú me mandas tu logo, yo te hago un *free proof* — lo ves, lo apruebas, y de ahí salen 5 días para tenerlo en tu mano. Con ganas le entramos a que tu taquería se vea bien *hecha bien*. ¿Para cuándo lo necesitas?"

This sample is illustrative of the target tone, not a transcript of any candidate (co-CEO recommendation). The voice rules it must obey live in [[png-voice]] and [[bilingual-spanish-moat]].
