# Sales Onboarding & Enablement — Best Practices Brief
### What high-performing teams do to ramp reps fast, and how Promo & Grow applies it.

**For:** Carlos · **Built:** June 6, 2026 · from current sales-enablement research (sources at the end).
**Why this doc:** grounds the enablement materials we're building for Venus (and the future team) in what's proven to work.

---

## The 6 findings that shaped our build

**1. Structure the ramp in three phases — Learn → Apply → Accelerate.**
The 30/60/90 model is the standard: Days 1–30 = product/process/tools + shadowing at ~25% productivity and **zero quota pressure**; Days 31–60 = supervised selling and pipeline building; Days 61–90 = independent selling. The payoff is real: structured onboarding **retains ~50% more new hires, cuts ramp time ~34%, and produces ~23% more first-year revenue**; highly structured programs cut ramp from ~9.1 to ~5.7 months. → *We built Venus a 30/60/90 with this shape.*

**2. Gate on competency, not just activity.**
The best programs use **milestone "certifications"** — the rep proves a skill before advancing, instead of just hitting call counts. → *We added a Closer Certification with pass/fail gates per phase, tied to your rate ladder.*

**3. The core enablement kit is consistent everywhere:** playbook, scripts, battlecards, buyer personas, email templates, **role-play**, and **quizzes/certifications.** Battlecards + role-play "accelerate ramp time and keep messaging consistent" and make a natural foundation for certifications and quarterly refreshers. → *You already have the playbook/scripts/price sheet; we're adding role-play, drills, and quizzes.*

**4. Objection handling has a backbone: LAER.** Listen → Acknowledge → Explore → Respond is the most-recommended mental model because it stops reps from answering an objection they haven't fully heard. Pair it with Feel-Felt-Found for price pushback. The stat that matters: **60% of sales need 5+ "no"s, but 44% of reps quit after the first** — persistence is a trainable edge. → *Our objection drills are built on LAER + Feel-Felt-Found.*

**5. Make learning stick with microlearning + spaced repetition + retrieval practice.** 8–12 minute single-objective chunks, with **quizzes/flashcards at the end and repeated at spaced intervals**, move knowledge into long-term memory and beat one-time training. → *The knowledge quiz and objection flashcards are designed as retrieval practice, meant to be repeated weekly.*

**6. For a remote rep, recorded calls are the unlock.** Live shadowing doesn't scale; top teams give new hires a **library of recorded top calls** to study (listen → take notes → debrief), then have the manager listen to the rep's live calls and give feedback, and finally "reverse-shadow" to confirm readiness. AI/role-play practice can cut ramp ~50%. → *Venus is remote in El Salvador, so we lean on your Looms + a "study these calls" habit + role-play, not in-person shadowing.*

## Design principles we followed
- **Win early, win warm** — point her at proven warm buyers first (reactivation + drafts), no cold, no quota in month one.
- **Short and repeatable** — bite-size, quiz-reinforced, designed to revisit, not one-and-done.
- **Manage to the scoreboard** — competency gates + the weekly scorecard, not hovering.
- **Reusable for the team** — everything is templated so the next hire starts from the same system.
