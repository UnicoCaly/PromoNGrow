# Promo & Grow — SMS Marketing Playbook

**Why SMS for PNG:** Your email data proves your audience responds to *conversation*, not hard sells
(the Apr 23 "reply to me" email got 13% clicks vs. 0% on the Jan 30 discount blast). Texting IS
conversation — and your market (LA-metro, Spanish-speaking small-business owners on their phones all
day) lives on SMS. ~98% of texts get read, usually within minutes. For a business where a conversation
triggers the call that closes, SMS is a structural fit.

**Core principle (same as email):** SMS does NOT push checkout. It starts the conversation that the
concierge close finishes. Drive to "reply" or "call," not "buy now."

---

## ⚠️ COMPLIANCE FIRST (this has legal teeth — TCPA)
Texting without consent = real fines. Do this right from day one:
- **Explicit opt-in required.** You can only text people who agreed to receive marketing texts.
  An email list or a phone number collected for order updates is NOT marketing-SMS consent.
- **Build consent going forward:**
  - Add an SMS opt-in checkbox at checkout (Shopify supports this) — unchecked by default.
  - Add "Text us for a fast quote: (number)" on the site — inbound texts are consented by nature.
  - On quote forms, add an optional "OK to text me about my order? [y/n]".
- **Every marketing text must include:** sender ID ("Promo & Grow") + opt-out ("Reply STOP to opt out").
- **Frequency:** low. SMS is intimate. One bad blast = instant STOP. High-value, low-volume only.
- **Quiet hours:** no texts before 9am / after 8pm local. (Also just respectful.)

---

## SETUP (lean, fits your all-Shopify stack)
- Shopify's native SMS is limited. Most stores use an app: **Postscript, SMSBump (Yotpo), or Recart.**
  Pick one that: integrates with Shopify checkout opt-in, supports 2-way replies (so texts become
  conversations the closer can answer), and handles compliance/opt-out automatically.
- **2-way is the key feature for PNG** — you want replies to land somewhere the closer/Carlos sees and
  responds, turning a text into a call into an order.
- Start with a US 10DLC number (required for business SMS; the app walks you through registration).

---

## WHERE SMS FITS THE FUNNEL
1. **Order/proof updates (transactional, highest trust):** "Your proof is ready — reply to approve."
   These are consented by nature and build the habit of texting with you.
2. **Warm re-engagement (marketing):** to opted-in past customers — "Event season's here, want a quick
   refresh quote?" Conversational, not salesy.
3. **Quote follow-up:** the unpaid-quote nudge works great as a text — fast, personal, gets a reply.
4. **Inbound "text us":** let prospects text for a quote. Instant concierge entry point.

## TEMPLATES (Carlos voice — bilingual. Drive the reply/call, not checkout.)

**1. Proof ready (transactional)**
EN: "Hi [Name], it's Promo & Grow — your free proof is ready. Reply YES to approve & we start your 5-day production. Questions? Just reply or call (844) 883-3308."
ES: "Hola [Name], somos Promo & Grow — tu prueba gratis está lista. Responde SÍ para aprobarla y empezamos tu producción de 5 días. ¿Dudas? Responde o llama al (844) 883-3308."

**2. Warm re-engagement (marketing — opted-in only)**
EN: "Hey [Name], Carlos at Promo & Grow. Event season's starting — want me to send a quick proof for your booth setup? Reply and I'll get on it. Reply STOP to opt out."
ES: "Hola [Name], soy Carlos de Promo & Grow. Empieza la temporada de eventos — ¿te mando una prueba rápida para tu stand? Responde y me pongo en eso. Responde STOP para cancelar."

**3. Quote follow-up (unpaid quote)**
EN: "Hi [Name], following up on your custom tent quote — any questions before we lock production for your [date] event? Happy to adjust anything. — Carlos, Promo & Grow"
ES: "Hola [Name], le doy seguimiento a su cotización de carpa — ¿alguna duda antes de empezar la producción para su evento del [date]? Con gusto ajusto lo que necesite. — Carlos, Promo & Grow"

**4. Inbound auto-reply (someone texts your number)**
EN: "Thanks for reaching out to Promo & Grow! Send us your logo + what you need and we'll text back a free proof. Or call (844) 883-3308. Hablamos español."
ES included in same message (bilingual auto-reply builds instant trust).

---

## HOW SMS + THE CLOSER WORK TOGETHER
- Every inbound text / reply = a closer lead, same as email replies and clicks.
- 2-way SMS app routes replies to a shared inbox the closer monitors → fast response (speed-to-lead).
- The closer works EMAIL repliers + SMS repliers + inbound calls as one warm queue.
- This is the human bridge until the website self-serves.

---

## SEQUENCING (don't rush it)
1. Set up the app + compliance + checkout opt-in NOW (start building a consented list).
2. Use SMS for transactional/proof updates first (warms the channel, all consented).
3. Once you have a consented marketing list (~weeks), layer warm re-engagement texts.
4. Keep volume low, value high, always conversational.
