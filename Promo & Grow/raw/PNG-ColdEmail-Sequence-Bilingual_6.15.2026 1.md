# PNG Cold Email Sequence — Bilingual (EN / ES)
### Net-new LA-Metro Spanish SMBs · 3 touches over 7 days · June 15, 2026

**Who this is for:** *cold* prospects who don't know us yet (restaurants, food vendors, churches, party/quinceañera vendors, auto shops, salons, real-estate teams in LA metro). This is **different from the reactivation list** — those are warm past customers worked by *phone first*. Cold email is net-new only.

**The job of these emails:** not to close in the inbox. **Start a conversation Venus finishes.** Every email earns a reply, then a human takes over.

**The wedge, every time:** *free proof before you pay* + *5-day delivery* + *we speak Spanish*. No competitor combines all three. Lead with it.

---

## ⚙️ Send settings (so we don't get blacklisted)
- **Send from `promotengrow.com` only** — never the main domain.
- **Plain text, no images, no fancy HTML.** Touch 1 = **zero links** (links in a first cold touch tank deliverability). Touches 2–3 = **one link max.**
- Every email includes a **one-line unsubscribe** + a real physical address (CAN-SPAM).
- **Personalize the first line** of every email — generic = spam.
- Keep each email **under ~90 words.** Short reads as personal; long reads as a blast.
- Warm the domain first (see the game plan); real sends start ~week 3, ramp to 50–75/day.

**Personalization tokens:** `{{first_name}}` · `{{business}}` · `{{city}}` · `{{product_angle}}` (pick from the snippet library below by business type).
