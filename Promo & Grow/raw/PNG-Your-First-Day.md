## How we work together
- **Speed wins.** When a warm lead comes in, reach out within minutes. First to respond usually gets the sale.
- **Warm, never pushy.** We equip people, we don't pressure them. The free proof does the selling — it lets them see it with their logo, no commitment.
- **You're measured by a scoreboard, not a clock.** Hit the activity and the speed and you run your own day. (More on that in *Your Scorecard*.)
- **Ask me anything, anytime.** Cell, WhatsApp, Slack — I've got you.

## A few things to know (the guardrails)
- **Hold the floor:** $100 minimum order, and no discounts without checking with me (this week, I review all quotes before they go out).
- **Don't contact anyone tagged `DNC:`** in Shopify — those are institutional accounts I handle personally.
- **Bigger branding** (vehicle wraps, full brand systems) — pitch it, then loop me in; that's our Proágo side.
- **On the sheet → quote it. Not on the sheet → ask me.** Never guess a price.
