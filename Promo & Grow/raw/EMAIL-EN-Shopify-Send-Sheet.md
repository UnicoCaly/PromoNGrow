# 📧 Send Sheet — English Email · Shopify Email (block-by-block)
### "Want your patio packed on game day?" · Build in ~10 min, send tomorrow AM

> **How to use this:** In Shopify admin go to **Marketing → Create campaign → Shopify Email**.
> Add the blocks below **in order**, paste each chunk, set the noted styling. `**bold**` = make that part bold.
> Shopify auto-adds the unsubscribe + mailing-address footer (required) — you don't build those.

## ② BLOCKS (add in this order)

### Block 1 — Logo / header
Use the **store-logo header** the template gives you (or an **Image** block with your logo on a navy `#1B2838` background). Centered.

### Block 2 — IMAGE (the header photo) ⭐ this is the one that was "not loading"
Add an **Image** block → **Upload** → select your **Match Day Fan Zone** photo. Full width. (Optional: link the image to `https://promongrow.com/collections/bundles`.) Shopify hosts it automatically — no file path, no broken image.

### Block 3 — TEXT (greeting + intro)
```
Hi [First name],

Venus here from Promo & Grow. It's the summer of fútbol, the patios are filling up — and I thought of your spot.

Here's the honest part. No countdown clock, just the calendar. **Order by June 26** and you're booth-ready in time for the **4th of July weekend and the big matches** — back-to-back, the busiest patio nights of the summer. We print most jobs in **5 business days** and ship in 2–3 more, so June 26 leaves room to spare. Wait till the week of, and the math stops working.
```
*"Hi [First name]," = use the Personalize button. Make the **bold** parts bold.*
