# Promo & Grow — Weekly Scorecard + 30-Day Checkpoint (Venus)
### Manage to the scoreboard, not the clock. ~10 minutes a week.

**Owner:** Carlos · **For:** Venus · **Cadence:** review together every Friday (or Monday for the prior week).
**The philosophy:** these 4 numbers are the "thumb." If they're green, Venus runs her own day. We don't watch hours.

---

## The 4 numbers that matter

| # | Metric | What it means | Target (steady state) | Ramp (Weeks 1–2) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Speed-to-lead** | Avg time from a warm lead landing → first response | **< 15 min** in business hours | < 60 min while learning |
| 2 | **Daily activity** | Calls + emails + texts + quotes sent per day | **15–25 touches/day** | 8–12/day |
| 3 | **Follow-through** | % of unpaid quotes worked on the Day 1 / 3 / 7 cadence | **100%** (nothing slips) | 100% — habit from day one |
| 4 | **Quote → paid** | % of quotes sent that become paid invoices | **30–40%** on warm leads | track it, don't judge it yet |

> Speed-to-lead and follow-through are **effort metrics** — fully in her control, expected at target immediately. Quote→paid is an **outcome metric** — it climbs as she learns the catalog and the close. Coach effort first; the outcome follows.

**How to fill it (5 min for Venus):** she keeps a running tally in Shopify tags / a simple note; Friday she drops the numbers in. **Review (5 min for Carlos):** scan colors, celebrate the win, pick the ONE fix. That's the whole system. No KPI app needed — the scorecard is enough now.

---

## The ramp arc (what "good" looks like, week by week)

- **Week 1 — Learn the rails.** Access works, first practice quotes, first supervised real touches. Carlos reviews every quote before send. Win = she sends one real warm touch with sign-off.
- **Week 2 — First close + the unlock.** She closes her first warm lead. *After that win*, introduce the voice-capture habit (`PNG-Your-Playbook-Capture-Page.md`). Raise her hours cap if she's flowing.
- **Weeks 3–4 — Rhythm.** Speed-to-lead and follow-through at target. Quote→paid trending up. She's working tiers 1–3 independently; Carlos spot-checks instead of reviewing every quote.

## 30-Day Checkpoint (the trial → commit decision)

Run this at Day 30. It pairs with the **rate ladder** (built-in retention + performance incentive):

| Milestone | Rate |
|---|---|
| Start | **$15.00/hr** |
| 3 months | $15.75 |
| 6 months | $16.54 |
| 9 months | $17.36 |
| 12 months | $18.23 |

*(5% every 3 months. Honor the $15 start — it's what the job post and her proposal set.)*

### Pass criteria (did the bridge prove out?)
- ✅ **Closed real warm leads** — at least her first handful of paid invoices.
- ✅ **Speed-to-lead + follow-through green** — the effort metrics are habit.
- ✅ **Quotes are accurate** — pricing from the sheet, floor respected, clean draft orders.
- ✅ **Voice is on-brand** — warm, bilingual, never pushy; customers respond.
- ✅ **Self-manages** — runs her day off the scoreboard without hovering.

### If she passes
- **Raise the weekly hours cap** (the clean trial→commit mechanism — no separate contract needed).
- Keep her on the rate ladder.
- Begin graduating her: more of the warm queue, then eventually toward cold once the funnel's proven.
- **Set up her Cowork account** for voice-matched follow-up drafting and scheduled tasks (the automation unlock — only *after* she's proven the close).

### If she's close but not there
- Name the one or two metrics that need work, give it another 2 weeks, re-check. Don't move the goalposts; coach to the scoreboard.

---

## The discipline flag (read before raising the target)
The bar that matters **right now** is Venus closing her first warm leads and the bridge proving out — **not** the $50K number. Keep $50K on the wall as the 12–18 month north star; keep the operating focus on the next 30 days. Hit the $20K path first, then restage to $50K with a real AOV plan (POs + reorders). Raising the goal before she's closed a single lead is moving the goalposts.
