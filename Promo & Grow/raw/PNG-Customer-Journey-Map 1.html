The core problem

You don't have a conversion problem. You have a missing bridge.

The concierge close (call → proof → invoice) converts beautifully. But cold organic traffic has no on-ramp into it — the only door is "Add to Cart," which is the wrong door for custom. Volume and the close never meet in the same month.

The hidden engine

Proágo + Promo & Grow are one ecosystem

Same tagline ("Helping Brands Promote & Grow"), same LA market, same concierge motion — but they don't feed each other. The agency builds the brand; the store ships the physical presence. A client who gets a logo from Proágo needs tents, throws, and displays from PNG — and vice versa. Cross-routing these two is a near-zero-cost growth channel sitting in plain sight.
