# Your Scorecard
### How we track your wins — and what earns your certifications and raises.

Hi Venus — here's exactly how you're measured. It's simple and it's all in your control. We manage to these numbers, not to the clock — hit them and you run your own day. Every Friday we'll do a quick 15-minute check-in: look at the numbers, celebrate the win, and pick one thing to sharpen.

---

## The 4 numbers that matter

| # | Metric | What it means | Target | While you ramp (Weeks 1–2) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Speed-to-lead** | Time from a warm lead landing → your first response | **< 15 min** in business hours | < 60 min while learning |
| 2 | **Daily activity** | Calls + emails + texts + quotes per day | **15–25/day** | 8–12/day |
| 3 | **Follow-through** | % of open quotes you worked on the Day 1 / 3 / 7 cadence | **100%** (nothing slips) | 100% — habit from day one |
| 4 | **Quote → paid** | % of quotes you send that get paid | **30–40%** on warm leads | track it — it climbs as you learn |

Speed-to-lead and follow-through are fully in your control — those should be green right away. Quote→paid grows naturally as you learn the catalog and the close. Effort first, results follow.

## Your growth path (certifications + raises)
You earn your way up by proving skills, not just putting in time. Each milestone unlocks more autonomy — and your rate climbs on a built-in ladder.

| Milestone | About when | What it means for you |
|---|---|---|
| **Foundations Certified** | ~Day 30 | You can quote, build orders, and pass the Knowledge Quiz (85%+). Your quotes go out with quick spot-checks instead of full review. |
| **Closer Certified** | ~Day 60–90 | You're closing warm leads, metrics green, voice on-brand. **Rate moves to $15.75/hr** and your weekly hours open up. |
| **Senior Closer track** | ~Day 90+ | You own the warm queue and start pushing bigger orders. Continued rate ladder + you help train the next hire. |

**Rate ladder:** $15.00 → $15.75 (3 mo) → $16.54 (6 mo) → $17.36 (9 mo) → $18.23 (12 mo). Built-in raises every 3 months as you grow with us.

## What "great" looks like in your first 30 days
- Closed your first warm leads (reactivation and/or inbound).
- Speed-to-lead and follow-through green for 3+ weeks.
- Quotes accurate, floor respected, clean draft orders.
- Handle the top objections smoothly (we'll drill these).
- Running your own day off the scoreboard.

Hit these and you're Closer Certified — that's the goal of month one. You've got this. 🎯
