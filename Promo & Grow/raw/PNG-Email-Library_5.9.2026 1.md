## SETUP INSTRUCTIONS — SHOPIFY EMAIL

### Campaign emails (1-8, 14-15):
1. Shopify admin → Marketing → Campaigns → Create campaign → Shopify Email
2. Choose "Brand" template (cleanest)
3. Paste subject line and body
4. Set the "To" field: filter by tag "Reactivation-Apr2026"
5. For reactivation emails 2-3: exclude customers who have purchased since April 15
6. Schedule or send immediately
7. Review open rate and click rate after 48 hours

### Automated flows (9-13):
1. Shopify admin → Marketing → Automations
2. For abandoned cart: use Shopify's built-in "Abandoned checkout" trigger
3. For post-purchase: use "Order fulfilled" trigger with a delay
4. For win-back: use "Customer win-back" template or create custom with date filter
5. Paste subject line and body into each step
6. Set delays per the specs above
7. Activate — they run automatically from here
