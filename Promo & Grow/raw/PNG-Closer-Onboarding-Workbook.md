## §3 — SALES SCRIPTS (frameworks, not robot lines)

Your cadence is already strong — these are scaffolds, say them in your own warm voice. Bilingual:
match the customer's language. **Never pushy — we equip, we don't pressure.** Always anchor on the
**free proof** (removes risk) and **5-day delivery** (we respect their moment).

### 3.1 Inbound call — someone interested
> "Thanks for calling Promo & Grow, this is Venus! Tell me about your event — what've you got coming
> up?" → *listen, get the moment & the date* → "Perfect, we can absolutely help with that. Here's how
> easy it is: send me your logo, I'll get you a **free proof** — no charge, no commitment — and once
> you love it, we deliver in **5 business days**. Want me to start that proof for you?"

### 3.2 Discovery (the 3 things to learn every time)
1. **The moment:** "What's the event / when is it?" (date = urgency + production planning)
2. **The need:** "What are you picturing — just the tent, or the full setup?"
3. **The stakes:** "Is this your first time setting up, or leveling up from last time?"
*(This tells you which persona she is and how to help — print-for-the-ready vs. support-the-hustler.)*

### 3.3 The quote handoff
> "Based on what you told me, here's what I'd recommend…" → present **good-better-best** → "Everything
> includes your **free proof** before anything prints, and it ships in **5 days**. I'll send this over
> right now — want it by email or text?"

### 3.4 Objection handling (warm reframes, never defensive)
- **"That's more than I wanted to spend."** → "Totally hear you. Let's look at what matters most for
  your event — we can start with [smaller tier] and you'll still show up looking sharp. What's your
  target?" *(explore the real need; never just discount — respect the floor §1.4)*
- **"I need to think about it."** → "Of course! Want me to send the free proof in the meantime? That
  way you can *see* it with your logo — no commitment — and decide from there." *(keeps it moving)*
- **"Can you do it faster?"** → "Our standard is 5 business days, and we hit it. If it's tight, tell
  me your date and I'll tell you straight if we can make it." *(honest, confident)*
- **"Why you vs. [online site]?"** → "Those are templates and a faraway printer. We're real people in
  LA, you get a free proof, a person who answers, and we make sure you show up right. *Hecho bien.*"

### 3.5 The bilingual warmth (the heart)
When the customer speaks Spanish, switch naturally. *"Tu evento, tu marca, hecho bien. Mándame tu logo
y te hago una prueba gratis — sin compromiso — y te lo entregamos en 5 días."* Warm, neighborly, real.

## §4 — FOLLOW-UP SEQUENCE (speed + persistence, never annoying)

**Speed-to-lead is #1:** respond to any new warm lead **within minutes** if possible, same business
day at worst. First to respond usually wins.

**If you can't reach them by phone:**
- **Attempt 1 (Day 0):** Call. No answer → leave a warm voicemail + immediately **text/email** ("Hi
  [Name], Venus from Promo & Grow — tried you just now about your [event]. Send your logo and I'll get
  a free proof started! Or call me back at [ext].")
- **Attempt 2 (Day 1–2):** Call again at a different time of day → no answer → email with the free-proof
  offer + restate 5-day.
- **Attempt 3 (Day 3):** "Any questions before your [event date]? Happy to jump on a quick call."
- **Attempt 4 (Day 7):** "Your event's coming up — want to start the 5-day production clock so you're
  covered?" *(deadline-aware, helpful)*
- **After Day 7:** move to monthly "checking in" / nurture. Don't hound.

**For unpaid quotes/invoices (the hidden revenue):** same Day 1 / Day 3 / Day 7 cadence — re-send the
proof, offer to adjust, then the deadline nudge.
