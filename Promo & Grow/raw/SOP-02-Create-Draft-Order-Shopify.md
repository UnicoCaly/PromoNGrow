# SOP-02 — Create a Draft Order in Shopify
**Covers Looms 4–5 (creating & sending orders in the dashboard) · Owner: Carlos · Updated: June 6, 2026**

### Purpose
Turn a quote into a Shopify **draft order** that becomes an invoice the customer pays — the concierge close in action.

### Scope
All orders built by hand (the way ~most of our revenue comes in). We build the cart for the customer and send them the invoice instead of making them self-serve.

### Who / When
Venus, after she's agreed a price with a warm lead (SOP-01). Week 1: Carlos reviews every draft before it sends.

### Where you live
**Shopify Admin → Orders → Drafts.** This is your home base. Drafts = orders you've created and emailed; they flip to **Completed** when they turn into paid invoices.

### Steps
1. **Start the draft:** Orders → Drafts → **Create order**. (Always start from Drafts, not from a live order.)
2. **Add catalog products:**
   - Click **Add product**, search (e.g. "tent", "table throw").
   - Pick the **exact variant/combo**. For tents: remove the default selection, then scroll to the right combination — reinforcement strip color, sewing thread, bag type, wall config (Tent Only / Half Wall / Full Wall / Complete Set). The admin side lists each combo as its own line; you choose it (the customer-facing site builds it for them).
3. **Add print collateral — now in our catalog!** Business cards, postcards, flyers/brochures, NCR forms & pads, 13oz banners, and vehicle magnets are loaded in Shopify as **hidden quoting products** (they don't show on the website). Search the name (e.g. "business cards"), pick the **run-size / size variant**, and the **set price fills in automatically** — no price math.
   - *Truly custom* item not in the catalog at all? Add a **custom item**, name it clearly (e.g. "16PT Business Card — 4/4 — 1,000"), get the price from Carlos (route-through), and enter price per unit (total ÷ quantity).
4. **Attach the client** (SOP-03): search existing by name/email/phone, or add new.
5. **Shipping:** add the shipping line. **Orders $500+ ship free (ground)** — most tents, canopies, and large items clear that automatically. Smaller items: **~$30 standard ground** ($25–45 by size). Faster = a custom/closing quote (expedited).
6. **Save** the draft.
7. **Send the invoice email** (SOP-04). The customer gets a checkout link, enters their shipping/payment, and pays (SOP-05).

### Output
A saved draft order with correct products, client, and shipping — ready to invoice.

### Exceptions & edge cases
| Scenario | What to do |
|---|---|
| Product isn't in the catalog | Add it as a custom item with a clear name + price per unit (Step 3). |
| Multiple items (e.g. tent + business cards) | Add each as its own line; both show on the customer's cart. |
| Missing customer info (e.g. no phone) | Get it from the client or note it; complete in SOP-03 before sending. |
| Rush / custom shipping | Don't guess — confirm the figure with Carlos, then add the line. |
| Price uncertain | Stop. Finish pricing (SOP-01 / route-through) before sending. |
