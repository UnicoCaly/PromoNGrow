# Re-Record Outlines — clean scripts for QuickTime refilms
### Carlos: use these to refilm tighter, shorter videos. Each is built from your original Loom, trimmed to the essentials.

**Updated:** June 6, 2026 · pairs with the matching SOP for each.

## Universal rules (apply to every video)
- **Keep it 2–4 minutes.** One task per video. If it runs long, split it.
- **Say the goal in the first 10 seconds:** "In this video you'll learn how to ___."
- **Narrate what you click** ("I'm going to Orders, then Drafts, then Create order").
- **End with a one-line recap** ("So that's how you ___").
- **On screen, hide:** vendor cost, passwords, and any other customer's private data. Blur or use a test record.
- **Re-record if you ramble** — these replace messy first takes, so trim the "umms."

> ⚠️ **Confidentiality split:** Videos **1–3 are your internal pricing engine** ("top-down," not for entry-level reps). Keep those private **or** record a separate Venus-facing version that quotes from the **Master Price Sheet without showing 4over cost**. Videos **4–10 are Venus-facing** operational SOPs.
