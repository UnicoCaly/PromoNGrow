## 1. WHO WE ARE

Promo & Grow is a Los Angeles (Lynwood) branding & custom-display company — event tents, table covers,
flags, trade-show fabric displays, wall art. We're the evolution of **PrintCo Direct**, built on its
promise: *"Promote & Grow."* Nearly 20 years of print and branding craft, produced in-house, delivered
in **5 business days**.

**But here's what we actually do:** we equip the entrepreneur operating on *ganas* — the raised-on-hustle
business owner who's building their own brand — to show up like a pro. Some have their design handled
and just need us to print it right, fast. Others need support to get there. We serve both.

**We don't sell tents. We sell the moment a scrappy hustle starts looking legit.**

*(Ganas = the hunger, the drive, the will to push when you've got more ambition than budget. It's the
fuel of the self-made — and it's exactly who this brand is for.)*

---

## 2. WHO WE'RE FOR

Design the brand's **heart** for Persona 1, and let it scale up to 2 and 3. (A brand that authentically
champions the underdog can still win the corporate buyer; the reverse doesn't work.)

1. **"La Emprendedora" (the heart)** — the local, often Spanish-speaking small-business owner: taquería,
   quinceañera/party vendor, panadería, food truck, neighborhood shop. Operates on ganas. Has an event
   coming and needs to look legit, fast. Lives on her phone. **Fear:** looking small at her big moment.
   **Dream:** showing up like she's already made it.
2. **The Brand Manager** (e.g. a Donut Media marketing lead) — growing company, trade show or activation,
   tight deadline, knows design. **Wants:** a partner who just *delivers*, fast, no drama.
3. **The Agency / Event Pro** — runs activations, needs a dependable production partner who makes them
   look good. **Wants:** consistency, craft, easy reorders.

**The thread connecting all three:** someone with a moment that can't wait, something to prove, and the
drive to show up bigger than they are.

A bilingual growth partner — **warm, confident, encouraging, real.** Talks *with* you like a neighbor

## 5. CREATIVE DIRECTION — inspiration, not instruction 🎨

**This section is where we're *drawn* — not where you're *boxed in.*** Read it, take what serves the
brand, leave what doesn't, bring us something better if you've got it.

**Things Carlos is excited about (prompts to explore, free to set aside):**
- **CMYK as a palette idea** — there's something meaningful about a 20-year *print house* building its
  identity from CMYK, the literal language of print. It's heritage no national competitor or agency can
  authentically claim. *We're drawn to it as a nod to who we are — but if a different direction serves
  the brand better, we trust your call.*
- **A "neon meets vintage" energy** — neon for the ganas (bold, energetic, "don't blend in"), tempered
  by vintage flare for soul and craft (so it reads established and premium, never trendy-for-trendy's-sake).
  Roughly a balanced mix of the two.
- **Fresh logo** — start clean. Keep only the *vintage spirit*, not the old retro-bubble wordmark.
- **Open on the neutral/grounding tone** — we're open to bold, neon-forward directions; the grounding
  color that keeps it premium is yours to find. *(Carlos is also collecting visual references — he'll
  share his moodboard to show the vibe he's reaching for.)*

**The ONE thing that's a real guardrail (not negotiable):**
> The brand must feel **premium, warm, and legit** — never cheap, never bargain-bin, never generic
> print-shop, never cold/corporate. Our customer is accessible-budget but wants to look like a market
> leader. *Accessible ≠ cheap-looking.* This is the line to protect. Everything else — color, type,
> logo, layout — is your creative territory.

**The test for any direction:** *Would this make a taquería owner proud to show up with it — AND make a
Donut Media brand manager trust us with their deadline?* If yes, it's on-brand.

## 6. WHAT WE NEED (scope)

**Brand foundation (identity):**
- Logo system (primary, secondary, mark/icon, clear-space, sizing)
- Color palette — primary/secondary, with print **and** web/digital breakdowns
- Typography system (display, headline, body, UI), with hierarchy + bilingual (ES/EN) character support
- Logo usage do's & don'ts, iconography style, imagery/photography direction

**Digital design system (Figma):**
- Design tokens/variables (color, type, spacing as reusable variables — one source of truth)
- Spacing & layout: 8-pt grid, padding/margin scale, breakpoints (mobile-first — our buyers are on phones)
- Components with real states: buttons (default/hover/pressed/disabled), forms, cards, nav, badges, the
  quote/"free proof" CTA, trust elements
- Accessibility: passing contrast ratios, proper touch-target sizes
- Usage docs so the team can build on-brand without you in the room

**Where it shows up first:** the website (product pages + the quote/free-proof flow), social templates,
email templates — all working in both English and Spanish.
