# SOP-01 — Quoting & Pricing a Job
**Covers Looms 1–3 (business cards, postcards, brochures) · Owner: Carlos · Updated: June 6, 2026**

### Purpose
Produce a fast, accurate quote a customer can pay — without exposing cost or guessing.

### Scope
All quoting for catalog + standard print collateral. Fully custom / non-listed items route to Carlos. Complex branding (wraps, channel letters, brand systems) escalates to Proágo.

### Who / When
Venus, the moment a warm lead asks "how much?" — by phone, email, or text. Speed-to-lead is #1: respond within minutes.

### Steps
1. **Get the 5 inputs:** product, quantity, size/specs, art/logo, and the event / needed-by date.
2. **Find the price:**
   - On the **Master Price Sheet**? → use the set price. Done.
   - Tent/display by configuration? → build the combo in the draft order (SOP-02); price builds itself.
   - Print collateral? → it's now in the Shopify catalog as a hidden quoting product: search the name, pick the run size, and the price fills in. (Run-size ladders are also on the Master Price Sheet for reference.)
   - Not listed / fully custom? → **ask Carlos for the number** (route-through). Don't invent it.
3. **Check the floor:** minimum order **$100**. No discounts without Carlos's OK (Week 1).
4. **Offer good-better-best** when natural (Tent Only / Half Wall / Complete Set; or qty breaks on print).
5. **Quote it** with the warm note (SOP-04) — restate **free proof** + delivery time.

### Product knowledge (so you sound expert)
- **Business cards:** default is **16PT stock, matte, double-sided (4/4)** — that's ~95% of orders. UV is the glossy option. **Spot UV** (gloss on one element, e.g. the logo) is a nice **upsell** — mention it, but standard is matte. Turnaround **~4 business days**.
- **Postcards:** **16PT**, gloss. Single- and double-sided are basically the same price, so **always quote double-sided**. Sizes: 4x6, 5x7, 5.5x8.5 (half-page).
- **Brochures / flyers:** All-Inclusive 8.5x11. Standard coating is **aqueous** (subtle gloss; mostly the paper's luster). **Folding is included** in the price (trifold, half-fold). Turnaround **~3 days** (printed and shipped from regional facilities). Pricier than online template shops — we win on speed + a real person + free proof.

### Output
A clean quote/price ready to drop into a Shopify draft order (SOP-02), at or above the floor, with proof + delivery stated.

### Exceptions & edge cases
| Scenario | What to do |
|---|---|
| Item not on the price sheet | Route-through: ask Carlos for the number. |
| Client wants below list / a discount | Explore the real need first (SOP-04 scripts). Escalate to Carlos; never go below the $100 floor. |
| Rush (faster than standard) | Tell them honestly; rush adds cost → Carlos confirms a custom/closing quote. |
| Spot UV / specialty finish | Upsell opportunity — quote separately, confirm cost with Carlos. |
| Complex branding (wraps, channel letters, brand system) | Pitch it, escalate to Carlos/Proágo — don't quote it here. |
