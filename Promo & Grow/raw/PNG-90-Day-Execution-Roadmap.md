# Promo & Grow — 90-Day Execution Roadmap

**Goal:** Bridge cold web traffic into the concierge close that already converts, prove it organically,
then layer cold email + ads. Target: a $20K-capable funnel by peak season.
**Operating law:** Prove organic conversion → THEN cold email → THEN paid. No paid spend until the
funnel passes BOTH the Strategist and Direct Response checks (see Proago SOP — the CMAA lesson).

**Owners:** [C]=Carlos/VA · [CC]=Claude Code (theme) · [AI]=Claude (admin/copy/data)

---

## THE FUNNEL TRUTH (what we're fixing)
180-day Shopify funnel: 3,936 sessions → 7 add-to-actions → 31 orders.
More orders completed than carts started = the revenue runs through the **concierge close**, not the web cart.
**The leak is at the top: cold visitors have nothing to do but a checkout they'll never use.**
The entire roadmap builds + feeds the "Get a Free Proof" on-ramp into the concierge close.

## THE TWO-MODE GATE (paste before every launch)
1. **Strategist:** Right audience? Right message? Right time? Protects the brand?
2. **Direct Response:** Works on a phone Wednesday 9am? One-sentence ask? Form tested? Tracking live?
   Follow-up staged? Baseline established?
**Both must pass. Either fails → do not launch.** (This is the lesson that cost 82 burned visitors.)
