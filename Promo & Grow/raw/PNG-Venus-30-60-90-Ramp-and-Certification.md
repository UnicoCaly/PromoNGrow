# Promo & Grow — 30/60/90 Ramp + Closer Certification
### Learn → Apply → Accelerate. Gated on skill, not just activity.

**For:** Venus (and every closer after her) · **Owner:** Carlos · **Built:** June 6, 2026.
**How to use:** Each phase has a focus, daily/weekly work, and a **certification gate** Venus must pass to advance. Weekly 15-min check-in every Friday (pairs with the Weekly Scorecard). No quota pressure in month one — we reward learning velocity and warm wins.

> Why gated: structured, competency-gated ramps retain ~50% more reps, cut ramp ~34%, and lift first-year revenue ~23% (see the Best-Practices Brief). The gates make "ready" objective.

---

## Phase 1 — LEARN (Days 1–30) · "Foundations"
**Goal:** know the product, the price, the process, and our voice — and bank a few warm wins with the safety on.

**Focus & work:**
- Read the kit: Vision, Closer Workbook, Master Price Sheet, Brand Voice, SOP Library.
- Watch Carlos's Loom SOPs (Shopify draft order, client, invoice email, proof/checkout). **Study them like recorded top calls** — take notes, then debrief with Carlos.
- Build practice draft orders: 1 catalog (tent) + 1 print item (now in the Shopify catalog) — Carlos reviews before anything sends.
- Work the **warmest** queue supervised: the open drafts (#D77 ask for Temo, #D66), then the 25 hottest reactivation accounts. **Carlos reviews every quote before it goes out.**
- Speed-to-lead habit from Day 1; tag every touch in Shopify.

**🎓 Gate to advance — "Foundations Certified" (Carlos signs off when Venus can):**
- [ ] Quote any catalog item + any print item correctly from the price sheet (no help).
- [ ] Build a clean Shopify draft order → client → invoice email end-to-end.
- [ ] Pass the **Knowledge Quiz at 85%+** (products, pricing, process, voice).
- [ ] Name the floor + escalation triggers (institutional = DNC; wraps/channel letters/brand systems → Carlos/Proágo).
- [ ] Run one live warm call using the script, reviewed by Carlos.
- [ ] First reactivation conversation logged.

---

## Phase 2 — APPLY (Days 31–60) · "Closer Certified"
**Goal:** sell independently on the warm queue, close her first deals, build follow-through into muscle memory.

**Focus & work:**
- Quotes go out with **spot-checks**, not full review (she earned it in Phase 1).
- Reactivation calling at volume — work down the 162-account list, warmest first; draft follow-ups; Day 1/3/7 cadence, nothing slips.
- First closes. Begin the **"Your Playbook"** voice-capture habit (the week-2 unlock) — save winning calls/emails.
- Objection drills 2x/week (LAER + Feel-Felt-Found); re-take the quiz weekly (spaced repetition).
- Start tracking AOV — offer good-better-best and bundles.

**🎓 Gate to advance — "Closer Certified" (the trial→commit milestone, ~Day 60–90):**
- [ ] Speed-to-lead and follow-through **green** on the scorecard for 3+ weeks.
- [ ] Closed her first handful of paid invoices (reactivation and/or inbound).
- [ ] Quotes consistently accurate; floor respected; clean draft orders.
- [ ] Handles the top 5 objections fluidly in a role-play (Carlos scores it).
- [ ] Voice is on-brand and customers respond.
- [ ] **Self-manages** off the scoreboard.
> Passing aligns with the **3-month rate bump to $15.75** and **raising her weekly hours cap** (the clean trial→commit mechanism).

---

## Phase 3 — ACCELERATE (Days 61–90) · "Senior Closer track"
**Goal:** own the warm queue, push AOV, and start compounding.

**Focus & work:**
- Owns the full warm queue + inbound; Carlos out of the day-to-day close.
- Higher daily activity; lean into **AOV levers** — bundles, reorders, institutional-style POs (the private ones), complete-set tents.
- Reverse-shadow: Carlos listens to a few live calls to confirm she's fully independent.
- Begins feeding the system — her saved playbook trains the next hire.
- **Unlock candidate:** once the close is proven, set up her Cowork account for voice-matched follow-up drafting (the automation step).

**🎓 Milestone — "Senior Closer track":**
- [ ] Consistent monthly closes; pipeline building on its own.
- [ ] Measurable AOV lift (bundles/reorders).
- [ ] Could onboard the next rep from her own playbook.
- [ ] Ready for graduated scope (eventually cold, once the funnel's proven).

---

## The cadence that runs it
- **Daily:** speed-to-lead, warm-queue work, tag every touch.
- **Weekly (Fri, 15 min):** scorecard review + one fix + one drill + re-quiz. Celebrate the win.
- **Per phase:** certification gate before advancing. Don't move the goalposts — coach to the gate.

## Certification = the rate ladder
| Cert | ~Timing | Unlocks |
|---|---|---|
| Foundations Certified | ~Day 30 | Quotes go out with spot-checks (more autonomy) |
| Closer Certified | ~Day 60–90 | 3-month rate bump ($15.75) + higher hours cap |
| Senior Closer track | ~Day 90+ | Graduated scope + Cowork automation |
