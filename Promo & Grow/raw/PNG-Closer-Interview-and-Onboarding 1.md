# PNG — Closer Interview Guide + Onboarding Package

**Role:** Bilingual Sales Closer / Account Manager · **Candidate:** Venus C. (or shortlist)
**Format:** 30–40 min phone/Zoom · **Goal:** confirm phone warmth + relationship-selling fit, then
fast-start a paid trial on warm leads.

---

## PART 1 — THE INTERVIEW (30–40 min)

**Before the call (2 min):** Re-listen to her voice clip. The #1 question this interview answers:
*"Would my customer — a taquería owner, a stressed brand manager — trust this voice and feel taken
care of?"* Everything else is secondary.

### Opening (5 min) — set tone, watch how she builds rapport
- Warm welcome, in Spanish or English — see which she defaults to and how naturally she switches.
- "Tell me a little about yourself and why this role caught your eye."
  *(Listen for: warmth, does she connect? Is she easy to talk to? This IS the job.)*

### Core questions (20 min)

**On relationship selling (her stated strength — test it's real):**
1. "Walk me through a time you turned a hesitant customer into a happy one. What did you actually say?"
   *(Listen for: specifics, empathy, no pressure. She wrote about this — does she live it?)*
2. "A customer says 'that's more than I wanted to spend.' How do you handle it?"
   *(Listen for: she explores the real need, doesn't discount-dump or get pushy. Reframes value.)*
3. "How do you follow up with someone who got a quote but went quiet — without being annoying?"
   *(This is half the job — quote follow-up. Listen for: persistence with warmth.)*

**On speed + reliability (our core promise):**
4. "This business runs on responding fast — first to answer often wins. How do you stay on top of
   leads so none slip?"
   *(Listen for: a system, organization, follow-through.)*

**On the bilingual customer (the heart of the brand):**
5. *Switch to Spanish:* "Imagina que te llama un dueño de taquería que necesita una carpa para su
   evento, pero no está seguro de comprar en línea. ¿Cómo le hablarías?"
   *(THE key test — live, unscripted Spanish phone warmth. Does she sound like someone the customer
   would trust? Natural, warm, not stiff?)*

**On AI + tools (she claims it — verify):**
6. "You mentioned using ChatGPT — give me a specific example of how you used it last week and what
   you did with it." *(Vague answer = it's not real. Specific = she's genuinely AI-proficient.)*

**On the work itself:**
7. "You'd be learning our product, our Shopify, building quotes. How do you get up to speed on a new
   product fast?" *(Listen for: curiosity, self-starting, not needing hand-holding.)*

### Logistics & close (10 min)
- Confirm: weekly hours, overlap with LA business hours (9–5 PT), start availability (next week?).
- Confirm rate ($15/hr) and the **paid-trial structure** (below) — frame it positively: "I like to
  start with a short paid trial on real leads so we both see the fit. Sound good?"
- Tell her next steps + when she'll hear back. End warm.

### Red flags to watch
- Talks *at* you / over-scripted / pushy → wrong for our trust-based market.
- Vague on the AI question → padded the résumé.
- Can't give a specific customer story → less real experience than the letter suggests.
- Stiff or uncomfortable in the live Spanish prompt → the one non-negotiable miss.

## PART 2 — THE 60-SECOND SCORECARD (fill out right after)

Rate 1–5. Phone warmth is weighted double — it's the job.

| Trait | Score (1–5) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Phone warmth / would my customer trust her? (×2)** | | |
| Relationship-selling (empathy, no pressure) | | |
| Speed / organization / follow-through | | |
| Live Spanish warmth (Q5) | | |
| AI-proficiency (real, not vague) | | |
| Learns fast / self-starter | | |
| Reliability signals (showed up prepared, on time) | | |

**Decision rule:** Strong on phone warmth + Spanish + relationship-selling → move to paid trial.
A miss on phone warmth → pass, no matter how good the résumé.

## PART 3 — THE PAID TRIAL (de-risks the "new on Upwork" flag)

Before full commitment, a short paid trial (1–2 weeks, ~10–15 hrs):
- Give her **3–5 real WARM leads** — email repliers/clickers, a couple of VIP/reactivation contacts.
  NOT cold outreach. We want to see her close warm, which is the day-one job anyway.
- Watch: response speed, tone on calls/texts, how she logs notes, whether she follows up unprompted.
- Success = she handles warm leads with warmth + organization and ideally moves one toward a quote/sale.
- If strong → onboard fully (below). If not → you've learned cheaply.

## PART 4 — THE ONBOARDING PACKAGE (build this so she's productive Day 1)

A cohesive starter kit so she can run estimates and work leads without you hand-holding. Assemble:

### Access & identity
- [ ] **Crescendo VOIP extension** — her own line for calls (inbound + outbound)
- [ ] **Company email address** (e.g. firstname@promongrow.com) — for quotes, follow-ups, customer comms
- [ ] **Shopify staff account** (limited permissions: orders, draft orders, products, customers — NOT
      settings/payments/theme). Shopify lets you scope staff access — give only what the role needs.
- [ ] Any quote-app access (if you install one)
- [ ] Shared lead tracker access (the warm-leads queue + her pipeline notes)

### Reference docs (the "do her own estimates" kit)
- [ ] **Price list / estimate sheet** — base prices + bundle pricing + "starting at" figures, so she can
      quote without asking you each time. (The single most important doc for autonomy.)
- [ ] **Product cheat-sheet** — the catalog in plain language: what each product is, sizes, use cases,
      the 5-day promise, the free-proof process. (She references this on calls.)
- [ ] **How-to-build-a-draft-order SOP** — step-by-step (ideally a Loom): from customer info → draft
      order in Shopify → send invoice. This is the core workflow.
- [ ] **Lead-handling SOP** — how to log a lead, response-time expectation, follow-up cadence
      (Day 1 / 3 / 7 on unpaid quotes), when to escalate to you.
- [ ] **Brand voice one-pager** — pull from the Company Brief: warm, bilingual, never pushy, "free proof"
      framing, the promise. So her customer comms sound like P&G.
- [ ] **FAQ / objection-handling sheet** — common questions + the on-brand answers (price, turnaround,
      proof, shipping, rush orders).

### The "record and loop" habit (your scale strategy)
- [ ] From Day 1, ask her to **Loom her workflows** as she learns them — building a quote, handling a
      call type, a follow-up. This builds your SOP library *as she works*, so the next hire onboards
      faster and you can automate more later. (This was your instinct — bake it in now.)

### First-week ramp
- **Day 1–2:** access setup, read the docs, shadow/listen to a couple of your calls if possible,
  learn the product + draft-order flow.
- **Day 3–5:** start on the warm-lead trial — supervised, you review her quotes before they send.
- **Week 2:** more autonomy on warm leads; she's building estimates herself, you spot-check.
- **Day 30:** review against the scorecard traits; decide on expanded hours / scope.

---

## PART 5 — A NOTE ON SECURITY (credentials)
- Use **scoped Shopify staff permissions**, never your master login. Give the minimum the role needs.
- Share passwords through a proper manager (1Password/Bitwarden shared vault), not plain text/email.
- For a brand-new contractor, start with the trial-level access; expand once trust is established.
