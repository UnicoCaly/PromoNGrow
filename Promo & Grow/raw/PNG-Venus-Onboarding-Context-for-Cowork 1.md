$50K/mo is a good **12–18 month north star**, but it is NOT "$20K with a bigger number" — it's a different engine. Three levers, only one is near-term:

- **More traffic** (~5,000 qualified sessions) → paid + cold + SEO all firing = Phases 3–4, *post-proof.* Not near-term.
- **Higher AOV** (~$1,000 avg vs. $400) → institutional POs (South Gate), reorders (Donut), bundles, Proágo-side club work. **This is the realistic lever.**
- **Blend:** ~3,000 sessions × 3% × ~$550.

**Takeaway:** $50K is powered by **AOV + repeat/institutional revenue**, not by cranking traffic. That changes who Venus chases first — **PO accounts and reorders over volume.**

**The discipline flag (important):** The bar that matters *right now* is **Venus closing her first warm leads and the bridge proving out** — not the target number. Raising the goal before she's closed a single lead is the goalpost-moving version of the build-vs-sell pattern. **Keep $50K on the wall; keep the operating focus on the next 30 days.** Hit the $20K path, then restage to $50K with a real AOV plan.
