## THE JOB POST (paste into Upwork)

**Title:** Bilingual Creative Operator — Design + AI + Sales + Shopify (Small LA Brand, Big Growth)

**Type:** Part-time to start (15–20 hrs/week), strong path to full-time
**Rate:** $20–30/hr DOE (we pay for range + reliability)
**Location:** Latin America (El Salvador / Central America ideal — must overlap US Pacific hours)

---

**The short version**
We're a 20-year Los Angeles custom event-display company (branded tents, table covers, flags, trade-show displays — made in-house, delivered in 5 days). We're looking for one rare person who can wear several hats well: **design + content, AI-powered execution, customer sales/relationships in Spanish & English, and Shopify/order operations.** Most people are great at one of these. If you're genuinely strong across most of them, keep reading — this is a role you can grow into running.

**What you'd own (a mix — not all at once on day one):**
- **Design & content:** social graphics + short video, email campaigns, simple product/marketing assets, on-brand and clean (we have a brand kit and templates — you make them sing)
- **AI-powered execution:** you use AI tools daily to work faster — drafting copy, speeding design, building SOPs. You're not afraid of new tools; you reach for them.
- **Sales & relationships (bilingual):** respond fast to warm leads and past customers by phone/email, walk them through our free-proof process, and close orders. Many of our customers are Spanish-speaking small-business owners — you'll take care of them in their language.
- **Operations:** build orders in Shopify, manage products, follow up on quotes/invoices, keep clean records.
- **Document everything:** record short Looms of your workflows so we can build a system as we grow.

**You're a great fit if you:**
- Are **fully bilingual** — natural, warm Spanish AND clear English (non-negotiable)
- Can show a **design portfolio** that's clean and professional (not flashy — trustworthy and sharp)
- **Actually use AI tools** in your daily work and can prove it
- Are **comfortable on the phone** and genuinely good with people (this is the rare part — most designers aren't, and that's okay, but this role needs it)
- Are organized, reliable, and follow up without being chased
- Overlap with Los Angeles business hours (9–5 Pacific)

**Honest note:** This is a "several hats" role for someone who likes variety and wants to grow into a lead/manager seat as we scale. If you only do ONE of these things, that's totally fine — but this particular role isn't the fit, and we have other roles. We're looking for the rare generalist.

**To apply:** follow the test below exactly. We only review applications that complete it.

## THE SCREENING TEST (proves all four skills at once — put at end of post)

> **How to apply — read all the way through (we're testing that you do):**
>
> 1. Start your application with the word **"CINCO"** in all caps. We skip any application without it.
>
> 2. **Design (show, don't tell):** Link a portfolio OR attach 2–3 samples. Bonus: take any product photo of an event tent/table cover (yours or a stock example) and make one clean branded social graphic for a company called "Promo & Grow" — show us your eye.
>
> 3. **AI (prove it's real):** In a few sentences, tell us one specific way you used an AI tool in the last week — the actual prompt or workflow, and what you did with the output. (Vague "I use ChatGPT a lot" answers tell us it's not real.)
>
> 4. **Sales voice (the rare skill):** Record a 60–90 second voice clip or Loom — **in Spanish** — as if you're talking to a small-business owner who called about a custom tent but is unsure about ordering online. Talk to them the way you actually would. Warm, natural, trustworthy.
>
> 5. **Ops/reliability:** Tell us your time zone, weekly availability overlapping LA hours, and one sentence on how you keep your tasks/follow-ups organized.

## WHY THIS POST IS BUILT THIS WAY (notes for Carlos)

- **The test demands all four skills in one application.** A pretender who's only a designer will nail #2 and #3 but freeze on the Spanish sales clip (#4). A salesperson with no design will skip #2. **The test does your filtering — almost nobody fakes all four.** That's how you find a real unicorn vs. a confident generalist.
- **"CINCO" gate** (different word than the closer post, so you can tell which post an applicant answered).
- **#4 is still the make-or-break,** same as the closer role — because sales is the skill designers most often lack, and your goal is transactional revenue. If the clip isn't warm and trustworthy, pass, no matter how good the design is.
- **Rate set a notch higher ($20–30)** — a true unicorn is worth it and won't bite at closer-level pay. You said design is only "nice-to-have," so don't overpay for design polish alone; pay for the *combination*.
- **"Several hats, grow into a lead seat"** framing attracts the ambitious generalist who wants ownership — that's who unicorns actually are.

## HOW TO JUDGE THE APPLICANTS (fast)
1. Delete anything without "CINCO."
2. **Listen to the Spanish sales clip FIRST.** No warmth on the phone = pass, regardless of design. (Sales is the rare gene; design you can supplement.)
3. Then check design (clean & trustworthy? matches a small-brand vibe?).
4. Then the AI answer (specific & real, or vague & fake?).
5. Shortlist only those strong on **clip + at least 2 of the other 3.** A true 4/4 is the unicorn — move fast, they get poached.
6. Paid trial before hiring: one small real task touching design + one warm lead. Watch them switch hats.

## RUNNING BOTH POSTS AT ONCE
- Different gate words (TOLDO = closer, CINCO = unicorn) tell you which role each person is going for.
- If a strong **unicorn** lands → they can be your single hire (design + sales + ops).

- If only strong **designers** land (great design, weak phone) → they're the future *design/ops* hire, NOT the closer. Don't force a designer into the sales seat.
- Let the market decide which post performs — that's the cheapest market research you'll run.
