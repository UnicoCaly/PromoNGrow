## 🎨 Creative direction (what the image/video should be)

Use **real proof, not stock.** Strongest → easiest:
1. **Carousel of actual jobs** — photos of real tents/banners/table covers you've produced (logos visible). Authentic > polished.
2. **"Free proof" mockup shot** — a logo shown as a digital proof next to the finished printed product (the visual promise of the wedge).
3. **15-sec Reel** — quick montage: blank product → logo drops on → "FREE proof in your inbox" → "Delivered in 5 days" stamp → Spanish VO. Vertical 9:16.
4. **Text-on-image** (fast option): a clean product photo with overlay — *"Tu diseño GRATIS antes de pagar · Entrega en 5 días"* in PNG navy/copper.

**Brand:** navy `#1B2838`, copper `#C17A3A`, off-white. Keep on-image text minimal (Meta favors low text density). Logo bottom-corner.

## 📋 The 60-second qualifier (Instant Form questions)

Order matters — easy first, filters last. This is what keeps junk leads off Venus's plate.

1. **What do you need?** *(multiple choice — fastest qualifier)*
   Tents/Canopies · Table covers · Banners/Flags · Signage · Business cards/Print · Vehicle magnets/graphics · Other
   *(ES: Carpas · Manteles · Banners/Banderas · Letreros · Tarjetas/Impresión · Imanes/Gráficos para auto · Otro)*
2. **Business name** *(short answer)*
3. **When do you need it / event date?** *(Within 1 week · 2–4 weeks · 1–2 months · Just exploring)* — flags urgency
4. **Roughly how much are you looking to invest?** *(Under $250 · $250–$1,000 · $1,000–$3,000 · $3,000+ · Not sure yet)* — the budget filter
5. **Best phone / WhatsApp** *(prefilled)* + **Email** *(prefilled)*
6. **Preferred language / Idioma:** English · Español

> **Keep it to these 6.** More questions = fewer (but not better) leads; these six give Venus everything she needs to call prepared and skip the price-shoppers.
