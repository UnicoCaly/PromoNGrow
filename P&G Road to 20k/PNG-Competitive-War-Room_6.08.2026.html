01 · The Strategy Spine

Two tracks, sequenced — not one bet.

Your own 180-day data settled the debate: the business converts through a human concierge close, not the web cart. So we don't gamble the bridge revenue on a self-serve rebuild. We run the proven close now and engineer the self-serve machine alongside it — and every concierge order documents what the website must eventually do on its own.

Track A · Now (revenue)

The Concierge Bridge

Give cold traffic a low-friction on-ramp into the close that already works: "Get a Free Proof." Measure quote requests, not checkouts.

  • Tiered CTA: quote-first on tents/displays/bundles, cart-first on flags/covers
  • Free-proof form → instant auto-reply + speed-to-lead alert → draft order
  • Trust stack on every PDP (reviews, free-proof-before-pay, 5-day, how-it-works)
  • Pixels installed now · Venus works warm leads · gate before paid
Track B · Build-toward (the machine)

Self-Serve Checkout

Engineer the $1,500 add-to-cart the way Expoprint & Signwin do — so it's safe enough for a stranger to buy without you.

  • Package-ize SKUs + visible "buy → design → approve → deliver" ribbon
  • Self-serve design: locked Canva templates now → 3D configurator later
  • "Free AI Artwork Check" (Signwin already proves the market wants it)
  • Bilingual Spanish + Shop Pay installments — moats none of them combine

02 · Competitor Teardowns

Six players, three journey models.

Your named three (Packeze, Expoprint, Instent) plus the SEG specialists (Signwin, Displays2go, ExpoMarketing). Open each card for the full journey, conversion mechanics, ad posture, and what to steal. Badges: Self-serve checkout-first · Hybrid buy + quote · Quote-led concierge.

Expoprint

expoprint.io
Self-serve
10×10 tent from $595 · booths to ~$10k · made-to-order · CA LLC, ~23-mo domain
The blueprint for Track B. "Design & Order Event Displays in Minutes — customize in 3D." A real self-serve high-ticket flow built on a proprietary 3D studio.
Full teardown

Journey: Product page (3D badge + trust chips: Free Design · Free Artwork Fix · 3D Tool) → configurator (size, walls, turnaround/Rush inside the PDP) → 3D Design Studio at app.expoprint.io (drop multiple products into one real-time scene, add logo/text, save to account) → Add to cart → Shopify checkout → mandatory free PDF proof before production. CTA is "Add to cart," not "Request a quote."

Artwork — 3 lanes: DIY in the 3D tool · free pro design in 30–60 min w/ unlimited revisions · upload your own + templates. Removes the "I don't have art ready" objection entirely.

Mechanics: instant price ✓ · 3D ✓ · reviews on-page ✓ (self-hosted) · on-time guarantee ✓ (no price-beat) · "no promo codes" (anti-discount) · phone ✓ · chat was offline · turnaround 5–15 days (a fuzzy range).

Meta: UnknownGoogle: Likely Search · unconfirmedGTM present

Steal: the multi-product 3D scene + saved projects; three artwork lanes on every PDP; buy-then-proof model with an "As Easy as 1-2-3" strip.

Beat them: your hard 5-day promise beats their "5–15 day" range; bilingual + real LA production vs. their global facility network; offer the free proof before payment (they gate it post-purchase) + a price-beat.

06 · The Top-Down Game Plan

Plugged into your 90-Day Roadmap.

Same gated discipline you already wrote (prove organic → then cold email → then paid). The competitive findings sharpen each phase. Owners: C Carlos/VA · CC Claude Code · AI Claude admin · V Venus.

Phase 1 · Weeks 1–2 · Verify the bridge

Make the concierge on-ramp real — and start collecting ad data.

Ship the tiered "Get a Free Proof" CTA + quote form + auto-reply. Put the trust stack on every PDP — and steal now: free-proof-BEFORE-pay banner (beats Packeze/Displays2go), reviews on the PDP with count (Displays2go), stacked trust ribbon (Signwin). Install Meta Pixel + Google remarketing tag immediately — the data-loss clock. Direct-Response test the form on a phone.

Gate: funnel mechanically verified, pixels collecting → start the organic baseline count.
Phase 2 · Weeks 3–4 · Organic baseline

Prove cold traffic converts into quote requests — no spend yet.

Optimize GBP (drives calls), fire VIP + South Gate outreach, let SEO/AEO compound, publish 1–2 FAQ articles. Add the 4-door intent router to the homepage (ExpoMarketing) and size-segmented landing pages 10×10/10×15/10×20 (Instent). Venus works warm leads.

Gate (green light for paid): quote requests ~2 → 20+/mo, add-to-action 0.18% → 1%+.
Phase 3 · Weeks 5–8 · Cold email

Sequence the LA-metro ICP (only after the funnel proves out).

Separate sending domain. Field-marketing brands, breweries, event agencies, schools/districts, franchises — local first, bilingual where it fits. Drive to the free proof, not a hard sell (your 13%-click "reply to me" insight).

Gate: replies/quote requests at benchmark (reply 2–5%, positive 1–3%).
Phase 4 · Weeks 9–12 · Paid + retargeting

Retarget the pixel audience first, then high-intent search.

$5–10/day retargeting ("free proof before you pay"), then Google Search/Shopping on the high-intent terms. Run the live Ad-Library sweep first to counter-program competitor angles. Pre-flight checklist every launch; cost-per-sale < ~$60–70.

Gate: profitable cost-per-sale on one channel → scale into Oct–Nov peak.
Track B · Parallel · The self-serve machine

Build the $1,500-checkout the market already proves works.

B1 Package-ize SKUs + visible 4-step ribbon (Packeze). B2 Locked Canva templates per SKU now → 3D configurator later (Expoprint). B3 "Free AI Artwork Check" — your Phase-3 file checker, badged (Signwin proves demand). B4 Shop Pay Installments. B5 Bilingual go-live, hero pages first — the moat. Every Venus close gets documented (SOP/Loom) → maps exactly what the site must self-serve next.
