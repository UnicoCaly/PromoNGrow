## THE JOB POST (paste into Upwork)

**Title:** Bilingual (Spanish/English) Sales Closer & Account Manager — Custom Event Products

**Type:** Part-time to start (15–20 hrs/week), ongoing, with room to grow into full-time
**Rate:** $15–25/hr DOE
**Location preference:** Latin America (El Salvador / Central America ideal — US Central/Pacific time overlap required)

---

**About us**
Promo & Grow is a Los Angeles–based custom event-display company. We make branded tents, table covers, flags, and trade-show displays for small businesses — printed in-house and delivered in 5 business days. We've been doing this for 20+ years. A lot of our customers are Spanish-speaking small-business owners (food trucks, party/event vendors, local shops, schools), and we want someone who can take care of them in their own language.

**What this role is**
You'll be the friendly, fast, trustworthy voice that turns interested people into happy customers. Most of our sales happen by phone and email: someone asks about a custom tent, we send a free design proof, and we close the order. We already have warm leads — past customers to reconnect with, people who request quotes on our site, and a list of repeat clients. **Your job is to work those warm leads and close them — not cold-call strangers from scratch.**

**What you'll do**
- Respond fast to incoming quote requests and questions (speed matters — first to respond usually wins)
- Call and email warm leads and past customers (in Spanish and English) to reconnect and reorder
- Walk customers through our simple process: send your logo → free proof → 5-day delivery
- Build the order in Shopify (draft order) and send the invoice — we'll train you
- Follow up on quotes/invoices that haven't been paid (kind, helpful, persistent)
- Keep clean notes on every lead in our tracker
- Help our best clients feel taken care of so they come back

**You're a great fit if you:**
- Are **fully bilingual** — warm, natural, conversational Spanish AND clear English (this is non-negotiable)
- Are genuinely comfortable on the phone and enjoy talking to people
- Are organized and reliable — you follow up without being reminded
- Are quick with email/computers and can learn Shopify (we'll train)
- Bonus: any experience with sales, customer service, or small-business / event / printing industries
- Bonus: comfortable using AI tools (ChatGPT, etc.) to draft and speed up your work

**You're NOT a fit if:** you only do cold outreach scripts, you're slow to respond, or you're uncomfortable on the phone. This role is about relationships and trust, not pressure.

**Hours:** 15–20/week to start, with real overlap with US Pacific business hours (we're in LA). Growth to full-time for the right person.

**To apply:** (see screening test below — applications that don't follow it won't be reviewed)

## THE SCREENING TEST (the filter — put this at the end of the post)

> **How to apply — read carefully (we're testing if you do):**
>
> 1. Start your application with the word **"TOLDO"** in all caps. (We ignore any application that doesn't — it tells us you read to the end.)
>
> 2. In 3–4 sentences (in English), tell us about a time you turned a hesitant or unsure customer into a sale. What did you say or do?
>
> 3. **Record a short voice clip or Loom (60–90 seconds): pretend I'm a small-business owner who called asking about a custom tent for my taquería but I'm not sure about ordering online. Talk to me — in Spanish — the way you actually would.** This is the most important part. We want to hear your real phone voice and warmth.
>
> 4. Tell us your time zone and your weekly availability that overlaps with Los Angeles business hours (9am–5pm Pacific).

---

## WHY THIS POST IS BUILT THIS WAY (notes for Carlos)

- **"TOLDO" gate:** instantly filters the ~80% who blast applications without reading. Only review the ones that start with it.
- **The voice clip is the whole hire.** For a bilingual phone closer, their *actual spoken Spanish warmth* is the job. Text applications hide this; a 90-second clip reveals it in one listen. Judge: Do they sound like someone YOUR customer would trust? Warm, not scripted? Natural barrio-fluent Spanish, not stiff textbook?
- **"Warm leads, not cold calls" is stated explicitly** so you attract relationship-closers, not boiler-room types — and so the hire isn't set up to fail on the hardest, unproven part.
- **Timezone overlap required** — El Salvador's US-Central-ish time is a real advantage here; make it a filter.
- **AI is a bonus, not a barrier** for this role (unlike the future design hire, where it's core).

## HOW TO RUN IT (quick)
1. Post it. Wait 48–72 hrs.
2. Delete every application that doesn't start with TOLDO. (Ruthless. It's the point.)
3. From the rest, listen to voice clips FIRST — before reading anything else. Shortlist on voice/warmth.
4. Top 3–5 → short paid trial: have them work 2–3 real warm leads (a few reactivation replies / VIP follow-ups) and watch how they handle it.
5. Hire the one your customers would trust. Closing is a feel test as much as a skills test.
