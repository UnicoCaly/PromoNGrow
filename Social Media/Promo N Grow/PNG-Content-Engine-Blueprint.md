# Promo N Grow — Content Engine Blueprint

**Prepared:** April 2026
**Owner:** Carlos (solo founder/operator)
**Scope:** Instagram (primary) + Facebook (mirror), with Reels expansion
**Goal:** A repeatable, largely automated content engine producing 20+ on-brand posts per month, plus a self-filling review-to-post pipeline fed by Judge.me.

---

## 1. Executive Summary

This blueprint turns Promo N Grow's social content from a discretionary task into a production system. The system has four moving parts:

1. **A Content Pillar OS** — a locked 4-pillar mix with pre-defined formats and hook libraries, so you never start from a blank page.
2. **An Education Framework Library** — 8 reusable Matt Gray–style carousel templates that each tie directly to a PNG product. Build them once in Canva, reuse them monthly.
3. **A Judge.me → Canva Review Pipeline** — a semi-automated flow that converts 4–5★ reviews into approve-then-publish social posts, while also generating more reviews and customer photos on every order.
4. **A Solo-Founder Production Loop** — one 2-hour batch block per week produces the next week's content, with Canva Bulk Create doing the heavy lifting.

**The operating principle:** Education earns the follow. Product closes the sale. Reviews remove the risk. Promo converts the warm. Every post does one job.

## 5. The Review Engine — Judge.me → Canva → Approval Queue

The review system has two halves: **getting more reviews/images**, and **turning them into posts.**

### 5A. Getting more reviews & images (inbound)

**Current state:** Judge.me is installed on Shopify. Likely receiving some reviews but not consistently with photos.

**Upgrades to implement (in order):**
1. **Turn on Judge.me photo/video review requests.** Default the email to ask for a photo at their event. Incentivize with a coupon code ("Send us a setup photo, get 10% off your next reorder").
2. **Set review request cadence:** Day 7 post-delivery (first email) + Day 21 (reminder) + Day 45 ("how was your event?" photo request). Judge.me supports this natively.
3. **Post-purchase insert card** in every shipment: QR code → "Show us your setup. Tag @promongrow. Best photo each month gets a free table throw."
4. **SMS follow-up** via Shopify (if you're on a plan that supports it): Day 14 — "How did your event go? Reply with a photo."
5. **Monthly "Client Spotlight" incentive:** feature the best review/photo each month on social; DM the client for permission and a $50 store credit.

**Target:** Move from current review volume to 8–12 new reviews/month with at least 40% carrying a photo.

### 5B. Turning reviews into posts (outbound)

**Architecture (semi-auto with approval queue):**

```
[Judge.me review lands on Shopify product page]
         ↓
[Weekly export: Judge.me → CSV of 4–5★ reviews from last 7 days]
         ↓
[Paste/import into Airtable (or Notion) "Review Queue" table]
         ↓
[Canva Bulk Create reads Airtable → generates 3–5 Review Post drafts]
         ↓
[Carlos reviews drafts in Canva, approves or edits]
         ↓
[Approved posts scheduled in Meta Business Suite (free)]
         ↓
[Client DM'd: "We featured your setup. Here's the link."]
```

**Airtable "Review Queue" schema:**
| Field | Type | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Review ID | Text | From Judge.me |
| Customer Name | Text | First name + last initial |
| Company (if B2B) | Text | Optional |
| Star Rating | Number | Only pull 4+ |
| Review Quote (short) | Long text | ≤18 words for slide, rewrite/tighten as needed |
| Product Ordered | Single select | Tent 10x10, Backdrop, etc. |
| Has Photo | Checkbox | If yes, upload to "Photo URL" field |
| Photo URL | URL | From Judge.me or client submission |
| Use Case (1-liner) | Text | "Their pop-up at SXSW", "Trade show in Vegas" |
| Status | Single select | New / Drafted / Approved / Scheduled / Posted |
| Scheduled Date | Date | Fill on approval |

**Canva Bulk Create setup:**
1. Build **2 review-post Canva templates:**
   - **Template A (single image):** Customer photo on right, stars + quote + company name on left, Copper CTA bar at bottom.
   - **Template B (carousel, 3 slides):** Slide 1 = stars + quote + product; Slide 2 = client photo (full bleed); Slide 3 = CTA + social proof stack.
2. Connect the template to the Airtable Review Queue view via Canva's Bulk Create (CSV or native Airtable connector if available on your plan).
3. Map fields: `{{CustomerName}}`, `{{Quote}}`, `{{Product}}`, `{{UseCase}}`, `{{PhotoURL}}`, `{{StarRating}}`.
4. Weekly cadence: Fridays at 9 AM — Bulk Create generates that week's review post drafts.

**Approval flow:**
- Drafts go into a "Pending Review" Canva folder.
- Carlos reviews Friday afternoon (15-min block), approves or tweaks.
- Approved posts exported as PNG/MP4 → loaded into Meta Business Suite scheduler for the following week.

**Weekly target:** 1–2 review posts per week (matches the 25% Client Results pillar).

## 8. Solo-Founder Production Workflow

Designed around **one 2-hour weekly batch block + one 30-minute mid-week touch-up**.

### Weekly rhythm

**Sunday night (15 min) — Plan**
- Open Airtable Content Calendar. Confirm next week's 5 slots (pillar, format, framework #).
- Pull any new reviews from Judge.me → Review Queue (if Make.com not set up yet, 10-min CSV export).

**Monday (2 hours) — Produce**
- Hour 1: Draft all 5 captions in Airtable (use templates from Section 10 below).
- Hour 2: Open Canva, run Bulk Create on review posts, customize other 4 posts from templates.
- Load all 5 into Meta Business Suite. Schedule across Mon–Fri.

**Thursday (30 min) — Touch up**
- Review last week's analytics in Meta Business Suite (saves, shares, profile visits).
- Adjust next week's calendar if a post flopped or crushed.
- Reply to DMs and comments. Log any new review requests.

**Monthly (45 min, first Sunday of month) — Recalibrate**
- Confirm the 20-post mix hit pillar targets (7 Edu / 6 Product / 5 Client / 2 Promo).
- Review top 3 posts → note what worked, bank the hook formula.
- Review bottom 3 posts → kill the pattern.
- Queue next month's 20 slots.

### Batching principles
- **Cluster by template, not by day.** Do all 4 framework slides, then all 4 product singles, then all 4 CTA slides. Your brain is faster on repeated tasks.
- **Caption templates are sacred.** Don't write from scratch. Open the template, swap the variables.
- **Photo library first.** Maintain a Canva / Airtable-linked folder of product photos, event shots, and review photos tagged by product. You should never search mid-batch.

## 10. Caption Library & Hook Patterns

### Hook patterns (Matt Gray–style, PNG-tuned)
Every carousel Slide 1 hook follows one of these 9 patterns. Keep this list open while drafting.

1. **The Cost-of-Wrong** — "Picking the wrong [X] costs more than picking the right one."
2. **The Hidden Math** — "Your 10x10 booth is 100 sq ft of billboard. Most marketers waste 80%."
3. **The Expert Confession** — "After 20 years producing event displays, here's what we tell every first-time buyer."
4. **The Before It's Too Late** — "One gust is the difference between a photo op and a lawsuit."
5. **The Reframe** — "Trade show ROI isn't won at the show. It's won 2 weeks before."
6. **The Checklist Promise** — "[X] things to know before ordering a [product]."
7. **The Behind-the-Curtain** — "Here's exactly what happens between 'upload your file' and 'it's on your doorstep in 5 days.'"
8. **The Myth Buster** — "'Custom printing takes 2 weeks' is a holdover from 2010. Here's the new reality."
9. **The Client Win** — "[Client] walked into [event] with a booth that cost $X and got $Y in leads. Here's what they ordered."

### Caption framework (for any non-carousel post)

```
[Hook line — 8–12 words, one of the 9 patterns above]

[Context — 1–2 sentences. What is this? Why does it matter?]

[Proof — 1 sentence. The number, the time, or the name that makes it real.]

[Soft CTA — 1 line. Low friction ask.]

[Hard CTA — 1 line. Phone + link.]

#PromoAndGrow #[product tag] #[use-case tag] #[audience tag]
```

**Example (Product Showcase single):**

> Your 10x10 is a 100 sq ft brand moment.
>
> Full-color canopy, matching backdrop, branded table throw — all produced here in 5 business days.
>
> Free artwork proof before a single inch goes to print.
>
> Save this for your next trade show.
>
> Shop: promongrow.com · Call (844) 883-3308
>
> #PromoAndGrow #CustomTent #TradeShowBooth #EventMarketing

### Hashtag strategy
Use **8–12 hashtags per post**, grouped as:
- **Brand (always):** #PromoAndGrow
- **Product (1–2):** #CustomTent #CustomBackdrop #TableThrow #EventDisplays
- **Use case (2–3):** #TradeShowBooth #EventBranding #PopUpShop #FieldMarketing
- **Audience/intent (2–3):** #MarketingManager #EventPlanner #BrandPresence
- **Quality/locale (1–2):** #MadeInUSA #CustomPrinting #EventProduction

Rotate the non-brand hashtags to avoid IG flagging repeated sets.
