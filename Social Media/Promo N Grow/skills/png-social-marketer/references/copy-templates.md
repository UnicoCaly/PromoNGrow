# PNG Copy Templates — Hooks, Captions, CTAs

Copy-paste starting points. Swap in the specific. Don't use as-is.

## Hook catalog (use these openers — steal structure, replace specifics)

### Contrarian observation
- "Most 10x10 booths waste their best real estate."
- "The biggest booth mistake isn't the tent. It's the first 90 seconds."
- "'Bigger booth' is rarely the answer. Usually it's cheaper than that."

### Specific number
- "A 6ft table eats 25% of a 10x10's footprint. Here's what to do with that space instead."
- "40% more foot traffic hits an end-of-aisle booth than a mid-row one."
- "8 linear feet. That's how much front-facing graphic space a 10x10 gives you."

### Framework name
- "The 5-move 10x10 Domination Playbook:"
- "3-step pre-show checklist most teams skip:"
- "The Booth Recovery Framework for when teardown goes sideways:"

### Reframe
- "A trade show booth isn't a tent. It's a 72-hour storefront."
- "Lead capture isn't a post-show task. It's the only thing happening at the booth."

### Mistake → fix
- "If booth graphics are getting lost at shows, it's almost always one of these 3 things."
- "Most teams over-spec the tent and under-spec the graphic. Here's the reverse play."

### Behind-the-scenes / proof
- "What an 800-lb custom tent looks like in a 50 mph wind test."
- "The 2-minute teardown spec we engineered for show ops teams."

## Reel scripts (30-45s)

### "The move" format (Education)
```
[0-3s] HOOK over a clean booth shot: "Most 10x10s waste 25% of their footprint on a back table. Here's the move."
[3-20s] DEMO: show the rearranged layout — counter at front, back wall open, table pulled to the side
[20-35s] WHY IT MATTERS: "More sightline, more traffic, more conversations."
[35-45s] CTA: "Full playbook — link in bio."
```

### "Spec and scale" format (Product)
```
[0-3s] HOOK + product reveal: "Rated for 35 mph sustained. Sets up in 2 minutes. This is the Stratus 10x10."
[3-25s] WALKAROUND + quick specs on screen
[25-40s] CONTEXT: "Built in Los Angeles. Ships in a case you can roll through a hotel lobby."
[40-45s] CTA: "Quote link in bio."
```

### "Teardown story" format (Client/BTS)
```
[0-3s] HOOK over show-floor shot: "This booth did 3 cities in 5 weeks."
[3-30s] MONTAGE: setups, crowds, teardown clips
[30-40s] CREDIT: "The crew from [brand] made the call. We just made sure the tent didn't slow them down."
[40-45s] Soft CTA: "More client work at promongrow.com/work."
```

## CTA library (pick one per post)

**Hard CTAs:**
- "Start a custom quote at promongrow.com/quote"
- "Call (844) 883-3308"
- "Reply QUOTE and we'll spec it"

**Medium CTAs:**
- "See the [product line] at promongrow.com/[slug]"
- "Browse tent builds at promongrow.com/tents"
- "DM us a photo of your space — we'll spec it"

**Soft CTAs:**
- "Reply [WORD] and I'll send the [asset]"
- "More client work at promongrow.com/work"
- "Full playbook — link in bio"

## Don't use

- "Click the link in our bio"
- "Tap for more"
- "Learn more"
- "Visit our website"
- "Check it out"
- "DM us for info" (be specific)
- "Stay tuned" (nothing to stay tuned to)
