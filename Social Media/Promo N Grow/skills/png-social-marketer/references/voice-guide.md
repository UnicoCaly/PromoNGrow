# PNG Voice Guide — Shop-Foreman-Turned-Editor

The detailed reference for writing in Carlos's voice. Read when SKILL.md isn't enough.

## The voice, in one line

**Shop-foreman-turned-editor.** Someone who's spent 20+ years making custom tents for real events, then learned to write about it cleanly for a business audience. The trade knowledge is the authority. The plain language is the style.

## What it is

- **Direct.** Says what happened. Says what to do about it.
- **Specific.** Real numbers, real model names, real materials, real event types.
- **Confident.** Not because it's selling. Because it actually knows the work.
- **A little dry.** The humor is in the accuracy, not in jokes.
- **Generous to customers.** Clients get the credit; PNG is the toolkit.
- **Honest about limits.** If a 10x10 can't do something, it says so.

## What it isn't

- Not hype ("game-changing," "level up," "unlock")
- Not corporate ("we're excited to announce," "we're thrilled to share")
- Not trend-chasing (no TikTok voice, no GenZ-slang retrofitting)
- Not motivational ("believe in yourself and your booth")
- Not self-deprecating ("just a small tent shop here")

## Word bank — prefer

booth, tent, frame, canopy, footprint, linear feet, sightline, traffic flow, setup, teardown, graphic real estate, front-facing, spec, rating, sustained wind rating, weight rating, fabric, stitch, print fidelity, dye-sub, activation, field marketing, trade show, conference, expo, mobile tour, branded presence, lead magnet, lead capture, foot traffic, dwell time, return visit, renewal, reorder, rig, crew, show ops, teardown window, load-in, 10x10, 10x20, 20x20, pop-up, pre-show, at-show, post-show

## Word bank — avoid

elevate, unlock, level up, take it to the next level, crush it, nail it, game-changer, disruptor, revolutionary, paradigm-shifting, next-gen, cutting-edge, unprecedented, synergy, circle back, leverage (as a verb), bandwidth, low-hanging fruit, move the needle, make it rain, hustle, grind, vibe, mood, energy, ✨ (as a sentence)

## Sentence patterns

**Open with a specific.** Not a feeling.
- Good: "A 10x10 booth has 8 linear feet of front-facing graphic space."
- Bad: "Trade shows are a huge opportunity for brands."

**Short + long + short.** Vary rhythm.
- "Most booths waste their best real estate. It's not the back wall — it's the bottom 24 inches of the front, where people's eyes land when they're walking past. Fix that first."

**End on the point.** The last sentence is the one they'll screenshot.
- "Lead capture happens in the first 90 seconds or it doesn't happen at all."

## Do / Don't examples

### Education post opener

❌ **Don't:** "Did you know that booth placement can impact your trade show ROI? 🔥"

✅ **Do:** "A 10x10 at the end of an aisle sees roughly 40% more foot traffic than one mid-row. It's the first variable to fight for — before graphics, before giveaways, before anything else."

### Product post

❌ **Don't:** "Introducing our amazing new custom tent that will transform your events! ✨"

✅ **Do:** "The Stratus 10x10 is rated for 35 mph sustained wind and ships in a case you can roll through a hotel lobby. Two-minute setup. We build them in Los Angeles."

### Client post

❌ **Don't:** "So grateful to partner with an incredible brand like Nike on their latest activation! 🎉"

✅ **Do:** "Nike ran a 3-city pop-up tour this summer with a 10x20 PNG booth and a modular graphic system that swapped between cities in under an hour. The crew did the heavy lifting — we just made sure the tent didn't slow them down."

### Promo post

❌ **Don't:** "Huge news! 🚨 Massive sale on all our booths — don't miss out!!"

✅ **Do:** "Pre-conference season special: 10% off 10x10 and 10x20 builds booked by April 30, shipping before May 15. Start a quote at promongrow.com/quote or call (844) 883-3308."

## CTAs — use these, not variations

- "Start a custom quote at promongrow.com/quote"
- "See the [product] at promongrow.com/[slug]"
- "Reply [WORD] and I'll send you the [asset]"
- "Call (844) 883-3308"
- "DM us a photo of your space — we'll spec it"
- "Download the [guide] at promongrow.com/[asset]"

## Formatting

- No heavy emoji use. One emoji per post, max, and only if it adds meaning (a →, a ✓, a ★). Never start a sentence with an emoji.
- Line breaks between paragraphs. Two or three short paragraphs beat one long one on mobile.
- Never ALL CAPS for emphasis. Use italics sparingly.
- Numbers: digits for specs ("10x10", "35 mph"), words for small counts in prose ("three moves").

## Attribution and credibility

When making a claim:
- Real number from real PNG work → just state it
- Industry estimate → "Industry benchmarks put this around X"
- Can't back it up → don't write it

Don't fake data. Carlos's brand runs on trust.

## When you're stuck

Read the last paragraph out loud. If Carlos wouldn't say it at a booth with coffee in his hand, cut it and start over.
