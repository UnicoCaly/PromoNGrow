Account Architecture

The principle: Apollo bills per seat, not per brand. One login runs both businesses — you separate them with a dedicated sending mailbox per brand plus folders, lists, labels, and sequences. No second license needed (until a second person operates one brand).

Why this works
  • Deliverability is per-domain, not per-account — each brand's domain keeps its own reputation.
  • The sending mailbox is the brand identity on every email.
  • Folders + prefixes make mis-sends obvious before you hit launch.

Setup Walkthrough

Step by step (≈30 min, one-time)
1Add the P&G mailbox. Apollo → Settings → Mailboxes → Link Mailbox. Connect a cold-sending inbox on promotengrow.com (or outreach@promongrow.com). Set daily send limits low at first.
2Warm it ~2 weeks. Turn on Apollo's warm-up (or a warmer) before real volume — protects deliverability and keeps you out of spam.
3Create two folders: PNG and PC (for sequences + lists). Everything lives in one or the other — nothing loose.
4Adopt the prefix rule. Every list, sequence, and saved search starts with PNG · or PC ·. No exceptions — this is what prevents mix-ups.
5Label every contact by brand (a custom field or tag: PNG / PC) as you add or import them.
6Bind sequence → mailbox. When you build a sequence, set the sending mailbox to that brand's inbox. PNG sequences → promotengrow.com; PC sequences → proagobranding.co.
7Pre-launch check (every time): confirm folder, prefix, and sender mailbox match the brand before you hit start. 5 seconds; saves a brand-bleed disaster.
8Watch the credit meter. Both brands draw the same monthly pool — glance at usage so one brand's prospecting doesn't starve the other.
✓ Do
  • One mailbox per brand, on its own domain
  • Prefix everything PNG · / PC ·
  • Confirm the sender before every launch
  • Keep each domain's SPF/DKIM/DMARC clean
  • Tag/label contacts by brand
✕ Don't
  • Send P&G outreach from the Proágo mailbox
  • Mix both brands' leads in one untagged list
  • Run cold volume on a brand-new, un-warmed inbox
  • Assume reporting is per-brand (filter by sequence)
  • Buy a 2nd license before a 2nd person needs one
When a 2nd license actually earns its cost
A different person operates one brand independently (Venus on P&G + a Proágo teammate) · you need clean billing separation (Proágo's costs on Proágo's books) · credit consumption outgrows one plan. Until then — one seat, two mailboxes.
