## 1. The markup formula

| Tier | What's in it | Markup | Multiplier |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Low-ticket** | Print & marketing collateral — business cards, postcards, flyers, brochures, NCR forms, stickers, small format | **400%** | **cost × 5** |
| **High-ticket** | Tents, large-format, trade-show displays, SEG, structural items | **80–100%** | **cost × 1.8 – 2.0** |

- **Low-ticket default = ×5.** Confirmed from your business-card Loom ("400% markup"). Go to ×6 on tiny orders / heavy customization / rush; compress toward ×4 on large volume.
- **High-ticket default = ×2.0 (100%).** Compress toward ×1.8 (80%) on big volume, repeat clients, or competitive deals.
- **⚠️ Brochures — confirm.** Your brochure Loom mentioned a different mark ("~188%" in the auto-caption — unclear). The All-Inclusive Flyers/Brochures selling prices on the Master Price Sheet came straight from your 4over quote PDF, so they're correct as listed — but confirm the exact multiplier you want for re-quotes outside those run sizes.

### Tier decision (when it's not obviously catalog)
1. **Category first** — is it print/collateral (low) or tent/large-format/display (high)?
2. **Dollar backstop** — if cost is under ~$50 → low-ticket; ~$50+ and structural → high-ticket. (Adjust this threshold to taste.)

## 2. Rounding convention
- Always round to a clean number; drop the cents on the sheet where natural.
- Your business-card pattern: **1,000 → $108** (rounded clean), larger runs rounded up to clean hundreds (e.g. 5,000 → ~$291–$300). Small runs round down a touch to stay friendly; big runs round up.
- Rule of thumb: end on $X8, $X5, $X9, or a clean hundred. Never quote a raw $487.33 — make it $495 or $499.

## 3. The floor (absolute — after negotiation, NOT the target)
- **Minimum order: $100.** Anything under → raise to $100 or add setup/handling.
- **Minimum margin under $1,000: 60%.**
- **Minimum margin $1,000 and above: 35%.**
- This is the worst-case floor you'll accept after days of back-and-forth or a note/relationship order — **not** the opening price. Open at list (Master Price Sheet). Only walk toward the floor for volume, repeat, or strategic accounts.
- If a client pushes below the floor → **don't** discount past it. Hold, or walk.

## 5. How a price becomes a set price (the build)
1. Configure the exact product/options in the vendor portal (4over / B2Sign / Soar Dist).
2. Read the vendor cost.
3. Apply the tier markup (§1) → round (§2) → check the floor (§3).
4. That number goes on the **Master Price Sheet** as the rep-facing set price.
5. Re-baseline periodically as vendor costs move.
