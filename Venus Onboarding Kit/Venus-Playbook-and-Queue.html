Follow-Up & Close Playbook

Two rules that run everything:  ①  No quote dies from silence — every quote runs the cadence until it's Won, Nurture, or Dead.  ②  No lead sits without a next step — every lead has a next action + date. Empty next-step = a leak.
Most deals close on the 3rd–6th touch. The money is in the follow-up — but persistence ≠ pestering. Every touch carries a reason and is easy to reply to.

When a deal goes COLD — change the pattern
  • Switch the channel. Called 3×? Text or email — lower pressure, often gets the reply.
  • Switch the time. Try early AM or just after lunch.
  • The takeaway / breakup. Highest re-engage move — signal you'll close the file.
  • Shrink the ask. Down-sell to the easy yes (a smaller tier / one piece).
  • Diagnostic question. "Is it timing, budget, or do you need to run it by someone?"
  • The 1/2/3 reply. Make it a 2-second tap. Even a "3" frees the slot.

Say-it-out-loud templates (ganas voice)
The takeaway (revive a cold deal)

"Hey [name] — Venus from Promo & Grow. No quiero seguir llamándote 🙂. Your [proof/quote] is ready, but I won't hold your production slot if timing changed. Reply 1 (lock it in), 2 (love it, not yet), or 3 (pass) — lo que sea, sin pena. If it's budget or timing, dime y lo resolvemos."

The down-sell (easy yes)

"Totalmente entiendo que [full order] es una decisión grande. ¿Quieres empezar con [smaller piece] so you're moving now, y el resto cuando estés listo? Te mando un free proof hoy — en 5 días hábiles estás listo. ¿Te lo envío?"

The diagnostic

"Sin presión, [name] — para ayudarte mejor: ¿es el timing, el presupuesto, o lo tienes que consultar con alguien? Whatever it is, I'll work with you."

Monthly nurture (re-touch)

"¡Hola [name]! Pensé en ti — [season/event] is coming up. Sigo teniendo tu info; want me to put together a quick free proof so you're ready? No compromiso."
