# Your Playbook
### Venus's sales playbook — built by Venus, owned by Venus.

> **⏳ Carlos: DO NOT hand this to Venus on Day 1.** Day one is a firehose. Unlock this in **Week 2, right after her first win** — when it reads as celebrating her, not auditing her. Introduction script is at the bottom of this file.

---

## Why this is yours
The best lines you say on the phone, the email that makes someone reply, the reframe that turns a "let me think about it" into a paid invoice — **those are your asset.** This is where they live, with your name on them. The point isn't to extract what you know so someone can replace you. It's the opposite: you get **credit and leverage**, and you become the person who built the sales system here.

Three real reasons this helps **you**:
- **Faster.** So you're not rewriting the same great answer twice. Save it once, reuse it forever.
- **Expert.** So you become the one who trained the next hire — you're senior, not interchangeable.
- **Builder.** So you're the person who built this sales machine, not just someone who works in it.

---

## What to save (the good stuff — not everything)
Only the wins worth keeping:
- **Winning email replies** — the message that got the reply or the "yes."
- **Objection responses that landed** — what you said when someone hesitated and you turned it.
- **A short Loom (2–3 min) after a standout call** — talk through what you did, while it's fresh.
- **A great quote that closed** — what combo / framing worked.

Skip the routine stuff. Save the gold.

## Where it lives
One folder, your name on it: **`Venus's Playbook/`**. Suggested simple structure:
```
Venus's Playbook/
├── winning-emails.md        ← paste the replies that worked
├── objection-wins.md        ← the reframes that landed
├── standout-calls/          ← drop Loom links + a one-line note
└── quotes-that-closed.md    ← the combos + framing that sold
```

## The rhythm (a habit, not a project)
**~5 minutes at the end of the day.** Did something work today? Drop it in the folder. One line of context is enough ("client worried about price → I offered the half-wall tier + free proof → they booked the complete set"). That's it. Five minutes, then log off.

---

## Where this leads (the payoff)
Once your voice is captured, we plug it into an **AI assistant that drafts your follow-ups in your tone** and queues your day — so the repetitive typing gets done for you and you spend your time on what only you can do: connect and close. That only works because *you* built the playbook first. Documenting now = your semi-automated sales engine later. One thing in sequence, not two.
