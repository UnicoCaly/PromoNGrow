# Promo & Grow — Role-Play Scenarios & Objection Drills
### Practice makes the close automatic. Run these 2x/week.

**For:** Venus (+ team) · **Owner:** Carlos · **Built:** June 6, 2026.
**How to use:** Carlos (or a peer) plays the customer; Venus closes. Score with the rubric. Or Venus practices solo out loud / with an AI voice. **Repeat weekly** — spaced practice is what makes it stick. Research: 60% of sales need 5+ "no"s, but 44% of reps quit after the first — these drills train the persistence that wins.

---

## The objection backbone: LAER
**L**isten (fully — don't jump in) · **A**cknowledge (empathize, no defensiveness) · **E**xplore (open question to find the real concern) · **R**espond (tailored, then ask for the next step). For price, layer in **Feel-Felt-Found**. Always have the **free proof** as your fallback ("no commitment, just see it").

---

## Role-Play Scenarios

### Scenario 1 — Inbound discovery (new lead)
**Setup:** A taquería owner calls; has a grand opening in 3 weeks; never ordered from us.
**Customer opens:** "Hi, yeah… I saw you guys do tents? How much for a tent with my logo?"
**What good looks like:** warm greeting → get **the moment + the date** before price → learn need (tent only vs full setup) → present good-better-best → anchor free proof + 5-day → ask to start the proof. Doesn't blurt a price first.

### Scenario 2 — Quote handoff with good-better-best
**Setup:** Lead wants "a 10x10 tent." Budget-conscious.
**Customer:** "Just the basic tent, what's that run me?"
**What good looks like:** "Here's how it works — Tent Only is $800, but most folks doing a grand opening go Complete Set at $1,600 so the whole booth matches. Want me to proof the Complete Set so you can see it?" Anchors the middle/up, ties to their moment, free proof.

### Scenario 3 — Reactivation warm call (the whale)
**Setup:** Alegre Music — 174 past PrintCo orders, quiet 34 months. Venus is reconnecting.
**Customer:** "Promo & Grow? …I think we used to use PrintCo?"
**What good looks like:** the reframe — "Same shop, same family, we leveled up to Promo & Grow." Reference what they bought before, reconnect warmly, offer the reorder OR the new event line, low pressure. Goal = re-open the relationship, not hard-sell.

### Scenario 4 — Draft follow-up (#D77 / ask for Temo)
**Setup:** $3,400 quote billed to Sarieliza; her husband Temo (Del Rio Brewing Co.) is the decision-maker.
**Customer (Temo):** "Yeah I got the quote… we're still thinking about it."
**What good looks like:** ask for Temo by name, warm, no pressure → explore the hesitation (LAER) → reassure (free proof, 5-day) → offer to adjust or lock production for their date → clear next step.

### Scenario 5 — Price pushback (objection focus)
**Setup:** Quoted a Complete Set; customer balks.
**Customer:** "That's more than I wanted to spend."
**What good looks like:** LAER + Feel-Felt-Found + explore the real budget + offer a smaller tier (never just discount; hold the $100/Carlos floor) + free proof to keep it moving.

## Scoring rubric (1–5 each; 18+/30 to pass)
| Skill | What you're scoring |
|---|---|
| Warm open | Friendly, on-brand, not robotic |
| Got the moment + date | Learned the event + deadline before pricing |
| Discovery | Understood the real need (tier, stakes) |
| Good-better-best | Presented tiers, anchored the middle/up |
| Free proof + 5-day | Anchored both naturally |
| Objection handling | Used LAER; stayed warm; didn't fold or over-discount |
| Asked for the next step | Clear close ("want me to start the proof?") |

## Objection Flashcards (drill these)
*Front = what they say. Back = the move (LAER) + a line. Cover the back, practice the line, flip to check.*

1. **"That's more than I wanted to spend."** → Acknowledge + explore budget; offer smaller tier; free proof. *"Totally hear you — what's the target you're working with? We can start with [tier] and you'll still show up sharp."*
2. **"I need to think about it."** → Don't push; keep it moving with zero-risk. *"Of course! Want me to send the free proof in the meantime so you can see it with your logo? No commitment."*
3. **"Can you do it faster than 5 days?"** → Honest + confident. *"Tell me your date and I'll tell you straight if we can make it. We hit our 5 days — if it's tight, I'll be upfront."*
4. **"Why you instead of Vistaprint / online?"** → Reframe the value. *"Those are templates and a faraway printer. We're real people in LA — free proof, a person who answers, 5-day delivery. Hecho bien."*
5. **"Just send me a price."** → Get one detail first. *"Happy to! Quick thing so I quote it right — what's the event and when is it?"*
6. **"We already have a vendor."** → No fight; plant a seed. *"Love that you've got someone. Keep us in your back pocket — free proof, 5 days. If they're ever slammed, I've got you."*
7. **"Can you design it for me?"** → Set the line warmly. *"We proof and print — and I'll send free Canva templates + a proof so it comes out great. For full custom branding, our Proágo side can help — want me to loop Carlos in?"* (escalate if bigger.)
8. **"It's been a while — who is this again?"** (reactivation) → The reframe. *"It's Venus from Promo & Grow — we're the shop you knew as PrintCo Direct, Carlos's team. Just reconnecting with our favorite accounts."*
9. **"Email me the info."** → Agree + keep a thread. *"Done — I'll text it too so it doesn't get buried. What's the best number?"*
10. **"I'll get back to you."** → Set the next touch. *"Sounds good — your event's [date], so I'll check in [day] to keep you covered. Sound fair?"*
