# SOP-05 — Proof, Checkout & Shipping
**Covers Looms 8–9 (proof emails, checkout & shipping) · Owner: Carlos · Updated: June 6, 2026**

### Purpose
Know exactly what the customer experiences after you send the invoice, so you can guide them through to paid — and start the production clock.

### Scope
The customer-side flow from invoice email → paid, plus the proof step and shipping rules.

### Who / When
Venus, after sending the invoice (SOP-04), and whenever a customer has a question about checkout, shipping, or their proof.

### What the customer sees (so you can walk them through it)
1. They get your email and **click the link** → it opens the **Shopify checkout** for their order.
2. They **verify their phone number** (quick).
3. Most already use **Shop Pay** → checkout is fast and familiar.
4. Checkout shows **estimated shipping + sales tax**.
5. They pay → the draft flips to a paid invoice → production starts.

### The proof step (our risk-remover)
- We send a **free proof** before anything prints. Nothing goes to production until they **approve** it.
- Approval **starts the 5-business-day delivery clock** (4 days on print).
- Use it in your close: *"You'll see a free proof with your logo before we print a thing — zero risk."*

### Shipping rules
- **Orders $500+ ship free (ground).** Most tents, canopies, and large items clear that threshold automatically — say it, it's a real incentive. Smaller items run **~$30 standard ground** ($25–45 by size).
- **Need it faster?** Standard ground won't cut it → it's a **custom/closing quote.** Get the customer's date, confirm the rush figure with Carlos, then add the shipping line (SOP-02).

### Output
A paid order in production, customer clear on proof + timing.

### Exceptions & edge cases
| Scenario | What to do |
|---|---|
| Customer stuck at checkout | Walk them through phone verify + Shop Pay; offer to resend the link. |
| Wants expedited delivery | Custom shipping quote via Carlos; don't promise a date you can't hit. |
| Wants changes after paying | Flag Carlos before production locks. |
| Hasn't approved the proof | Follow up (Workbook §4) — the clock doesn't start until they approve. |
