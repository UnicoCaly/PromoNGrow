## The 6 SOPs
1. **SOP-01 — Quoting & Pricing a Job** — price from the Master Price Sheet, product knowledge for cards/postcards/brochures, turnaround, custom route-through. *(The vendor-pricing Looms 1–3 stay internal.)*
2. **SOP-02 — Create a Draft Order in Shopify** *(covers Looms 4–5)* — catalog products, custom line items, price-per-unit, save & send.
3. **SOP-03 — Add / Find a Client in Shopify** *(covers Loom 6)* — search existing, add new, address verify, marketing opt-in, tags.
4. **SOP-04 — Draft the Invoice Email & Notes** *(covers Loom 7)* — CC rules, invoice naming, AI-assisted voice.
5. **SOP-05 — Proof, Checkout & Shipping** *(covers Looms 8–9)* — what the client receives, Shop Pay checkout, free-shipping promo, rush handling.
6. **SOP-06 — Legacy Order Lookup in Ordant** *(covers Loom 10)* — pull a legacy client's last price + file for reprints.

---
*The full order lifecycle: **quote (SOP-01) → build the draft (SOP-02) → attach the client (SOP-03) → send the invoice email (SOP-04) → they approve the proof & check out (SOP-05)**. SOP-06 is the lookup you run for returning/legacy clients before quoting a reprint.*
