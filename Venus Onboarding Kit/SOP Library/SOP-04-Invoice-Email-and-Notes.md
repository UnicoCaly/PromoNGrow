# SOP-04 — Draft the Invoice Email & Notes
**Covers Loom 7 (drafting client emails & invoice notes) · Owner: Carlos · Updated: June 6, 2026**

### Purpose
Send the invoice with a warm, persuasive note that gets the customer to open the checkout link and pay — in our voice.

### Scope
Every draft order's outgoing email. Week 1: Carlos reviews before send.

### Who / When
Venus, right after saving the draft (SOP-02) with the client attached (SOP-03).

### Steps
1. **Send it from your personal email.** In the top-right **From** dropdown, set it to your own promongrow.com address so the invoice comes from *you*, not the generic hello@. **CC hello@promongrow.com** so we keep a copy of the communication. CC the client's accounting dept or business partner if they ask. *(Week 1: Carlos still reviews your quotes before they send.)*
2. **Subject line:** `Promo Invoice [number] — Promo & Grow (formerly PrintCo Direct Inc.)`. The "(formerly PrintCo Direct Inc.)" helps legacy clients who still know us as PrintCo recognize the order.
3. **Write the note** — warm, specific, never pushy (Brand Voice). Hit:
   - A personal opener (reference their event / what they told you).
   - What's in the order (restate the estimate briefly).
   - **Free proof** before anything prints, and the sequence: **production doesn't start until the invoice is paid AND the proof is approved — then the 5-day clock begins** (4 on print).
   - A clear nudge to the checkout link: "Tap to review and lock it in."
   - Bilingual if they speak Spanish.
4. **Use AI to draft, then make it yours.** Carlos uses AI (prefers Claude) to produce persuasive emails fast. Draft with AI → adjust to the customer → keep it warm and human. *(A shared brand-voice prompt library is coming. Tip: paste screenshots of the prior conversation so the AI has full context and catches errors.)*
5. **Attach/confirm the invoice link** and send (after Carlos's review in Week 1).

### Output
A sent invoice email, CC'd correctly, that drives the customer to checkout (SOP-05).

### Quick template (adapt — don't robot it)
> **Subject:** Promo Invoice D87 — Promo & Grow (formerly PrintCo Direct Inc.)
> Hi [Name] — great talking about your [event] on [date]! Here's your invoice for the [product summary]. Everything includes your **free proof** before we print, and once you approve we deliver in **5 business days**. Tap below to review and lock it in — any tweaks, just reply and I'll adjust. — Venus, Promo & Grow
> *ES: ¡Hola [Name]! Aquí está tu factura para [producto]. Incluye tu prueba gratis antes de imprimir, y al aprobarla te lo entregamos en 5 días. Toca para revisar — cualquier ajuste, me dices.*

### Exceptions & edge cases
| Scenario | What to do |
|---|---|
| Legacy client | Use the invoice-number subject convention so it ties to their history (see SOP-06). |
| Customer wants changes | Adjust the draft, re-send; keep the same thread. |
| No reply | Run the follow-up cadence (Workbook §4: Day 1 / 3 / 7). |
