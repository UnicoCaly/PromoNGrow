## Who we are
Promo & Grow is a 20-year-old, family-built, **Lynwood-LA** company that makes brands look unforgettable at their moment — custom tents, table covers, flags, trade-show displays, wall art, and the print that fills out a booth. Made to order, **delivered in 5 business days**, 4.8★ on Google. We're not a faraway template printer. We're real people who answer the phone, send a free proof, and make sure you show up right. *Tu evento, tu marca, hecho bien.*

## What we figured out (the strategy in one breath)
For years the business has quietly run on a **concierge close**: a customer calls or emails, we build their order by hand, send an invoice, they pay. That human close converts **10x better** than our website cart ever has. The website isn't broken — it was just missing the **bridge** between people finding us and that warm human close.

**You are that bridge.** Every warm lead — every inbound call, every "reply to me" email click, every text — now lands with a person whose whole job is to help them, fast, in their language, and close. That's the engine. You're running it.

## The road
- **Now → $20K/month.** We already have the pieces: 2,000 qualified visits × ~2.5% × ~$400 = $20K. The job this quarter is simple and huge — **work the warm queue, close, repeat.** Speed and follow-through win it.
- **Next → $50K/month (12–18 months).** That's not "$20K but bigger" — it's a different lever. It comes from **higher average order value**: institutional POs (like South Gate), reorders (like Donut Media), bundles, and repeat relationships. Which means **the accounts you nurture today become the $50K engine tomorrow.** Chase POs and reorders, not just volume.
- **Then → it scales.** Bilingual Spanish moat, SMS, cold outreach, paid ads — layered on **after** the warm engine is proven. You're proving it.

## Where you fit
You're not "a rep." You're **employee #1 of the sales machine we're building.** Carlos is deliberately stepping off the day-to-day close so you can own it — that's how much this seat matters. The plan is:
1. **You close the warm queue** and we watch the bridge prove out.
2. **We capture your playbook** — your best replies, your winning calls — so you get credit and leverage, and so you become the expert who trains the next hire (more on this in Week 2).
3. **We tool you up** — once you've proven the close, you get an AI assistant that drafts follow-ups in your voice and queues your day, so you do more of what only you can do: connect and close.

You build the sales system here. You don't just work in it.
