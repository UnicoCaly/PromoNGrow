## §1 — QUOTING (how to price a job)

**The golden rule: you quote from the Master Price Sheet.** Most of what we sell already has a set price. No math, no guessing.

### 1.1 Three cases
1. **It's on the Master Price Sheet** → use the set price. Done. (This is ~95% of jobs.)
2. **Tents / displays priced by configuration** → build the exact combo in the Shopify draft order; the price builds itself from the sheet's options.
3. **Custom / not on the sheet** → **ask Carlos for the number** (we call this route-through). Don't invent a price. Send it to him, he gives you the figure, you quote it.

### 1.2 The floor (never go below)
- **Minimum order: $100.** If a job comes in under that, raise it to $100 or add setup/handling.
- **No discounts without Carlos's OK** during your trial. List price is the price. If a client pushes hard, see §3.4 — explore the real need before touching price, and escalate if they want below list.

### 1.3 Build the quote (workflow)
1. Get from the customer: product, quantity, size/specs, **art/logo**, and the **event / needed-by date**.
2. Price it (§1.1). Check the floor (§1.2).
3. Build the **Shopify draft order** (see SOP Library — Carlos's recorded walkthrough).
4. Offer **good-better-best** when natural (e.g. Tent Only / Half Wall / Complete Set) — people anchor to the middle.
5. Send the invoice + a warm note (§3). Always restate the **free proof** and **5-day delivery** (4-day on print).
6. Tag the customer in Shopify + set your follow-up reminder (§4).

### 1.4 Escalate to Carlos / Proágo (don't quote these)
Complex branding beyond our catalog — **vehicle wraps, channel letters / signage, brand guides or brand systems** — is Proágo Branding work. **Pitch the relationship**, then bring in Carlos to scope it. Script: *"That's exactly the kind of bigger branding work our Proágo side handles — let me loop in Carlos so we get it right."* It's upside; you still own the client.

## §3 — SALES SCRIPTS (frameworks, say them in your own warm voice)
Bilingual — match the customer's language. **Never pushy — we equip, we don't pressure.** Always anchor on the **free proof** (removes risk) and **5-day delivery** (we respect their moment).

### 3.1 Inbound call
> "Thanks for calling Promo & Grow, this is Venus! Tell me about your event — what've you got coming up?" → *listen, get the moment + the date* → "Perfect, we can absolutely help. Here's how easy it is: send me your logo, I'll get you a **free proof** — no charge, no commitment — and once you love it, we deliver in **5 business days**. Want me to start that proof?"

### 3.2 Discovery (the 3 things to learn every time)
1. **The moment:** "What's the event / when is it?" (date = urgency + production planning)
2. **The need:** "What are you picturing — just the tent, or the full setup?"
3. **The stakes:** "First time setting up, or leveling up from last time?"

### 3.3 The quote handoff
> "Based on what you told me, here's what I'd recommend…" → present **good-better-best** → "Everything includes your **free proof** before anything prints, and it ships in **5 days**. I'll send it right now — email or text?"

### 3.4 Objection handling (warm reframes, never defensive)
- **"That's more than I wanted to spend."** → "Totally hear you. Let's look at what matters most for your event — we can start with [smaller tier] and you'll still show up sharp. What's your target?" *(explore the need; never just discount — hold the floor, escalate if needed)*
- **"I need to think about it."** → "Of course! Want me to send the free proof in the meantime? You can *see* it with your logo — no commitment — and decide from there."
- **"Can you do it faster?"** → "Standard is 5 business days and we hit it. Tell me your date and I'll tell you straight if we can make it."
- **"Why you vs. [online site]?"** → "Those are templates and a faraway printer. We're real people in LA — free proof, a person who answers, and we make sure you show up right. *Hecho bien.*"

### 3.5 Bilingual warmth (the heart)
When the customer speaks Spanish, switch naturally: *"Tu evento, tu marca, hecho bien. Mándame tu logo y te hago una prueba gratis — sin compromiso — y te lo entregamos en 5 días."* Warm, neighborly, real.

## §4 — FOLLOW-UP SEQUENCE (speed + persistence, never annoying)
**Speed-to-lead is #1:** respond to any new warm lead **within minutes** if possible, same business day at worst. First to respond usually wins.

**If you can't reach them by phone:**
- **Day 0:** Call. No answer → warm voicemail + immediately text/email ("Hi [Name], Venus from Promo & Grow — tried you just now about your [event]. Send your logo and I'll start a free proof! Or call me at [ext].")
- **Day 1–2:** Call again at a different time → no answer → email the free-proof offer + restate 5-day.
- **Day 3:** "Any questions before your [event date]? Happy to jump on a quick call."
- **Day 7:** "Your event's coming up — want to start the 5-day production clock so you're covered?"
- **After Day 7:** move to monthly nurture. Don't hound.

**Unpaid quotes/invoices (the hidden revenue):** same Day 1 / 3 / 7 cadence — re-send the proof, offer to adjust, then the deadline nudge.

## §5 — PRIORITY GAME PLAN (who to attack first)
Work the **warmest leads first** — highest chance to close, builds your reps fast:
1. **🔥 Inbound right now** — calls/texts/emails/quote requests today. Respond in minutes. Always #1.
2. **♨️ Recent email engagers** — opened/clicked the campaigns (hands raised). *Carlos provides the list.*
3. **♨️ VIP / repeat buyers** — personal outreach, they already love us: **Josh @ Donut Media**, **South Gate / sogate.org** (institutional PO), **Pathita @ Tao Kae Noi**.
4. **🌡️ Reactivation** — broader past-customer openers.
5. **❄️ Cold — not yet.** Comes later, once you're sharp on warm leads.

**Your focus: tiers 1–3.** That's where the money already is. Lean into **PO accounts and reorders** — that's how we grow average order value.
