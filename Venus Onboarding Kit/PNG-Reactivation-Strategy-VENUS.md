## The reframe (how she opens — warm, not cold)
Every call rides on one truth: **"PrintCo Direct is now Promo & Grow — same family shop, same people, leveled up."** She's not selling to a stranger; she's checking in as the shop they already know.

Two angles, pick per account (the list shows what each bought):
1. **The reorder** — "You used to get your [banners / business cards / NCR forms] from us — want to refresh those?" Low-friction, fast yes.
2. **The new line** — "We've expanded into custom event displays — tents, table covers, backdrops, all delivered in 5 days. Got an event coming up?" Plants the bigger sale.

## Channel: CALL FIRST
She's a closer — lead with her voice. Email/text are the backup that earn the callback.

I was going through our accounts and saw we took care of your **[product]** a while back. I wanted to personally reconnect — we've leveled up, we now do custom event branding too, and we still deliver in 5 days. What've you got coming up this year?"

Vi que le ayudamos con sus **[producto]** hace un tiempo y quería reconectar personalmente. Crecimos: ahora también hacemos branding para eventos, y seguimos entregando en 5 días. ¿Qué tiene en puerta este año?"*

### Voicemail (if no answer)
> "Hi [Name], Venus from Promo & Grow — formerly PrintCo Direct, Carlos's shop. Reconnecting with a few of our favorite accounts. I'll text you too — reply anytime and I'll take care of you. Talk soon!"

### Follow-up text/email (same day, after the call/VM)
> "Hi [Name], Venus from Promo & Grow (formerly PrintCo Direct) 👋 Tried you today — we've expanded into custom event displays + still do all your print. Want a fresh quote on your [product], or something for an upcoming event? Reply here or call (844) 883-3308."

### Cadence (per account)
- **Day 0:** Call → no answer → voicemail + text/email same day.
- **Day 2:** Call again, different time of day.
- **Day 5:** Email — lead with the reorder or a relevant new product.
- **Day 10:** Last touch — "Should I keep you on our list for event season?"
- **No response after Day 10:** tag `stage:nurture` and move on. Re-touch quarterly.

## The tag system (track engagement + prioritize)
Tags live on the Shopify **customer** record. Your import already carries `Proágo (PCD) Legacy Clients`, `Legacy Client`, `PrintCo Direct Client`, and `Reactivation-Apr2026` — **keep those** (they mark the source). Venus layers these working tags on top:

| Group | Tags | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Stage** | `stage:new` → `stage:contacted` → `stage:quoted` → `stage:won` / `stage:nurture` / `stage:lost` | Where the lead is. Advance it on every touch. |
| **Priority** | `pri:hot` · `pri:warm` · `pri:cold` | How hard to chase. Hottest tier = `pri:hot`. |
| **Source** | `src:reactivation` · `src:draft` · `src:inbound` · `src:vip` | Where it came from. |
| **Interest** | `int:tents` · `int:displays` · `int:flags` · `int:print` · `int:banners` · `int:apparel` · `int:vinyl` | What they want — drives the pitch + future campaigns. |
| **Follow-up** | `fu:day2` · `fu:day5` · `fu:day10` · `fu:nurture` | Her reminder for the next touch. Clear/advance when done. |
| **Guardrail** | `DNC:institutional` · `DNC:reserved` · `owner:carlos` · `lang:es` | Do-not-contact (Carlos's). Never contact any `DNC:*`. `lang:es` = Spanish-preferred. |

**How she works it daily:**
1. **Build today's list with Shopify filters** (Customers → Filter → by tag): e.g. the `Reactivation-2026` segment (~574 clients), or `pri:hot` + `stage:new`. Run the query and work down it.
2. On each call: set `stage:contacted`, add the right `int:` tag, set the next `fu:` reminder, and `lang:es` if Spanish.
3. When she sends a quote → `stage:quoted`; when paid → `stage:won`. The scorecard reads these.
4. **Never touch anything tagged `DNC:institutional`.** (Already applied to the City of South Gate, Lynwood, and LUSD accounts in Shopify.)

- **Do not contact institutional accounts.** South Gate, Lynwood (city + LUSD), and Nova Storage are handled by Carlos — they're tagged `DNC:institutional` in Shopify. Some additional gov/edu accounts are on hold pending review, and a few private accounts (e.g., Virginia Country Club, Prime Lubricants) are reserved (`owner:carlos` / `DNC:reserved`). **Rule: never contact anything tagged `DNC:*`.**
- **Warm, never pushy** — we're reconnecting, not chasing. The reframe does the work.

- **Log every touch with a tag** — if it's not tagged, it didn't happen.
