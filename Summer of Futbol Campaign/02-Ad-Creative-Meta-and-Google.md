## Promo & Grow — "Start the 5-Day Clock" / Summer-of-Fútbol — Paid Ad Creative (Meta + Google)

**Big idea:** Start the 5-day clock. Honest urgency on the real 5-business-day promise.
**Master line — EN:** *The matches run through July 19. Free proof today, booth-ready in 5 days.*
**Master line — ES:** *El torneo va hasta el 19 de julio. Prueba gratis hoy, listo en 5 días.*
**Objective for all Meta ad sets:** Leads (Instant Form). Never Link Clicks, never a purchase event.

> **Date math used in copy (5 business-day production + 2–3 day ship):** A lead today (Mon, June 15) is comfortably booth-ready for the late-June group-stage and early-July knockout weekends. Where a deadline is named, it's a real "order by" cutoff — never a countdown, never "only X left."

> **CTA button note (Meta constraint):** Meta lead-form buttons must be chosen from Meta's fixed list — "Get a Free Proof" is not selectable. We use the closest native label (**"Get Quote"** EN / **"Cotizar"** ES) and carry the free-proof promise in the headline/description, which are free-text. The on-image CTA chips and the carousel CTA card *do* say "Get a Free Proof" / "Reciba su prueba gratis," since those are our own art.
