## 7. Guardrails (the rails this train runs on)

1. ⚖️ **FIFA-safe.** Never "FIFA / World Cup / Copa Mundial / the emblem / trophy / team crests" or any
   implied official tie. We use *"the matches," "the big tournament," "el torneo," "summer of fútbol,"
   "watch party," "game day."* **Team colors yes; crests no.** We print the customer's **own** logo —
   and the free-proof step is our checkpoint to reject any infringing art a customer sends.
2. 🚫 **No fake urgency.** Real 5-day lead-time math only — no countdown clocks, no "only X left." The
   "reply-to-me" email got 13% clicks; the discount blast got 0%. We drive to **reply / call / free proof.**
3. 💎 **Premium, never cheap.** Navy/copper system holds the gravity. Team colors live in the *real product
   photo*, never as bargain-flyer chrome.
4. 📸 **Real photos only.** No AI-generated tents/stadiums/crowds. Source real client + production shots
   from Carlos (placeholders flagged in each deliverable).
5. 🧰 **"Design not included — we proof before production."** We don't promise design; we offer free Canva
   templates + the free proof.
6. ☎️ **Every asset has a way to talk:** (844) 883-3308, plus one low-friction CTA.
7. 🧍 **Don't bury Venus.** Pace to ~5–6 quality leads/day; the qualifier form filters tire-kickers.
8. 🔒 **Channel firewall.** Email blast = owned warm list only. Cold prospecting stays on the separate
   warmed domain (CAN-SPAM). SMS marketing needs TCPA opt-in. **Exclude DNC:institutional** (City of South
   Gate, Lynwood/LUSD, Nova Storage) from every blast.
